# tools

**Layer 3 · Front end**

Interactive tools and the camera: navigation, selection, transform gizmos, and the modelling and UV tools.

## Purpose

Turns plain input (mouse, keys, what is under the cursor) into intentions: the viewport camera with a choice of navigation controls and cursor-point orbit and zoom, the selection methods, the move/rotate/scale gizmos, and every modelling tool (extrude, bevel, loop cut and so on) with live parameters. Each tool's result is an operation plus a command for `scene` to apply. See DESIGN.md §4.4 and §6.

## Must never

- Change the scene or a mesh directly: tools only produce operations and commands
- Depend on egui or the GPU: input arrives as plain data, so tools are testable without a window

## Depends on

`mesh` (bounding boxes; `scene`, `uv`, `config` planned). Outside libraries: `glam` (maths), `serde` (saving the camera).

## Status

- **`Camera`**: position, yaw, pitch and field of view; view and projection matrices (right-handed, Z up, depth 0 to 1); `project` (world to screen) and `ray_through` (screen to world). Opens looking at the origin from the front, right and above. With `ortho_height` set it is a flat (orthographic) camera showing that many centimetres from bottom to top; cameras saved before this load as perspective.
- **`ViewKind`**: Perspective, Top, Front, Right and Orthographic (flat, any direction, [decision 0069](../../docs/decisions/0069-orthographic-view.md)), with `name`, `is_flat` and `grid_plane` (the plane a view's grid lies in). `Navigator::for_view` opens a view of a kind and `Navigator::kind` tells which it is (a flat view looking exactly as Top, Front or Right is that view, any other Orthographic). `Navigator::toggled_projection` switches perspective and orthographic, keeping direction, pivot and the pivot's size on screen. Flat views orbit too, staying flat; panning uses the shown height, and zooming changes that height, keeping the point under the mouse still.

- **`Navigator`** (`navigation`): the camera plus the pivot it looks at. Orbit around the pivot wherever it is on screen (it stays still while the view turns; the scene follows the mouse; never flips over the top), `set_pivot` (the point under the mouse becomes the pivot, without moving the camera), pan (points at the pivot's depth stay under the mouse), dolly (exponential, up to 1 km away; within `ZOOM_THROUGH_DISTANCE`, 10 cm, zooming in moves in steady steps and pushes the pivot ahead, so it can pass a surface, [decision 0080](../../docs/decisions/0080-navigation-feel.md)), and `dolly_towards`/`wheel_towards` (zoom towards a point, which stays under the mouse and becomes the pivot); `framed` (the same view moved so a `Bounds` fits, keeping its direction) `blend` (part of the way between two views, for gliding) and `looking_along` (the same view turned round the pivot to look along a direction, at the same distance; straight down or up lined up like the Top view, [decision 0066](../../docs/decisions/0066-clickable-axes.md)); Plain input in, no egui. - **Navigation controls** (`controls`): five `Scheme`s (Alt + mouse, Mouse only, Mouse only middle zooms, Middle pans Alt orbits, Middle orbits) named by what they do, and `NavigationSettings` (scheme, Alt + mouse in every scheme, invert orbit, orbit and zoom around the mouse, show the turning point) that turn a button and keys into a `DragKind`. A plain left drag never navigates. Saved with serde.
- **Picking elements and objects** (`components`, decisions [0027](../../docs/decisions/0027-component-modes.md), [0029](../../docs/decisions/0029-selection.md) and [0035](../../docs/decisions/0035-heavy-meshes.md)): `MeshPick` indexes one mesh in its own space, with a bounding volume hierarchy (`bvh`: items sorted along a space-filling curve, a box per 8 items, a box per 4 boxes) over its triangles; those over its points, edges and polygon middles are built when first needed. `MeshPick::moved` makes the index for a mesh whose points moved from the old one, working out only the boxes around the moved points (all of them when many moved; afresh when the faces changed). `PickScene::from_picks` places indexes in the world (with each object's matrix and its inverse; rays are taken into each object's space), so selecting or moving objects never rebuilds an index; `PickScene::new` indexes meshes afresh. Hovering collects the points or edges near the mouse and checks visibility nearest first, so a dense mesh costs one or two ray casts. `under(ElementKind, camera, size, mouse)` returns a `Hovered` (object index and `mesh::Element`): points within `POINT_RADIUS` and edges within `EDGE_RADIUS` screen points, nearest first; polygons by the first face the mouse ray hits; hidden ones skipped. `object_under` finds the shape under the mouse; `edge_near` and `nearest_edge_of` choose the edge a double-click runs along. A `Region` (`Rect`, `Lasso`, `Circle` for the brush) selects with `elements_in` (points inside, edges with both ends inside or touching the brush, polygon middles inside plus those under the brush) and `objects_in`, through the shapes or `visible_only`.
- **Gizmo** (`gizmo`, [decision 0030](../../docs/decisions/0030-transform-gizmos.md)): `Gizmo` (origin, axes, `GizmoTool` Move, Rotate, Scale or All) gives each `Handle`'s screen `Shape` (arrows, plane squares, the centre dot, rings, the line-of-sight ring, scale boxes and scale squares; a `ScalePlane` drag scales its two axes equally, by how far the mouse goes along the square's diagonal, [decision 0070](../../docs/decisions/0070-extrude-freedom.md)), `GIZMO_SIZE` points long at any distance (`Camera::world_per_pixel`), leaving out locked axes and arrows pointing at the viewer; `handle_at` finds the one under the mouse. `Drag` turns mouse movement into a `Change` (translate, rotate around an axis, scale per gizmo axis), step by step so `FINE_FACTOR` applies only while the fine key is held, with locks. `snap`, `nearest_on_axis` and `into_plane` help snapping; `PickScene::snap_target` finds the point, edge spot or surface under the mouse (`SnapTargets`), leaving out what is moving.
