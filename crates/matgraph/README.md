# matgraph

**Layer 1 · Core**

Material graphs: boxes joined by wires, worked out into seamless textures.

## Purpose

The engine behind the Material mode (Phase 12, [decision 0256](../../docs/decisions/0256-material-graph-plan.md), [decision 0257](../../docs/decisions/0257-material-graphs-core.md), the plan in [docs/plans/phase-12.md](../../docs/plans/phase-12.md)): what a graph is, every change to it, and working it out into pictures. Everything here can be tested without a window or a GPU.

## Must never

- Know about scenes, undo, files, the GPU or UI (the scene keeps graphs and records their edits; `fileio` saves them; the interface draws them)
- Change a graph except through a checked `GraphEdit`, or refuse one without a reason in plain words
- Make a texture that doesn't tile, or that changes with the number of cores
- Allocate a picture outside `Size::MIN` to `Size::MAX`, or panic on a damaged graph

## Depends on

`words` (the language layer: every reason, name and note goes through it), `glam`, `shapes` (the Shape box's drawings, layer 1).

## Status

Phase 12, milestone 1 (the core), milestone 3 (the first boxes), milestone 4 (the seam check), milestone 5 (graphs in the painter), milestone 6 (baked export), milestone 7 (your own boxes), milestone 8b (boxes on any kind), milestone 9 (Scatter), milestone 10 (more boxes), milestone 14 (foliage output), milestone 15 (animated boxes), milestone 16 (bypass and reroute dots), milestone 17 (finding boxes), milestone 18 (variations and the dice), milestone 19 (project palettes) and milestone 20 (the Painted box), milestone 26 (trim sheets) and milestone 27 (the health check):

- **Value kinds** (`kind`): `ValueKind` (shape, height, colour, whole material; `name`, `described`), `fit(from, to)`: `Fit::Fits`, `Fit::Converts(note)` (a shape as a height, greys as a colour, a material's height or colour) or the reason it can't ("a colour has no inside or outside").
- **Pictures** (`image`, `value`): `Image<P>` (square, row 0 the top; `at` wraps round past every edge, `sample` blends and wraps), `Grey` and `Rgb` (sRGB 0 to 1, or normals), `Size` (a power of two, 16 to 4096; `new` refuses others with the reason and the nearest size as a fix; `nearest`; `PREVIEW` 256). `Value` (`Shape`, `Height`, `Colour`, `Material`, each shared in an `Arc`; `converted`), `MaterialMaps` (colour, height, roughness, metal, normal (OpenGL, y up the texture), occlusion, curvature, and optional opacity and translucency for alpha cards: `None` solid, no light through; `opacity_at`, `is_cut_out`).
- **Refusals** (`refusal`): `Refusal { reason, fixes }`; `Fix` (put a box between, add a box, connect another output instead, use a size, use the nearest value, look at a box, remove a box; `describe` words it as a choice). Nothing changes until a fix is picked, which the interface does (milestone 2).
- **Kinds of box** (`registry`): `BoxType` (`info`, `run`), `BoxInfo` (id saved in files, plain name, `Group` (Make, Shape, Detail, Colour, Material, listed as "Finish": the experts' working order), "What does this do?", input and output `PlugSpec`s (key, name, kind, needed), `SettingSpec`s, "usually comes next", one per graph), `Label` (an English key through `words`, or a person's own words for boxes they make, milestone 7), `Job` (size, seed, cores, settings by key, inputs converted to each plug's kind; `fill_grey`, `fill_rgb`, `map_grey`), `Registry` (`standard()` made once, `with_standard_boxes`, `add` refuses a taken id, `iter` by group).
- **Settings** (`settings`): `SettingKind` (number, whole number, colour, choice, switch, seed), `SettingValue`, `SettingSpec::check` (out of range says so, offering the nearest), `nearest`; `Settings` keeps changed values by key (`resolved`, `normalised`), so kinds can gain settings and old graphs still read.
- **Graphs** (`graph`): `Graph` (name, seed, `GraphBox`es by `BoxId`, `Wire`s between `PlugRef`s, one per input, comment `Frame`s by `FrameId` with title, colour, rectangle and note; ids from one counter, never reused). `GraphEdit` (add, remove, dissolve (keeping the wire through), move, set a setting, connect, disconnect, add, change and remove frames, seed, rename; `describe` for undo) through `Graph::apply`, all checked. `check_connection` says how a wire fits or why not, with fixes (boxes that could go between (not those only passing the wanted kind on), other outputs that fit, nearest first); loops and a box feeding itself are refused. `order` (each box after what feeds it), `feeds`, `boxes_in(frame)`, `to_parts` / `from_parts` (everything checked, settings kept in range, kinds from a newer app kept as they are). Limits: `MAX_BOXES`, `MAX_FRAMES`, `MAX_NAME`, `MAX_NOTE`.
- **Working out** (`eval`, `cache`, `parallel`): `Evaluator::run(registry, graph, size, wanted)` gives an `Evaluation` (each box's `BoxResult` (outputs and its picture) or the `Refusal` saying why it can't run: an empty needed input, a box it waits for, a kind from a newer app; `made`, `reused`, `problems`); `material` gives the Material out box's material. Each box's result is kept in the `Cache` under a hash of its kind, settings, size, seed and its inputs' keys, so only what changed is worked out again; results the last run didn't use are forgotten past the budget (512 MB standard). Each box's pixels are shared over the cores in bands of 64 rows (`std::thread::scope`), so the result is the same on any number of cores.
- **Painted-in light** (`light`, milestone 10): `sun`, `lit` (light to shade by how a surface faces the sun, with softness) and `warmed`: the painter's light and shade helper (`paint::Lighting`) calls them, as the Directional light box does, so both light alike.
- **The Shape box** (`boxes::drawn_shape`, milestone 11, [decision 0273](../../docs/decisions/0273-shape-canvas.md)): a box drawn on the shape canvas. `GraphBox::drawing` (a `shapes::Drawing`, kept only by a kind whose `BoxType::takes_drawing` says so; dropped from others by `from_parts`), changed only by `GraphEdit::SetDrawing` (checked again with `Drawing::from_parts`); `Job::drawing`; the drawing's `key` (not its reference image) is part of the box's cache key. Tests in `drawn_shape.rs`.
- **Edges, varied copies and veins** (milestone 13, [decision 0275](../../docs/decisions/0275-edges-and-variation.md)): the drawing's paths already carry edges and varying (`shapes::Drawing::drawn_paths`); `drawn` adds `shapes::raster::veins` to the Height (times the mask and the box's Height) and darkens or lightens the Colours along them, only where the shape is.
