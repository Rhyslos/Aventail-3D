# scene

**Layer 1 · Core**

The scene document: objects, their operation stacks, commands and undo history.

## Purpose

The single source of truth for an open project. Holds the object hierarchy (transforms, names, visibility), each object's base (a parametric primitive or a mesh) and its stack of recorded operations, evaluated and cached layer by layer. Every change is a command that can undo itself; history is bounded by a memory budget, not a step count. See DESIGN.md §4.2–4.3 and [decision 0006](../../docs/decisions/0006-base-plus-operation-stack.md).

## Must never

- Know about files, the GPU or UI
- Be changed except through commands
- Store world-space transforms: only local ones, with world transforms derived
- Assume the scene is frozen in time: evaluation takes a context, ready for animation later

## Depends on

`mesh` (bounding boxes), `uv` (unwrapping, relaxing and straightening UVs, the same layer), `booleans` (union, subtract and intersect, the same layer), `mesh::curve` (curves, sweeps and lathes), `words` (the language layer: undo labels, messages and reasons go through it, [decision 0134](../../docs/decisions/0134-shared-language-crate.md)). Outside library: `glam` (maths).

## Status

The object tree, commands and undo ([decision 0018](../../docs/decisions/0018-scene-document-and-undo.md)):

- **`Object`**: a name, a kind (`ObjectKind::Group`, or `ObjectKind::Primitive` with its settings, whose `mesh` and `local_bounds` come from the `mesh` crate), a visibility switch, a local `Transform` (position, heading/pitch/bank, scale: where its pivot is) and an `offset` (where its shape sits relative to its pivot, [decision 0068](../../docs/decisions/0068-pivot-editing-and-scene-units.md); nothing as standard). `Scene::world_matrix` is the pivot's place in the world, `Scene::shape_matrix` the shape's (pivot × offset): drawing, picking, bounds and joining use the shape's, children sit relative to the pivot. `Command::MovePivots` moves pivots without moving shapes or children (edits `SetTransform` and `SetOffset`, merged within a step). The scene's `Units` (metres or centimetres, `Scene::units`, `with_units`, `Document::with_units` for opening) are changed with `Command::SetUnits` (edit `SetUnits`); lengths are always stored in centimetres. `ObjectId`s count up and are never reused.
- **`Scene`**: the tree, with order among brothers and sisters. Reads: `children`, `parent`, `place`, `walk` (top to bottom with depth), `subtree`, `is_inside`, `is_shown` (it and all its parents shown), `world_matrix`, `bounds`/`bounds_of` (world boxes of what is shown), `unused_name`.
- **`Command`**: Create, Insert, Rename, SetVisible, Move (reparent or reorder, before the child at an index), Delete (with everything inside), Group, Ungroup. `describe` gives the Edit menu's wording.
- **`Command::SetPrimitive`**: new settings for primitives of the same kind (clamped; other kinds refused with `SceneError::WrongKind`), as a reversible `SetKind` edit.
- **Instances and duplicates** ([decision 0055](../../docs/decisions/0055-basics.md)): `ObjectKind::Instance(source)` shows another object's final shape (`Scene::evaluated` follows it; never an instance of an instance; `Command::Record` on one fails with `SceneError::Instance`; `MakeEditable` bakes it). `Command::Duplicate { ids, instances }` copies subtrees after their originals (`copy_tree`), naming copies with `unused_name`. `Command::Delete` bakes instances of what it deletes first (`bake_instances`). `Scene::from_objects` checks every instance's source. `Command::SplitOff` records a `DeleteElements` on the shape and inserts a `keep_only` copy after it; `Command::Join` bakes the shapes into the first with `mesh::parts::combine` (each placed by its world matrix seen from the first) and deletes the others, moving their contents out first. `Operation::SlidePoints` (`mesh::edit::Slide`s, anchors for the points then their neighbours, each found again as the nearest point).
- **Collision** ([decision 0078](../../docs/decisions/0078-kit-export.md)): `Object::collision`, a `Collision` (`None`, `Mesh`, `Convex`, `Parts`, `Box`; `ALL`, `name`, `description`); `Command::SetCollision` sets it on the objects with a shape, as an undo step.
- **Collision shapes** (`collision.rs`, `collision_tests.rs`, [decision 0151](../../docs/decisions/0151-collision-hulls.md)): `Object::collision_parts` (`ConvexPartsOptions`, kept when another collision is chosen) set by `Command::SetCollisionParts` (clamped; `Edit::SetCollisionParts`). `collision_shapes(mesh, collision, parts, offset)` gives the hulls (none for None and The shape itself; one of every point for Convex; the box square to the pivot; the parts, or one 64-corner hull with a reason when the shape can't be split) and a problem in plain words. The scene keeps each object's last ones (`CollisionCache`, not part of equality, forgotten on delete): `collision_state(id, shape)` (`Ready` or `Needed(CollisionJob)`, parts always heavy: `COLLISION_IN_BACKGROUND_FROM`), `store_collision`, `collision_shapes` (on the spot, for exports) and `collision_problem` (the Attributes panel's note). `with_collision_parts` for files.
- **Levels of detail** (`lod.rs`, `lod_tests.rs`, [decision 0155](../../docs/decisions/0155-levels-of-detail.md)): `Object::lods` (`Lods { on, levels: Vec<LodLevel { ratio, distance }>, preview }`, 3 standard levels, up to `MAX_LEVELS` 8; `clamped`, `with_count`, `next_level`, `targets` never finer than the level before) set by `Command::SetLods` (clamped; `Edit::SetLods`). `lod_shapes(mesh, lods, hardness)` makes the levels with `mesh::decimate::decimate_levels` (Decimate's standard Keep, nested). The scene keeps each object's last levels (`LodCache`, not part of equality, forgotten on delete; keyed by shape, ratios and smoothing, not distances): `lod_state(id, shape)` (`Ready` or `Needed(LodJob)`, heavy from `LODS_IN_BACKGROUND_FROM` 5 000 triangles), `store_lods`, `lod_shapes` (on the spot, for exports) and `lods_if_ready` (the Attributes panel's counts). `with_lods` for files.
- **Live mirror** ([decision 0088](../../docs/decisions/0088-live-mirror.md)): `Object::mirror` (`LiveMirror`: axes, the world point its planes go through, weld distance; `is_valid`, `across_x`), set by `Command::SetMirror { ids, mirror }` ("Mirror" / "Mirror off"; groups left alone, broken values refused with `NotANumber`) through `Edit::SetMirror`. `Scene::mirror_copies(id)` gives the copies' matrices in the shape's own space (each combination of axes, reflecting across the world planes), `with_mirror(id, shape)` the shape with them (welded where points meet near a plane, the distance measured in the world), and `shown_shape(id)` the evaluated shape so (the same `Arc` without a mirror). `bounds_of` includes the twins. Tests: `live_mirror_tests.rs`.
- **The edit list** ([decision 0092](../../docs/decisions/0092-the-edit-list.md)): `Command::DeleteEdit { id, edit }`; `Command::Freeze { id, edit }` bakes the base and the edits up to `edit` (`stack::evaluate_once`) into a plain-mesh base, removing them and the layers below, refused per `Stack::freeze_problem` (kept or switched-off edits or layers); `frozen_count`. Tests in `edit_list_tests`.
- **Edit layers** ([decision 0091](../../docs/decisions/0091-edit-layers.md)): `Command::AddLayer(ids)` (an empty layer on top of each shape, named by `Stack::next_layer_name`; groups and instances skipped) and `Command::ChangeLayer { id, index, change: LayerChange }` (`SetEnabled`, `Rename`, `Move { up }`, `MergeDown` with both layers on, `Delete`), built from `InsertLayer`/`RemoveLayer`. `Command::Record` puts new edits into `Stack::recording_layer()`, the top layer that is on, or a new layer when none is. Tests in `layer_tests`.
- **UVs survive modelling** ([decision 0104](../../docs/decisions/0104-uvs-that-survive-modelling.md)): `Operation::mends_uvs` (every operation listed: those that add, remove or rewire polygons mend); `Operation::apply` clones the shape before such an edit and runs `uv::mend` after it. Tests in `uv_survive_tests.rs` cover:
  - an extrude after a hand turn, changed and undone;
  - UV work replayed on a cube given more segments;
  - moved points leaving UVs alone.
- **Packing** ([decision 0101](../../docs/decisions/0101-packing.md)): `Operation::PackUvs { islands }` moves each island (its polygons, by id) by its move with `uv::pack::place`; never mirrored; no anchors.
- **Trim sheets** ([decision 0106](../../docs/decisions/0106-trim-sheets.md)): `trim::TrimSheet { rows }` (`TrimRow { name, bottom, top }`, shares of the texture's height; `even`, `is_valid`, `row_at`; `MAX_TRIM_ROWS`, `MAX_ROW_NAME`), kept by the scene (`Scene::trim`, `with_trim`) and changed by `Command::SetTrim` (one undo step, `Edit::SetTrim`). `Operation::PlaceOnTrim { faces, bottom, top, anchors }` lays pieces on a row with `uv::trim::place`; never mirrored.
- **Texture settings** ([decision 0100](../../docs/decisions/0100-texture-size-and-texel-density.md)): `TextureSettings { size, density }` (pixels; pixels per metre; `is_valid`, `uv_per_unit`, `pixels_per_metre`), `TEXTURE_SIZES`, `MAX_DENSITY`, kept by the scene (`Scene::texture`, `with_texture`) and changed by `Command::SetTexture` (one undo step, `Edit::SetTexture`). `Operation::SetTexelDensity { faces, density, anchors }` scales pieces to a density (texture widths per scene unit) with `uv::density::set_density`.
- **Locked pieces and piece settings** ([decision 0112](../../docs/decisions/0112-locked-pieces-and-piece-settings.md)): `Operation::SetUvLock { faces, lock, anchors }` and `SetPieceSettings { faces, settings, anchors }` (`PieceSettings` re-exported from `uv`; not mirrored; the density checked by `numbers_are_real`) run `mesh::uvs::set_locked` and `set_piece_values`. Tests in `uv_pin_tests.rs` and `uv_survive_tests.rs`.
- **Layout snapshots** ([decision 0114](../../docs/decisions/0114-layout-snapshots.md)): `snapshot::UvSnapshot` (a name and each object's `mesh::uvs::UvLayout`, shared), `take`, `restore` and `layout`; `snapshots_are_valid`, `free_name`, `MAX_SNAPSHOTS`, `MAX_SNAPSHOT_NAME`. `Scene::snapshots`, `Command::SetSnapshots` (undoable, `Edit::SetSnapshots`) and `Operation::RestoreUvs { layout }` (not mirrored) running `mesh::uvs::restore_layout`. Tests in `snapshot/tests.rs`.
- **Seam preferences and stretch budgets** ([decision 0111](../../docs/decisions/0111-seam-preferences-and-stretch-budget.md)): `Operation::SetSeamPreference { edges, preference, anchors }` (mirrored with symmetry like seams) and `SetStretchBudget { faces, budget, anchors }` (not mirrored; budgets checked by `numbers_are_real`) run `mesh::uvs::set_seam_preference` and `set_budget`. Tests in `uv_pin_tests.rs`.
- **Pins and unwrap methods** ([decision 0109](../../docs/decisions/0109-pins-and-unwrap-methods.md)): `Operation::SetUvPins { faces, points, pin, anchors }` pins or unpins UVs through `mesh::uvs::set_pins`, found again as `TransformUvs` is; never mirrored. `Operation::Unwrap` carries `options` (`UnwrapOptions`, with `UnwrapMethod`, `UnfoldAxis` and `HideSeams`, re-exported from `uv`) and runs `uv::unwrap_with`; `RelaxUvs` carries its `steps` ([decision 0110](../../docs/decisions/0110-controllable-automation.md)). Mending is the project's: `Scene::mending`, `Command::SetMending` (`MendOptions`, checked with `is_real`), carried into evaluation by `EvalContext { mending }`; the cache works an object out again when it changes. Tests in `uv_survive_tests.rs`. Tests in `uv_pin_tests.rs`.
- **Unwrap, Straighten, Relax** ([decision 0099](../../docs/decisions/0099-unwrap-relax-straighten.md)): `Operation::Unwrap { faces, options, anchors }`, `StraightenUvs { faces, anchors }` and `RelaxUvs { faces, steps, anchors }` run `uv::unwrap`, `uv::straighten` and `uv::relax` on the polygons found again by their anchors; never mirrored.
- **Seams** ([decision 0098](../../docs/decisions/0098-seams.md)): `Operation::SetSeams { edges, seam, anchors }` marks or clears seams through `mesh::uvs::set_seams` (only the marks; the UVs follow when unwrapped), finding the edges again by their anchors; mirrored with symmetry like hard edges.
- **UVs moved by hand** ([decision 0097](../../docs/decisions/0097-editing-uvs-by-hand.md)): `Operation::TransformUvs { faces, points, transform: glam::Affine2, anchors }` moves, turns or scales the UVs of polygons (or, with none, points) through `mesh::uvs::transform_uvs` (shared location: corners of the same point at the same spot move together), finding them again by their anchors; described "Move the UVs of N polygons/points"; never mirrored by symmetry.
- **Apply transforms** ([decision 0089](../../docs/decisions/0089-apply-transforms.md)): `Command::ApplyTransforms { ids, parts: ApplyParts }` (position, rotation, scale; `ROTATION_AND_SCALE`, `ALL`, `kept`, `name`) sets the objects' placement to what isn't applied, records `Operation::PlaceShape { matrix }` (new⁻¹ · old · offset) on objects with a shape, clears the offset and places their children again so nothing moves; parents first, instances left alone, a flattened object refused, one undo step ("Apply rotation and scale"). Tests: `apply_tests.rs`.
- **Stable element names** ([decision 0090](../../docs/decisions/0090-stable-element-names.md)): the stack names a primitive's base (`Mesh::name_base`) and each edit's work after its `EditId` (`begin_naming`/`end_naming` round `Operation::apply`), so finding points again can tell apart points on the same spot by their names (`stack_tests.rs`: points on the same spot told apart by their names).
- **Game project folder** ([decision 0076](../../docs/decisions/0076-engine-export-paths.md)): `ExportTarget { folder, on_save }` (`is_valid`: not empty, at most `MAX_FOLDER_LEN` bytes, one line) on the scene beside the units (`Scene::export_target`, `with_export_target`, `Document::with_export_target`); `Command::SetExportTarget` changes it as an undo step. The scene keeps the path only as text.
- **Materials** ([decision 0074](../../docs/decisions/0074-material-slots.md)): `Object::materials`, a list of `Material` (name, sRGB colour; `Material::standard` is "Default" in the viewport's grey, `is_valid`); empty means the standard one (`Object::material_list`). `Scene::material_list(id)` gives what an object's polygons use (an instance its original's). `Command::SetMaterials` replaces a list (names trimmed, at most `MAX_SLOTS`, refused with `EmptyName` or `TooManyMaterials`), as `Edit::SetMaterials`. `Operation::SetMaterial { faces, slot, anchors }` is the recorded edit giving polygons a slot (mirrored together with symmetry). `Command::Join` merges the parts' lists by name and renumbers their slots; making an instance editable copies its original's list. Tests in `tests.rs`.
- **Stack** (`stack`, [decision 0021](../../docs/decisions/0021-object-stack-foundation.md)): an object's kind is its base (`ObjectKind::Primitive`, or `ObjectKind::Mesh` for a plain mesh) and `Object::stack` holds `Layer`s of `Operation`s (`MovePoints`, `TransformPoints`, and from [decision 0038](../../docs/decisions/0038-extrude-and-inset.md) `ExtrudePolygons` (with an `ExtrudeShape`, [decision 0070](../../docs/decisions/0070-extrude-freedom.md): direction, end size, steps and flat end, `EditSettings::extrude`; older extrudes are `ExtrudeShape::EARLIER`; a bend set back to 0° on a bent extrude makes a straight one again), `InsetPolygons` and `ExtrudeEdges`, with a `Grouping`, re-exported from `mesh`, and from [decision 0050](../../docs/decisions/0050-pipe-bends.md) `ExtrudeBent` with a `Bend` (a straight extrude given a bend through `Operation::with` becomes one; past one turn it spirals, [decision 0062](../../docs/decisions/0062-spirals.md), described "with a spiral", and its mirrored copy gets `Bend::mirrored`; [decision 0081](../../docs/decisions/0081-bends-keep-their-shape-on-stretched-objects.md)); and from [decision 0040](../../docs/decisions/0040-bevel.md) `BevelEdges` with a `BevelShape` and `BevelPoints`; and from [decision 0086](../../docs/decisions/0086-groove.md) `Groove` with a `GrooveShape` and stretch, described as a groove or a ridge by its depth, mirrored together with its mirror; and from [decision 0047](../../docs/decisions/0047-loop-cut.md) `LoopCut`: an edge, cuts and a position, with anchors; found again by its anchors, it takes the first edge between the points found and turns the position round if that edge's lower point lies where the other end was, so the cut stays on its side; from [decision 0051](../../docs/decisions/0051-connect-bridge-weld.md) `Connect`, `Bridge` and `Weld` (a weld's target found again as the point nearest where it was); from [decision 0052](../../docs/decisions/0052-clean-up.md) `DeleteElements`, `Dissolve`, `FillHoles` and `FlipPolygons` (ids and anchors; deleted or dissolved elements found again as those within the anchored points) and `FixFacing` (worked out from the shape as it is); from [decision 0053](../../docs/decisions/0053-hard-and-soft-edges.md) `SetHardEdges` (ids and anchors) and `HardEdgesByAngle` (an angle, `EditSettings::angle`, worked out from the shape as it is); and from [decision 0049](../../docs/decisions/0049-slit.md) `Slit` with a `SlitLine`, re-exported from `mesh` with `Eye`: no ids or anchors, worked out from the shape as it is; and from [decision 0059](../../docs/decisions/0059-symmetry-for-every-edit.md) `Mirrored { op, mirror }`, an edit made with symmetry on: `mirroring::both_sides` finds its mirror on the shape just before it and makes it on the selection and its mirror together, or as mirrored copies (left out when one would be the edit itself); `settings`, `with`, `anchors` and `points` are the edit's own, and `Operation::inner` gives it; `mirroring::mirrors` says which edits can be mirrored; and `WeldMiddle { mirror, distance }`, the middle welded once when asked). Extrudes, insets and bevels (`Operation::keeps_stretch`) hold the object's `stretch` when made ([decisions 0081](../../docs/decisions/0081-bends-keep-their-shape-on-stretched-objects.md) and [0082](../../docs/decisions/0082-edits-keep-their-shape-on-stretched-objects.md)) and are applied through `mesh::stretch::as_seen`; `Operation::stretch`, `with_stretch` and `EditSettings::stretch` read and change it, and `scene::stretch_of(matrix)` works one out from an object's shape matrix. Each recorded edit is an `Entry` ([decision 0044](../../docs/decisions/0044-edits-that-hold.md)): an `EditId` (unique among the object's edits; `Stack::next_edit_id`, `Stack::entry`, `repeated_edit_id`), an on/off and a pinned switch, and the operation. `Command::Record` adds one to the top layer (making an `Edits` layer first), as `InsertLayer`/`InsertEntry` edits; `ReplaceOperation` keeps the id and switches; `SetEditEnabled` and `SetEditPinned` change the switches (switched-off edits are skipped, and left out by Make editable); `Scene::from_objects` refuses two edits sharing an id. An extrude, inset, bevel or loop cut (its ring's polygons times the cuts) that would grow the shape past `MAX_SHAPE_POLYGONS` (30 million) is skipped, with `StackWarning::too_big` ([decision 0045](../../docs/decisions/0045-limits-for-files.md)). Applying an edit trusts its ids when the shape below has the structure it was made on (`Anchors::same_structure`), searches by position only when it doesn't; groups refuse (`SceneError::NoShape`). `Scene::evaluated` gives the final shape (`Evaluated`: mesh plus `StackWarning`s for operations whose points are gone), from a per-layer cache inside the scene that re-evaluates only from the first changed layer (or everything, when the base changed), and inside that layer only from the first changed edit, starting from a kept shape: each layer keeps its shape after all its edits and after all but the last ([decision 0042](../../docs/decisions/0042-fast-edits.md)), so adding, dragging or changing the last edit applies one edit and undoing it none; `Scene::local_bounds` and the world bounds use it. `Command::InsertMany` adds ready-made objects at the end of the top level as one step, a taken name getting a number (importing, [decision 0071](../../docs/decisions/0071-obj-export-and-import.md)). `Command::MakeEditable` bakes shapes into mesh bases and empties their stacks; `Scene::can_make_editable` says whether it would change an object. Evaluation takes an `EvalContext`, empty until animation.
- **Rebuilding saved scenes**: `Scene::from_objects` (objects in tree order, checked: unique ids, parents first, names, groups without edits; `next_object_id` raised past every id), `Document::from_scene` (with a selection and a fresh history), `Stack::from_layers`, `ObjectId::from_raw`.
- **`Document`**: the scene, its history and the selection. `execute` carries out a command as small reversible edits and records them as one step (failures change nothing; no-ops record nothing); `undo`/`redo` replay the edits' opposites or the edits. `execute_merging` folds a change into the previous step when made with the same key (one drag of a value is one step; dragging back to the start leaves no step; a step undo returned to never merges). History is bounded by memory, not steps ([decision 0022](../../docs/decisions/0022-undo-memory-budget.md)): `memory` estimates each step's size (meshes in full), the history keeps a running total, and over the `HistoryLimit` (`Bytes`, standard 2 GB, or `Unlimited`) it forgets the oldest undo steps first, then far redo steps, always keeping the newest. `set_history_limit`, `history_memory`, `forgotten_steps`, `redo_steps`. Selection is set directly and is not an undo step yet. `revision` goes up with every change to the scene (not the selection), so the interface knows when to redraw.
- **Selected elements** ([decision 0029](../../docs/decisions/0029-selection.md)): `Document::elements(id)` gives an object's `ElementSelection`; `select_elements(changes, label, merge_key)` changes one or more objects' as one undo step (a merge key folds a brush stroke into one step; no step when nothing changes). Undo and redo restore them; a step holding only selection changes doesn't change `revision` (nothing to save). `element_revision` goes up with every change. Deleted objects lose theirs. Not saved in project files yet. **Selected objects** are undoable too: `set_selection`, `toggle_selected` and `clear_selection` record a step (labelled "Select Cube", "Select 3 objects", "Deselect all"), and `select_objects(ids, merge_key)` merges a brush stroke; `from_scene` sets its selection without one. **Transforms** ([decision 0030](../../docs/decisions/0030-transform-gizmos.md)): `Command::SetTransforms` (edit `SetTransform`, merged within a step; values that aren't numbers refused with `SceneError::NotANumber`); `Operation::TransformPoints` (points moved by a matrix in the object's own space); `Transform::from_matrix` (the reverse of `matrix`, nearest without skew); `MovePoints` and `TransformPoints` carry optional `mesh::Anchors`; when the IDs no longer fit (a settings change renumbered the points) the edit finds its points again by position, and its `StackWarning` says so (`found_again`) as well as how many are missing ([decision 0032](../../docs/decisions/0032-edits-find-their-points-again.md)); `Operation::points` gives the IDs and anchors (empty for polygon and edge operations), `Operation::anchors` the anchors of any. Extrudes and insets found again by their anchors take the polygons (or edges) whose points were all found. `Operation::settings` and `Operation::with` read and change an extrude's, inset's, bevel's or loop cut's `EditSettings` (each `None` when the edit hasn't it: distance or width, grouping, segments, profile, cuts, position, through, bend, bridge), with `distance`, `grouping` and `with_settings` as shorthands, `EditSettings::bevel_options` carries a bevel of edges' further settings ([decision 0085](../../docs/decisions/0085-bevel-control.md)) and `EditSettings::improved` marks a changed bevel as improved ([decision 0141](../../docs/decisions/0141-improved-bevel.md)) and `EditSettings::groove` a groove's shape (its depth is the `distance`); and `Operation::numbers_are_real` (and `Record`/`ReplaceOperation` refuse other edits with `SceneError::NotANumber`, [decision 0041](../../docs/decisions/0041-saves-that-always-reopen.md)); `Command::ReplaceOperation` puts a changed edit in its place (as `RemoveOperation` and `InsertOperation` edits; the same edit again records nothing), for the Attributes panel's Last edit. `Document::execute_labelled` (a command with the caller's undo label) and `Document::abandon(key)` (takes back and forgets the last step if made with that merge key: a gizmo drag replaces its previous frame, and cancels).

Tests: pivots and units (`pivot_tests`: moving and turning a pivot leaves the shape and the children where they were, undo puts back transform and offset, flat or not-a-number pivots refused, no step when nothing moves, copies and joins keep shapes in place, drags merge into one step, units as an undoable step and for new or opened scenes); mirrored edits (`mirroring_tests`: an extrude on both sides, a selection crossing the middle made as one slab, loop cuts copied unless their ring crosses the middle, slits, welds and bent extrudes copied and the result symmetrical, delete, flip and inset on both sides, settings changing both sides, a shape without a mirror there, and which edits mirror); the undo budget (`history_tests`: sizes growing and moving between undo and redo, forgetting the oldest, keeping the newest however large, unlimited, lowering the limit, redo steps, merged drags not growing, and a random run checking the running total against a fresh measure); the stack (`stack_tests`: recording, undo and redo of edits, replay after a settings change (a lifted top staying lifted with more segments, edits without anchors keeping their IDs), warnings for missing points, bounds following edits, an edit that would make the shape too large skipped with a warning, edit ids kept through undo, redo and setting changes, switches (skipped, restored, no step when unchanged, unknown edits refused, Make editable), repeated ids refused, an earlier extrude made longer keeping a later move on its ring, the cache re-evaluating only what changed (checkpoints: a drag after 20 edits applying one, a new edit then its drag, an earlier change replaying all, undo using the checkpoint, warnings kept right, layers switched off and on), switched-off layers, mesh bases, deleting and undoing, copies, determinism, and Make editable with groups, meshes, several objects and unknown ids); every command, its undo and redo (ids included), refused moves (into itself, unknown objects), names, visibility through parents, world bounds through parent transforms, and a fuzz test running random command sequences with seeds, checking the tree stays whole and that undoing everything and redoing it all lands exactly where it was, including recorded edits, Make editable and the evaluated shapes.

Speed: `tests/edit_speed.rs` is the edit-speed check (ignored in the normal run; `tools/heavy/heavy-check.sh` runs it in release mode): a drag after 200 edits on a 3k-polygon prop, dragging an extrude on the heavy shape, bevelling every edge and point of a 32k-polygon sphere, and finding 1,000 points again on the heavy shape, each against a target.

- **Bend along curve and Shrinkwrap** (`ModifierKind::BendAlongCurve(CurveBendSettings)`, `ModifierKind::Shrinkwrap(ShrinkwrapSettings)`, `wrap.rs`, `wrap_tests.rs`, [decision 0147](../../docs/decisions/0147-curve-deform-and-shrinkwrap.md)): family Deform, after Lattice on the palette.
