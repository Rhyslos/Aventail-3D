# mesh

**Layer 0 · Geometry**

Polygon meshes: the data structure, topology queries and modelling operations.

## Purpose

The foundation everything else builds on: an n-gon mesh with full adjacency (vertices, edges, faces, face corners), stable element IDs, typed attribute layers (hard edges, seams, UVs, later skin weights), and every topology operation as a pure function (extrude, inset, bevel, loop cut, bridge, weld and so on). See DESIGN.md §4.1.

## Must never

- Know about objects, scenes, undo, files, the GPU or UI
- Change a mesh in place behind the caller's back: operations take a mesh and return a result
- Reuse a deleted element's ID
- Crash or allocate without bound on degenerate input (zero-length edges, n-gons, open boundaries, non-manifold geometry)

## Depends on

No other app crates. Outside libraries: `glam` (maths).

## Status

- **`Bounds`**: an upright bounding box (`new`, `around` points, `union`, `centre`, `size`, `radius`, `contains`), used for framing the view. Skips points that aren't real numbers.

- **`Mesh`** (`polygon`): points and n-gon faces by id (`VertexId`, `FaceId`; ids count up and are never reused), and its attribute layers, hard edges (`EdgeKey`) among them. `add_face` refuses faces with fewer than three corners, unknown points or repeated points (`MeshError`, in plain words). `edges`, `bounds` and `face_normal` (Newell's method, so it is sensible for n-gons; straight up for a face with no area). Adjacency lives in `Topology`.
  - **Built for heavy meshes** ([decision 0035](../../docs/decisions/0035-heavy-meshes.md)): its parts are shared between copies until one changes them: positions in chunks of 4096 (each keeping the box around its points), faces as one flat list of corners with where each face starts (`face_layout`), hard edges. Copying is cheap, and a move copies only the chunks it touches. `same_structure` (the very same faces and hard edges) and `moved_since` (which points moved, comparing only chunks that aren't shared) let caches update only what changed. `topology` is worked out once per set of faces and kept with them; `edges` comes from it. `point_slots` and `face_slots` iterate the slots, `point_slot_count` counts them.
- **Attribute layers** (`attributes`, [decision 0043](../../docs/decisions/0043-attribute-layers-and-the-builder.md)): named values per point, edge, polygon or polygon corner (`Domain`), of one kind each (`AttributeKind`: on/off, whole number, 1–4 numbers), with a default. `Mesh::add_attribute`, `remove_attribute`, `attributes`, `attribute(name, AttributeElement)`, `set_attribute` (refusing the wrong domain or kind, missing elements and values that aren't numbers: `AttributeError`). Point, polygon and corner values are kept in typed lists, filled only as far as a value was set; edge values by edge, only where not the default. Layers are shared between copies until changed (`same_structure` checks them too). Hard edges are the built-in edge layer `HARD`, dropped when empty. `attribute_values` and `set_attribute_values` hand over and take back whole layers (`AttributeValues`), every value checked. `Value::mix` blends values (numbers blend, on/off and whole numbers take the nearer end).
- **UVs:** `attributes::UV` names the corner layer holding UV coordinates (Vec2), filled by OBJ import now and the UV tools later ([decision 0071](../../docs/decisions/0071-obj-export-and-import.md)).
- **Builder** (`builder`, decision 0043): `Builder::new(original, mesh)` makes and rewires geometry carrying every attribute layer over: `point(position, PointFrom)` (copy or between two points of the mesh being built), `face(corners with CornerFrom, like)` (polygon values from an original polygon, corner values copied or blended from original corners), `rewrite_faces`, and `copy_edge`, `move_edge`, `clear_edge` for edge values. Extrude, inset, extrude edges, bevel, loop cut and slit use it.
- **Deleting polygons**: `Mesh::delete_faces` (ids never reused; points stay for `remove_unused_vertices`; other polygons' corner values kept; edge values only on edges still used).
- **Bake check** (`bake`, [decision 0077](../../docs/decisions/0077-bake-check.md)): `BakeReport::new(mesh)` lists the elements that would spoil a bake (flipped polygons, non-manifold edges, flat polygons, short edges, n-gons bent past `BENT_SHARE` or cut into triangles sharper than `SLIVER_DEGREES`, loose points); `short_edge_groups` gives the points to weld together. Every primitive is clean (tested).
- **Material slots** (`material`, [decision 0074](../../docs/decisions/0074-material-slots.md)): `MATERIAL` names the polygon layer holding each polygon's slot number (`MAX_SLOTS` 64); `slot_of` (0 when unset), `faces_with_slot`, and `set_material` (re-exported from `edit`; missing polygons counted, a slot past the last refused). Every edit carries the layer along, so walls take their polygon's slot. Tests: slots set and found, refusals, extrude, inset and loop cut keeping them.
- **Samples** (`samples`): `heavy_ball(polygons, radius)`, a closed, bumpy ball of about that many polygons standing on the ground, for the heavy-mesh check and Help → Add a heavy test shape.
- **Parallel work** (`parallel`): `in_parallel(count, work)` splits work over the computer's cores when there is enough of it (used for shading and picking indexes).
- **Shading** (`shading`): each face corner's normal is the angle-weighted average of the faces joined to it across soft edges at its point. `smooth_groups` gives each corner its smooth group (split by hard edges; unchanged while points move), `corner_shading` the groups and every corner's normal in a few passes, `corner_normal_list`/`corner_normals` the normals alone. For updates after a move, `shading_reach` gives the points whose shading changes and `group_normals_at` works out one point's groups again. `shaded_triangles` cuts faces into triangles with those normals, as `triangulate` cuts them. `smooth_groups_with` and `corner_shading_with` take a `Hardness` ([decision 0064](../../docs/decisions/0064-automatic-hard-edges.md)): `Marked` (the hard edges) or `ByAngle(degrees)` (where polygons meet more sharply, or more than two share an edge, whatever is marked); `angle_hardness_changed` says whether moving points turned an edge around them sharp or soft. Groups and, by angle, face facings are worked out a share of the points or faces per thread.
- **Editing** (`edit`): modelling operations as pure functions on a mesh, working on the element ids that still exist and reporting how many were missing (`Outcome`) instead of failing. So far `move_points`, the first operation the scene's stack records. `Mesh::set_position`, `point_slots`/`face_slots`/`hard_edges` and `Mesh::from_slots` (rebuilds a saved mesh with its ids, checking every value), `Mesh::structure_fingerprint` (a fixed hash of the polygons' corners and the point slot count, kept with the faces), `Mesh::memory_size` (an estimate, for the undo budget, counting the topology too: as built, or estimated before), `Mesh::remove_unused_vertices` (deletes the unused ones of many points in one pass), and `VertexId::from_raw`/`FaceId::from_raw` for ids recorded earlier. `transform_points` moves points by an affine matrix (turns and scaling), each once, missing ones counted, a matrix that isn't numbers moving nothing.
- **Bevel** (`bevel`, [decision 0040](../../docs/decisions/0040-bevel.md), re-exported from `edit`): `bevel_edges` with a `BevelShape` (width, segments 1–`MAX_SEGMENTS`, profile from hollow -1 through flat 0 to round 1; `clamped`) and `bevel_points`. Fans of polygons around each end; corners meet both offsets or slide along plain edges; strips of rows along a quadratic profile; shared end rows, the end polygon taking a lone strip's end, or one corner polygon; hard edges carried over; unused points deleted in one pass. Rows are found by their ends in a lookup table and a point's cuts by a map range, so big selections take time in proportion to their size ([decision 0042](../../docs/decisions/0042-fast-edits.md)). `Mesh::rewrite_faces` (new corners, any number, ids kept) and `Mesh::remove_unused_vertex`. `set_hard` ignores a point joined to itself and missing points; `from_slots` leaves out such hard edges instead of refusing the mesh; long faces are checked for repeated corners by sorting ([decision 0041](../../docs/decisions/0041-saves-that-always-reopen.md)). Adding or changing faces clears the kept topology.
- **Loop cut** (`loopcut`, [decision 0047](../../docs/decisions/0047-loop-cut.md), `loop_cut` re-exported from `edit`): `loop_cut(mesh, edge, cuts, position)` walks the ring of four-sided polygons across `edge` both ways (closing on itself, or stopping at a polygon that isn't four-sided, a border, or after cutting an edge shared by three or more polygons), cuts every ring edge at the same shares measured from the same side (`cuts` 1–`MAX_CUTS` spread evenly, slid by `position` and kept 1% inside the edge), splits each crossed polygon into strips (the first keeps its id, winding kept), and gives the polygons at the ring's ends the new points as corners, so no gap opens. Values come along through the builder; a cut edge's values go to each piece. `ring_polygons` (how many polygons it would cut, for the size guard) and `preview` (the lines it would add, for the tool) walk the same ring without changing anything.
- **Slit** (`slit`, [decision 0049](../../docs/decisions/0049-slit.md), `slit`, `Eye` and `SlitLine` re-exported from `edit`): `slit(mesh, line)` cuts along a `SlitLine` (points in the mesh's own space, an `Eye`: a point, or a direction for flat views, and `through`). Each stretch sweeps a fan from the eye; per polygon, the edges it crosses within the stretch and the line points inside the polygon are put in order along the line and paired into chains (in at the border, any turns inside, out at the border). Unless `through`, a polygon must face the eye and its chain's middle must not be hidden (a ray against every polygon). New edge points are shared per edge and stretch; polygons beside a cut edge take them as corners; each chain splits the piece of its polygon holding both ends, keeping winding and the first piece's id. `slit_size` counts the pieces for the size guard; at most `MAX_LINE_POINTS` (256) points. A closed line ([decision 0058](../../docs/decisions/0058-closed-slit.md), `SlitLine::is_closed`: the first point again at the end) adds the stretch back to the start; where it starts inside a polygon, that polygon's meetings are turned round to begin after the first exit. Line points on a polygon's border are checked just before and after: touching from inside counts twice (out and back in), from outside not at all; a loop touching one spot becomes an `InnerLoop` with a `touch`, and edge spots met by several stretches share one point; with several pieces holding a chain's ends, `piece_holds` picks the one it runs through. A loop wholly inside one polygon (`InnerLoop`) is cut by `ring_cut`: the loop's shape becomes a polygon, and the ring round it two halves joined to the loop by edges from opposite outline corners (checked with `simple`, with a bounded search for other corners if a half would cross itself). The builder's `PointFrom::Inside` and `CornerFrom::Inside` blend a polygon's corners at a spot inside it (`weights_inside`, mean value weights).
- **Pipe bends** (`extrude`, [decision 0050](../../docs/decisions/0050-pipe-bends.md), `extrude_bent` and `Bend` re-exported from `edit`): `extrude_bent(mesh, faces, distance, grouping, bend)` extrudes ring after ring with `extrude_polygons` (following each point to its copies through the polygons' corners), then places the rings: with a radius, the border turned rigidly around a pivot `radius` from each piece's middle in `Bend::direction` (0 turns up, +X for upright), then the cap `distance` straight on; with no radius, the old border moved onto the halfway plane, stretched by 1 / cos(half the angle) (a mitre joint), and the cap turned and moved on. `Bend::clamped` (angle 0–`MAX_BEND_ANGLE` 3600 with a radius, or `MAX_MITRE_ANGLE` 150 without; segments 1–`MAX_BEND_SEGMENTS`; rise 0 or more), `bends`, `rings`. **Spirals** ([decision 0062](../../docs/decisions/0062-spirals.md)): past 360° (`spirals`) each row also climbs along the turn's axis by `climb_per_turn(width)` (the piece's width along the axis plus `SPIRAL_GAP`, plus `Bend::rise`) times its share of the turns; `rows` is the segments for each full turn, at most `MAX_BEND_ROWS`; `Bend::mirrored` climbs the other way, for a mirrored copy.
- **Slide** (`slide`, [decision 0055](../../docs/decisions/0055-basics.md), re-exported from `edit`): `slides_for` picks, for a move, each point's neighbour (still ones first) most nearly the way of the move and how far along (0 to 1); `slide_points` applies `Slide`s from where the points were.
- **Parts** (`parts`, decision 0055): `keep_only` (a copy with some polygons and their points, ids kept) and `combine` (shapes into one, each by a matrix; hard edges and value layers come along, corner values follow their points; a mirroring matrix turns polygons round).
- **Symmetry** (`symmetry`, [decision 0054](../../docs/decisions/0054-symmetry.md)): `mirror_plan` splits the points about to move into `MirrorGroup`s (the chosen points, and each mirror across the enabled own axes, found by position within `TOLERANCE` through a cell index), each with the axes it is mirrored across and the planes it stays on; `MirrorGroup::matrix` and `offset` turn a change into each group's. A `Mirror` ([decision 0059](../../docs/decisions/0059-symmetry-for-every-edit.md)) is the axes and the middle the planes go through (`point`, `vector`, `reflections`), with a clip switch: `weld_middle` (with a distance, for Weld the middle now) puts points near the planes on them and welds those meeting there, `keep_sides` puts points pushed across back on the plane, and `move_limits` says how far points may move without crossing; `mirror_plan` takes one, so the planes can go through any point. `Partners` finds a point's, edge's or polygon's mirror across one reflection, by position. `Anchors::mirrored` and `joined` give a mirrored edit its anchors, and `extrude::bend_towards`/`bend_direction` turn a bend's direction the mirrored way.
- **Triangulation** (`triangulate`, [decision 0057](../../docs/decisions/0057-after-phase-4-testing.md)): `triangulate(n, at, out)` and `face_triangles(mesh, corners, out)` cut a polygon into `n - 2` triangles, the same way every time: triangles as they are, quads 0–2 unless dented, flat polygons without dents as a fan, anything else laid flat on its best plane and cut along lines inside it (the shortest cut up to `BEST_CUT_UP_TO` corners, so a bent polygon is cut along its fold; ear by ear above that; the fan if nothing works). Drawing (`render`), picking (`tools`) and the selection tint use it.
- **Hard and soft edges** (`hardness`, [decision 0053](../../docs/decisions/0053-hard-and-soft-edges.md), re-exported from `edit`): `set_hard_edges`, `sharp_edges` (polygons meeting more sharply than an angle, and edges of three or more polygons; never borders or polygons with no area) and `hard_edges_by_angle` (those hard, every other inner edge soft). `harden_new_edges` marks the sharp edges of polygons an edit just made hard (Bridge and Fill hole use it, from `DEFAULT_ANGLE`, 30°). Dissolve forgets the values of edges it took away (`Mesh::forget_unused_edge_values`) and keeps two hard edges joined into one hard. Only shading changes.
- **Clean-up** (`cleanup`, [decision 0052](../../docs/decisions/0052-clean-up.md), re-exported from `edit`): `delete_elements` (points, edges or polygons with every polygon using them, then unused points), `dissolve_edges`, `dissolve_points` and `dissolve_faces` (polygons around merge into one keeping the lowest id; border elements and merges that wouldn't make one simple polygon stay; points left on a straight line go), `fill_holes` (a polygon per border loop touching the given points, every loop when none), `flip_faces`, `faces_facing_wrong` and `fix_facing` (agree with neighbours across shared edges; closed pieces face outward by signed volume), and `faces_facing` (within an angle of a direction).
- **Connect, Bridge and Weld** (`join`, [decision 0051](../../docs/decisions/0051-connect-bridge-weld.md), re-exported from `edit`): `connect_points` (selected points joined across each polygon, all the way round when three or more), `connect_edges` (each edge cut `cuts` times, the cuts joined across polygons holding two or more; neighbours take the new points), `bridge_edges` (border edges joined into chains; the first two get a band of `segments` rows, lined up by distance and `twist` for loops, a zip of triangles for different lengths, always running against the existing polygons), `bridge_faces` (two polygons removed and their borders banded), `weld` (points melted at their middle or a target; repeated corners dropped, collapsed polygons and orphaned points removed, edge values moved to the kept point).
- **Extrude and inset** (`extrude`, [decision 0038](../../docs/decisions/0038-extrude-and-inset.md), re-exported from `edit`): `extrude_polygons`, `inset_polygons` and `extrude_edges` (open border edges only; others counted in `Outcome::skipped`), each with a `Grouping` (`Together`: touching polygons, or edges meeting at a point, as one piece; `Separate`). Polygons keep their ids (`Mesh::replace_corners`, same number of corners); new points and polygons take the next ids in a fixed order; inside a piece kept together, points that touch nothing else move instead of being copied. `extrude_polygons` moves points along the average facing, lengthened at angles so every polygon ends up the full distance away (at most 4×). **Shaped extrudes** ([decision 0070](../../docs/decisions/0070-extrude-freedom.md)): `extrude_shaped(mesh, faces, distance, grouping, ExtrudeShape)` with `ExtrudeDirection` (`Straight`: the piece's size-weighted average facing for every point; `AlongNormals`: `extrude_polygons`' way; `AlongX/Y/Z`: the axis, signed by the facing), `end_size` (across the way, around the end's middle), `steps` (rings built with `extrude_polygons` at no distance, followed per piece, then placed evenly) and `flat_end`; `ExtrudeShape::EARLIER` is exactly `extrude_polygons`; `clamped` keeps 0–`MAX_END_SIZE` and 1–`MAX_EXTRUDE_STEPS`. Tests in `extrude/shaped_tests.rs`. Hard edges carry over to the new border, and to a wall's upright where a hard border edge meets it. Bad distances change nothing; missing ids are counted.
- **Anchors** (`anchors`, [decision 0032](../../docs/decisions/0032-edits-find-their-points-again.md)): `Anchors::record(mesh, kind, elements)` keeps where an edit's points, edges or polygons were (`AnchorShape`), relative to the mesh's box. `still_fit` says whether the IDs still sit there (a resized shape fits); `find` finds them again by position when they don't: the nearest point, every edge along an anchored edge, every polygon inside an anchored polygon, and how many weren't found. Anchors also keep the structure fingerprint of the shape they were made on (`same_structure`: ids can be trusted wherever points moved; [decision 0044](../../docs/decisions/0044-edits-that-hold.md)). It sorts the points into a grid first, so each element only looks near itself, and edges and polygons judge "the same place" by a quarter of their own shortest side at most, so on dense meshes they don't pick up neighbours ([decision 0042](../../docs/decisions/0042-fast-edits.md)). Flat boxes (a plane) keep distances on their flat side.
- **Topology** (`topology`, [decision 0025](../../docs/decisions/0025-mesh-topology.md)): `Topology::new(&mesh)` builds an adjacency index from the face list:
- **Selection** (`selection`, [decision 0029](../../docs/decisions/0029-selection.md)): `Element` (point, edge or polygon) and `ElementKind`; `ElementSelection` keeps the selected points, edges and polygons apart, by ID, with `combine` (`Combine::Replace`, `Add`, `Remove`, `Toggle`) and `valid_in` (drops IDs the mesh no longer has). Commands worked out from the topology: `all`, `inverted`, `grown` and `shrunk` (one step through shared points), `loop_through` and `ring_through` (an edge's loop or ring as points, edges, or the band of polygons across or along it), and `face_edges`.
  - `edge_uses` (faces and the direction each runs), `edge_faces`, `vertex_edges`, `vertex_faces`, `face_edges` (corner order), `face_neighbours`, `vertex_neighbours`;
  - `is_border`, `border_edges`, `is_non_manifold`, `non_manifold_edges`, `flipped_edges`, `pieces`;
  - `edge_loop` (straight on through points where four quads meet, or along a border; stops at poles, triangles, n-gons) and `edge_ring` (across quads to the opposite edge).

  Edges are named by their points (`EdgeKey`). Everything is kept in flat lists (each element's neighbours one after another, with where each starts), built in one pass with a counting sort: a 3-million-polygon mesh in about 0.6 s. `edge_list` gives the edges as a slice. The mesh keeps its topology with its faces (`Mesh::topology`); `Topology::new` builds a fresh one.
- **Check** (`check`): `MeshReport` counts points, edges, faces, triangles (fan count) and pieces, open borders, and problems (non-manifold, flipped, zero-length edges, faces with no area, loose points); `is_closed`, `has_problems`.
- **Primitives** (`primitives`, [decision 0020](../../docs/decisions/0020-primitives.md)): `Primitive` (cube, plane, cylinder, sphere, disc, tube, cone) with per-kind settings and standard values, `PrimitiveKind` (the kind without settings, with name and description), `clamped` (sizes 1 mm to 10 km, segment and side limits, holes smaller than their shape), `mesh` and analytic `bounds`. Shapes are 1 m across as standard and stand on the ground with the pivot at the base centre; round ends are single n-gons as standard, or cut into rings (`cap_segments`, `ring_segments`) and/or met at a centre point (`cap_centre`, `centre_point`), built by `round_end`; round sides are soft and rims hard; the cube's twelve edges are hard. `Primitive::polygon_estimate` gives an upper bound on the polygon count from the settings alone, for checking files before building anything ([decision 0045](../../docs/decisions/0045-limits-for-files.md)).
