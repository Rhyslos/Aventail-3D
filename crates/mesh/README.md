# mesh

**Layer 0 · Geometry**

Polygon meshes: the data structure, topology queries and modelling operations.

## Purpose

The foundation everything else builds on: an n-gon mesh with full adjacency (vertices, edges, faces, face corners), stable element IDs, typed attribute layers (hard edges, seams, UVs, later skin weights), and every topology operation as a pure function (extrude, inset, bevel, loop cut, bridge, weld and so on). See DESIGN.md §4.1.

## Must never

- Know about objects, scenes, undo, files, the GPU or UI
- Change a mesh in place behind the caller's back: operations take a mesh and return a result
- Reuse a deleted element's ID
- Crash or allocate without bound on degenerate input (zero-length edges, n-gons, open boundaries, non-manifold geometry)

## Depends on

`words` (the language layer, the same layer: error messages and the primitives' names and descriptions go through it, [decision 0134](../../docs/decisions/0134-shared-language-crate.md)). Outside libraries: `glam` (maths).

## Status

- **UVs and seams** ([decision 0095](../../docs/decisions/0095-uvs-before-advanced-modelling.md)): the `uv` corner layer and the `seam` edge layer (`attributes::SEAM`); `uvs::Uvs` reads them once for many lookups; `islands`, `island_of_each`, `uv_splits`, `seams`, `seams_from_splits`. Editing by hand ([decision 0097](../../docs/decisions/0097-editing-uvs-by-hand.md)): `corners_moved` (the corners a change moves: shared location, so islands never tear), `transform_uvs` (an `Affine2` in the texture's units; an `edit::Outcome`), `uv_bounds` and `uv_centre`. `average_density` gives a mesh's texel density (decision 0102), `set_uvs` writes every corner's UV at once ([decision 0099](../../docs/decisions/0099-unwrap-relax-straighten.md)). `pins` and `set_pins` read and set pinned UVs (the `uv_pin` corner layer, `attributes::UV_PIN`; pinning a polygon or point pins the corners sharing its spot, and the layer goes when nothing is pinned; [decision 0109](../../docs/decisions/0109-pins-and-unwrap-methods.md)). `seam_preference`, `seam_preferences` and `set_seam_preference` read and set seam preferences (`SeamPreference`: free, prefer, never; the `seam_preference` edge layer), and `budgets`, `budget_of` and `set_budget` stretch budgets (the `uv_budget` polygon layer, `BUDGET_RANGE`) ([decision 0111](../../docs/decisions/0111-seam-preferences-and-stretch-budget.md)). `locked`, `any_locked` and `set_locked` read and set locked UVs (the `uv_lock` polygon layer), and `PieceValues`, `piece_values` and `set_piece_values` a piece's own unwrap settings (the `uv_method`, `uv_strips` and `uv_density` polygon layers) ([decision 0112](../../docs/decisions/0112-locked-pieces-and-piece-settings.md)). `selection::connected` gives everything joined to a selection, across seams ([decision 0118](../../docs/decisions/0118-select-connected-and-outside-warnings.md); `selection::connected_in_uvs` the elements of every UV island the selection touches ([decision 0124](../../docs/decisions/0124-select-connected-in-the-uvs.md); without UVs, `connected`)). `UvLayout` and `restore_layout` keep a mesh's UVs and seams and put them back on the polygons that are still there with the same corners, with the seams between them ([decision 0114](../../docs/decisions/0114-layout-snapshots.md)). `set_seams` marks or clears seams ([decision 0098](../../docs/decisions/0098-seams.md); adds the layer when needed, skips open edges). Every primitive gets ready-made UVs and seams (`primitives::uvs`: box, flat, round with cones unrolled into a fan, and ball styles; pieces at their real relative sizes, packed in rows).


- **Curves** (`curve`, [decision 0138](../../docs/decisions/0138-curves-sweep-lathe.md)): `Curve` (Bézier `CurvePoint`s with in and out handles and a `PointKind`: sharp, smooth, broken or auto, auto handles settled from the neighbours after every edit, open or closed; `CurvePointId`s never reused; at most `MAX_CURVE_POINTS`, ids below `MAX_POINT_ID`, within `MAX_COORDINATE`; `new`, `corners`, `from_parts` checking everything), its `Span`s (`at`, `direction` exact at handles of no length, `is_straight`), `polyline`, `bounds`, `parts` and `parts_mesh` (points and handle ends as loose points named by `CurvePart::code`), and `edited(CurveEdit)`: `Transform` (handles come with their point; one handle of a smooth point turns its partner), `Delete` (points, the neighbours' handles stretched across each gap so the shape stays near; a lone handle back to nothing; at least two points kept), `SetKind`, `SetClosed` (a loop needs three points) and, in `edit.rs`, `shaping.rs` and `rounding.rs` ([decision 0139](../../docs/decisions/0139-spline-editing.md)), `Insert` (de Casteljau: the shape exactly the same), `Extend` (points at either end), `Reverse`, `RoundCorners` (circular arcs cut back to fit, `can_round`), `Resample` (points evenly by length) and `CloseEnds` (weld or a straight stretch); `join` (two open curves end to end, ends on one spot melted, closing if the result's ends meet), `split` (pieces at points), `end_of`; `CurveError` in plain words. Tested in `editing_tests.rs`: shape kept bit-close, ids unique and never reused, every degenerate case and a random run. `ready::ReadyCurve` (line, circle, arc, spiral; `clamped`, `curve`). `path`: the places along a curve (`Detail`: segments per stretch, or with `adaptive` per quarter turn, more for a twist; corners where the way in and out differ) and rotation-minimising frames (double reflection, the leftover turn of a closed curve spread round, twist). `sweep::sweep(path, Profile, SweepShape)` (`Profile::circle`, `rectangle`, `from_curve` laid flat on its plane, turned counter-clockwise; mitred corners up to four times; caps; hard profile corners, cap rims and corners past 30°) and `lathe::lathe(curve, LatheShape)` (axis X, Y or Z, angle, segments round; poles on the axis; discs, pie slices and flat sides as caps; an arch on the ground closed across). Both lay their pieces out with the primitives' UV packing (`primitives::uvs::set_charts`) through `grid::join` and `grid::cap`, stay under `sweep::MAX_POLYGONS`, and are tested closed, outward-facing and with UVs on straight, bent, closed, spiral, twisted, mitred, very tight, doubled-point and open-profile curves, lathes on every axis and part turns, silly settings, a random run that never panics, and a timing for a long sweep (ignored).
- **`Bounds`**: an upright bounding box (`new`, `around` points, `union`, `centre`, `size`, `radius`, `contains`), used for framing the view. Skips points that aren't real numbers.

- **`Mesh`** (`polygon`): points and n-gon faces by id (`VertexId`, `FaceId`; ids count up and are never reused), and its attribute layers, hard edges (`EdgeKey`) among them. `add_face` refuses faces with fewer than three corners, unknown points or repeated points (`MeshError`, in plain words). `edges`, `bounds` and `face_normal` (Newell's method, so it is sensible for n-gons; straight up for a face with no area). Adjacency lives in `Topology`.
  - **Built for heavy meshes** ([decision 0035](../../docs/decisions/0035-heavy-meshes.md)): its parts are shared between copies until one changes them: positions in chunks of 4096 (each keeping the box around its points), faces as one flat list of corners with where each face starts (`face_layout`), hard edges. Copying is cheap, and a move copies only the chunks it touches. `same_structure` (the very same faces and hard edges) and `moved_since` (which points moved, comparing only chunks that aren't shared) let caches update only what changed. `topology` is worked out once per set of faces and kept with them; `edges` comes from it. `point_slots` and `face_slots` iterate the slots, `point_slot_count` counts them.
- **Attribute layers** (`attributes`, [decision 0043](../../docs/decisions/0043-attribute-layers-and-the-builder.md)): named values per point, edge, polygon or polygon corner (`Domain`), of one kind each (`AttributeKind`: on/off, whole number, 1–4 numbers), with a default. `Mesh::add_attribute`, `remove_attribute`, `attributes`, `attribute(name, AttributeElement)`, `set_attribute` (refusing the wrong domain or kind, missing elements and values that aren't numbers: `AttributeError`). Point, polygon and corner values are kept in typed lists, filled only as far as a value was set; edge values by edge, only where not the default. Layers are shared between copies until changed (`same_structure` checks them too). Hard edges are the built-in edge layer `HARD`, dropped when empty. `attribute_values` and `set_attribute_values` hand over and take back whole layers (`AttributeValues`), every value checked. `Value::mix` blends values (numbers blend, on/off and whole numbers take the nearer end).
- **UVs:** `attributes::UV` names the corner layer holding UV coordinates (Vec2), filled by OBJ import now and the UV tools later ([decision 0071](../../docs/decisions/0071-obj-export-and-import.md)).
- **Builder** (`builder`, decision 0043): `Builder::new(original, mesh)` makes and rewires geometry carrying every attribute layer over: `point(position, PointFrom)` (copy or between two points of the mesh being built), `face(corners with CornerFrom, like)` (polygon values from an original polygon, corner values copied or blended from original corners), `rewrite_faces`, and `copy_edge`, `move_edge`, `clear_edge` for edge values. Extrude, inset, extrude edges, bevel, loop cut and slit use it.
- **Deleting polygons**: `Mesh::delete_faces` (ids never reused; points stay for `remove_unused_vertices`; other polygons' corner values kept; edge values only on edges still used).
- **Bake check** (`bake`, [decision 0077](../../docs/decisions/0077-bake-check.md)): `BakeReport::new(mesh)` lists the elements that would spoil a bake (flipped polygons, non-manifold edges, flat polygons, short edges, n-gons bent past `BENT_SHARE` or cut into triangles sharper than `SLIVER_DEGREES`, loose points); `short_edge_groups` gives the points to weld together. Every primitive is clean (tested).
- **Material slots** (`material`, [decision 0074](../../docs/decisions/0074-material-slots.md)): `MATERIAL` names the polygon layer holding each polygon's slot number (`MAX_SLOTS` 64); `slot_of` (0 when unset), `faces_with_slot`, and `set_material` (re-exported from `edit`; missing polygons counted, a slot past the last refused). Every edit carries the layer along, so walls take their polygon's slot. Tests: slots set and found, refusals, extrude, inset and loop cut keeping them.
- **Samples** (`samples`): `heavy_ball(polygons, radius)`, a closed, bumpy ball of about that many polygons standing on the ground, for the heavy-mesh check and Help → Add a heavy test shape.
- **Parallel work** (`parallel`): `in_parallel(count, work)` splits work over the computer's cores when there is enough of it (used for shading and picking indexes).
- **Shading** (`shading`): each face corner's normal is the angle-weighted average of the faces joined to it across soft edges at its point. `smooth_groups` gives each corner its smooth group (split by hard edges; unchanged while points move), `corner_shading` the groups and every corner's normal in a few passes, `corner_normal_list`/`corner_normals` the normals alone. For updates after a move, `shading_reach` gives the points whose shading changes and `group_normals_at` works out one point's groups again. `shaded_triangles` cuts faces into triangles with those normals, as `triangulate` cuts them. `smooth_groups_with` and `corner_shading_with` take a `Hardness` ([decision 0064](../../docs/decisions/0064-automatic-hard-edges.md)): `Marked` (the hard edges) or `ByAngle(degrees)` (where polygons meet more sharply, or more than two share an edge, whatever is marked); `angle_hardness_changed` says whether moving points turned an edge around them sharp or soft. Groups and, by angle, face facings are worked out a share of the points or faces per thread. A corner with a custom normal shades with it, its group split from corners whose normals now differ.
- **Custom normals** (`custom_normals`, [decision 0148](../../docs/decisions/0148-weighted-normals.md)): the `CUSTOM_NORMAL` corner layer (a direction, or none: zero length), read by `corner_shading_with` and so by everything that shades; `set_custom_normals`, `custom_normal_list`, `forget_custom_normals`, `shading_unchanged(before, after)` (same points, polygons and hard edges: edits that keep it keep them), `split_edges` (where they part; `hardness::shading_hard_edges` gives these when there are any). The live mirror drops them; `parts::append` turns them with the part.
- **Weighted normals** (`weighted_normals`, [decision 0148](../../docs/decisions/0148-weighted-normals.md)): `weighted_normals(mesh, &WeightedNormalsOptions, hardness)` sets custom normals on every corner: each smooth group (by `hardness`, or the whole point with `keep_hard_edges` off) adds its polygons' facings weighted by area, corner angle or both (`WeightBy`), each weight's share of the group's biggest raised to `1 ÷ (1 − influence)` (up to 64), blended from the usual normal by `strength`. Never fails; polygons with no area count for nothing.
- **Editing** (`edit`): modelling operations as pure functions on a mesh, working on the element ids that still exist and reporting how many were missing (`Outcome`) instead of failing. So far `move_points`, the first operation the scene's stack records. `Mesh::set_position`, `point_slots`/`face_slots`/`hard_edges` and `Mesh::from_slots` (rebuilds a saved mesh with its ids, checking every value), `Mesh::structure_fingerprint` (a fixed hash of the polygons' corners and the point slot count, kept with the faces), `Mesh::memory_size` (an estimate, for the undo budget, counting the topology too: as built, or estimated before), `Mesh::remove_unused_vertices` (deletes the unused ones of many points in one pass), and `VertexId::from_raw`/`FaceId::from_raw` for ids recorded earlier. `transform_points` moves points by an affine matrix (turns and scaling), each once, missing ones counted, a matrix that isn't numbers moving nothing.
- **Bevel** (`bevel`, [decision 0040](../../docs/decisions/0040-bevel.md), re-exported from `edit`): `bevel_edges` with a `BevelShape` (width, segments 1–`MAX_SEGMENTS`, profile from hollow -1 through flat 0 to round 1; `clamped`) and `bevel_points`. Fans of polygons around each end; corners meet both offsets or slide along plain edges; strips of rows along a quadratic profile; shared end rows, the end polygon taking a lone strip's end, or one corner polygon; hard edges carried over; a bevelled seam moved to its strip's far border and a point bevel's neighbours' UVs blended along their edges ([decision 0104](../../docs/decisions/0104-uvs-that-survive-modelling.md)); unused points deleted in one pass. Rows are found by their ends in a lookup table and a point's cuts by a map range, so big selections take time in proportion to their size ([decision 0042](../../docs/decisions/0042-fast-edits.md)). `Mesh::rewrite_faces` (new corners, any number, ids kept) and `Mesh::remove_unused_vertex`. `set_hard` ignores a point joined to itself and missing points; `from_slots` leaves out such hard edges instead of refusing the mesh; long faces are checked for repeated corners by sorting ([decision 0041](../../docs/decisions/0041-saves-that-always-reopen.md)). Adding or changing faces clears the kept topology. **Bevel control** ([decision 0085](../../docs/decisions/0085-bevel-control.md)): `BevelShape::options` (`BevelOptions`: `skew` reaching `SKEW_REACH`, `flip_side`, `even`, `clamp`, `weld`, `weld_distance` up to `MAX_WELD_DISTANCE`; `Default` for new bevels, `EARLIER` all off): each edge's width per polygon from `even_factor` (cos 45° / cos(θ/2)) and the skew on `chain_sides` (the polygon running along each chain of edges one way round, or the other with `flip_side`); with `clamp` a first pass finds the scale that fits (halfway towards another bevelled point, `MAX_SLIDE` along plain edges) and the bevel is made at it; with `weld` the new points go through `join::weld_close`. **The improved bevel** ([decision 0141](../../docs/decisions/0141-improved-bevel.md)): `BevelOptions::improved` (on in `Default`, off in `EARLIER`): `clamp` narrows each bevelled point by its own amount (`plan` keeps a fit per point) instead of the whole bevel by the smallest; and where every side of the hole round a bevelled point is a strip's end row (`corner::round_sides`, three or more) and there are 2 or more segments, `corner::round_patch` fills it with quads: one Coons-patch region per side, bordered by half of each side row and spokes to a centre (leaving along the strip, arriving level with the centre, bent by the profile), meeting at the centre (even segments) or round a middle polygon with a strip of quads between regions (odd); the side rows are reshaped to bend towards their edge; regions copy their polygon's corner values and look.
- **Groove** (`groove`, [decision 0086](../../docs/decisions/0086-groove.md), `groove_edges`, `GrooveShape` and `GrooveSides` re-exported from `edit`): `groove_edges(mesh, edges, GrooveShape { width, depth, sides })` bevels the edges at half the width (one segment, or two for sloped sides; clamp and weld on), turns the zero-area triangles where a line ends inside a surface into a clean end wall (`close_flat_ends`), then extrudes the strip together along its facing (`Straight`) or moves its middle line by the depth (`Sloped`, a V). Depth below 0 cuts in, above 0 raises a ridge; width kept to `MAX_GROOVE_WIDTH`; border edges skipped.
- **Live mirror** (`mirror`, [decision 0088](../../docs/decisions/0088-live-mirror.md)): `with_copies(mesh, copies, on_seam, distance)` is the shape with a copy placed by each mirroring matrix (its own points and polygons keep their ids; the copies come after, turned the right way round), and the points `on_seam` picks welded within the distance (`join::weld_close`); `reflection(axes, centre)` and `combinations(axes)` give the matrices. `parts::append` adds one placed copy into a shape (`combine` uses it).
- **Placing a whole shape** (`parts::place`, [decision 0089](../../docs/decisions/0089-apply-transforms.md), re-exported from `edit`): every point moved by a matrix, ids kept; a mirroring matrix turns every polygon round too; a flattening or broken matrix changes nothing. What applying an object's transforms records.
- **Stable element names** (`names`, [decision 0090](../../docs/decisions/0090-stable-element-names.md)): each mesh keeps names for its points and polygons by slot (shared between copies; not part of equality). `name_base` names a base shape by where its points sit in its box; between `begin_naming(salt)` and `end_naming` the builder names each new point after its `PointFrom` (`names::Origin`, by the sources' names, repeats counted), `add_face` each new polygon after its corners (from the lowest name), `add_vertex` plainly. `point_name`, `trusted_point_name`, `face_name` read them; a mesh never named is named after its ids (written out before an edit names anything). `Anchors::names` keeps the anchored points' names; `Anchors::find` prefers, among points on the same spot, the one so named. `names::mix` (SplitMix64's finaliser) is fixed, as names are saved.
- **Loop cut** (`loopcut`, [decision 0047](../../docs/decisions/0047-loop-cut.md), `loop_cut` re-exported from `edit`): `loop_cut(mesh, edge, cuts, position)` walks the ring of four-sided polygons across `edge` both ways (closing on itself, or stopping at a polygon that isn't four-sided, a border, or after cutting an edge shared by three or more polygons), cuts every ring edge at the same shares measured from the same side (`cuts` 1–`MAX_CUTS` spread evenly, slid by `position` and kept 1% inside the edge), splits each crossed polygon into strips (the first keeps its id, winding kept), and gives the polygons at the ring's ends the new points as corners, so no gap opens. Values come along through the builder; a cut edge's values go to each piece. `ring_polygons` (how many polygons it would cut, for the size guard) and `preview` (the lines it would add, for the tool) walk the same ring without changing anything.
- **Slit** (`slit`, [decision 0049](../../docs/decisions/0049-slit.md), `slit`, `Eye` and `SlitLine` re-exported from `edit`): `slit(mesh, line)` cuts along a `SlitLine` (points in the mesh's own space, an `Eye`: a point, or a direction for flat views, and `through`). Each stretch sweeps a fan from the eye; per polygon, the edges it crosses within the stretch and the line points inside the polygon are put in order along the line and paired into chains (in at the border, any turns inside, out at the border). Unless `through`, a polygon must face the eye and its chain's middle must not be hidden (a ray against every polygon). New edge points are shared per edge and stretch; polygons beside a cut edge take them as corners; each chain splits the piece of its polygon holding both ends, keeping winding and the first piece's id. `slit_size` counts the pieces for the size guard; at most `MAX_LINE_POINTS` (256) points. A closed line ([decision 0058](../../docs/decisions/0058-closed-slit.md), `SlitLine::is_closed`: the first point again at the end) adds the stretch back to the start; where it starts inside a polygon, that polygon's meetings are turned round to begin after the first exit. Line points on a polygon's border are checked just before and after: touching from inside counts twice (out and back in), from outside not at all; a loop touching one spot becomes an `InnerLoop` with a `touch`, and edge spots met by several stretches share one point; with several pieces holding a chain's ends, `piece_holds` picks the one it runs through. A loop wholly inside one polygon (`InnerLoop`) is cut by `ring_cut`: the loop's shape becomes a polygon, and the ring round it two halves joined to the loop by edges from opposite outline corners (checked with `simple`, with a bounded search for other corners if a half would cross itself). The builder's `PointFrom::Inside` and `CornerFrom::Inside` blend a polygon's corners at a spot inside it (`weights_inside`, mean value weights).
- **Pipe bends** (`extrude`, [decision 0050](../../docs/decisions/0050-pipe-bends.md), `extrude_bent` and `Bend` re-exported from `edit`): `extrude_bent(mesh, faces, distance, grouping, bend)` extrudes ring after ring with `extrude_polygons` (following each point to its copies through the polygons' corners), then places the rings: with a radius, the border turned rigidly around a pivot `radius` from each piece's middle in `Bend::direction` (0 turns up, +X for upright), then the cap `distance` straight on; with no radius, the old border moved onto the halfway plane, stretched by 1 / cos(half the angle) (a mitre joint), and the cap turned and moved on. `Bend::clamped` (angle 0–`MAX_BEND_ANGLE` 3600 with a radius, or `MAX_MITRE_ANGLE` 150 without; segments 1–`MAX_BEND_SEGMENTS`; rise 0 or more), `bends`, `rings`. **Spirals** ([decision 0062](../../docs/decisions/0062-spirals.md)): past 360° (`spirals`) each row also climbs along the turn's axis by `climb_per_turn(width)` (the piece's width along the axis plus `SPIRAL_GAP`, plus `Bend::rise`) times its share of the turns; `rows` is the segments for each full turn, at most `MAX_BEND_ROWS`; `Bend::mirrored` climbs the other way, for a mirrored copy. Each piece follows its own copies of shared corners, so polygons bent apart keep their size. **Stretched objects** ([decision 0081](../../docs/decisions/0081-bends-keep-their-shape-on-stretched-objects.md)): `extrude_bent_stretched(..., stretch)` is `extrude_bent` run through `stretch::as_seen`.
- **Slide** (`slide`, [decision 0055](../../docs/decisions/0055-basics.md), re-exported from `edit`): `slides_for` picks, for a move, each point's neighbour (still ones first) most nearly the way of the move and how far along (0 to 1); `slide_points` applies `Slide`s from where the points were.
- **Parts** (`parts`, decision 0055): `keep_only` (a copy with some polygons and their points, ids kept) and `combine` (shapes into one, each by a matrix; hard edges and value layers come along, corner values follow their points; a mirroring matrix turns polygons round).
- **Edits as seen** (`stretch`, [decision 0082](../../docs/decisions/0082-edits-keep-their-shape-on-stretched-objects.md)): `as_seen(mesh, stretch, edit)` runs any edit on the points stretched by an object's stretch (its scale on each axis over their average) and takes them back, so the edit keeps its shape as seen on a stretched object; points it doesn't move stay exactly as they were. `usable` turns a stretch that isn't real, or beyond `MAX_STRETCH` (1000) to 1, into none; `facing_as_seen` stretches a normal the opposite way. Tested with exact unmoved points and broken stretches.
- **Symmetry** (`symmetry`, [decision 0054](../../docs/decisions/0054-symmetry.md)): `mirror_plan` splits the points about to move into `MirrorGroup`s (the chosen points, and each mirror across the enabled own axes, found by position within `TOLERANCE` through a cell index), each with the axes it is mirrored across and the planes it stays on; `MirrorGroup::matrix` and `offset` turn a change into each group's. A `Mirror` ([decision 0059](../../docs/decisions/0059-symmetry-for-every-edit.md)) is the axes and the middle the planes go through (`point`, `vector`, `reflections`), with a clip switch: `weld_middle` (with a distance, for Weld the middle now) puts points near the planes on them and welds those meeting there, `keep_sides` puts points pushed across back on the plane, and `move_limits` says how far points may move without crossing; `mirror_plan` takes one, so the planes can go through any point. `Partners` finds a point's, edge's or polygon's mirror across one reflection, by position. `Anchors::mirrored` and `joined` give a mirrored edit its anchors, and `extrude::bend_towards`/`bend_direction` turn a bend's direction the mirrored way.
- **Triangulation** (`triangulate`, [decision 0057](../../docs/decisions/0057-after-phase-4-testing.md)): `triangulate(n, at, out)` and `face_triangles(mesh, corners, out)` cut a polygon into `n - 2` triangles, the same way every time: triangles as they are, quads 0–2 unless dented, flat polygons without dents as a fan, anything else laid flat on its best plane and cut along lines inside it (the shortest cut up to `BEST_CUT_UP_TO` corners, so a bent polygon is cut along its fold; ear by ear above that; the fan if nothing works). Drawing (`render`), picking (`tools`) and the selection tint use it.
- **Hard and soft edges** (`hardness`, [decision 0053](../../docs/decisions/0053-hard-and-soft-edges.md), re-exported from `edit`): `set_hard_edges`, `sharp_edges` (polygons meeting more sharply than an angle, and edges of three or more polygons; never borders or polygons with no area) and `hard_edges_by_angle` (those hard, every other inner edge soft). `harden_new_edges` marks the sharp edges of polygons an edit just made hard (Bridge and Fill hole use it, from `DEFAULT_ANGLE`, 30°). Dissolve forgets the values of edges it took away (`Mesh::forget_unused_edge_values`) and keeps two hard edges joined into one hard. Only shading changes.
- **Clean-up** (`cleanup`, [decision 0052](../../docs/decisions/0052-clean-up.md), re-exported from `edit`): `delete_elements` (points, edges or polygons with every polygon using them, then unused points), `dissolve_edges`, `dissolve_points` and `dissolve_faces` (polygons around merge into one keeping the lowest id; border elements and merges that wouldn't make one simple polygon stay; points left on a straight line go), `fill_holes` (a polygon per border loop touching the given points, every loop when none), `flip_faces`, `faces_facing_wrong` and `fix_facing` (agree with neighbours across shared edges; closed pieces face outward by signed volume), and `faces_facing` (within an angle of a direction).
- **Connect, Bridge and Weld** (`join`, [decision 0051](../../docs/decisions/0051-connect-bridge-weld.md), re-exported from `edit`): `connect_points` (selected points joined across each polygon, all the way round when three or more), `connect_edges` (each edge cut `cuts` times, the cuts joined across polygons holding two or more; neighbours take the new points), `bridge_edges` (border edges joined into chains; the first two get a band of `segments` rows, lined up by distance and `twist` for loops, a zip of triangles for different lengths, always running against the existing polygons), `bridge_faces` (two polygons removed and their borders banded), `weld` (points melted at their middle or a target; repeated corners dropped, collapsed polygons and orphaned points removed, edge values moved to the kept point), `weld_close` (points within a distance welded in chains, found through a grid of cells; for bevels, [decision 0085](../../docs/decisions/0085-bevel-control.md)); both through `weld_groups`, several groups in one pass.
