Makes a material see-through outside a shape, for alpha cards: leaves, grass, fences, chains. Plug the Shape box's Mask into Opacity: white stays solid, black is cut away. Translucency is the light passing through a thin part from behind, as through a leaf held up to the sun; thicker parts (the Thickness, or the material's own height) let less through. The colour under the cut-away parts is filled from the nearest solid colour, so no dark fringe shows round the edges in a game.

Turns
Turns a black-and-white shape into a height with rounded edges, as if each tile, brick or stone were cut with a rounded, sloped or stepped edge. Width is how far in from the outline the edge reaches, in centimetres on the finished material; Height is how high the top is; Roundness softens the edge's corners. It needs a shape, because only a shape has an inside and an outside.
Turns a bridge between two closed loops round by whole steps, if the points pair up wrongly and the band twists.
Turns a colour picture into a black-and-white shape, so boxes that need a shape (Bevel, Edge finder, a Where plug) can use it. A colour on its own has no inside or outside: this box decides one. By Lightness, light colours become white; by Close to a colour, colours like the chosen one become white. Height gives the same greys as a height.
Turns a height into a whole material: it works out the normal (how light catches the bumps), the occlusion (darker in dips) and the curvature (lighter on ridges), and adds a colour, roughness and metal. With nothing in Colour, the Colour setting is used; with nothing in Roughness, the Roughness setting.
Turns a texture into a few big square pixels, for pixel-art games. Pixels is how many squares across (and down) the whole texture has. A whole material is cut on the same squares, so its colour, height and roughness line up. Limit to a palette keeps every colour to one of the project's palettes, Tidy stray pixels removes lone specks, and Outline draws a dark line one pixel wide round shapes. Put it last, just before Material out: Export textures… then writes the textures at exactly that many pixels, set to stay crisp in the game.
Turns every colour into the closest of a palette's swatches.
Turns every pixel into the closest colour of one of the project's palettes, as pixel-art games keep to a few colours.
