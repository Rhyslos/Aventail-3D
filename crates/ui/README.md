# ui

**Layer 3 · Front end**

The interface: the dockable layout, menus, palettes, Attributes and Objects panels, the UV Editor panel, and the theme.

## Purpose

Draws everything around and on top of the viewports with egui and egui_dock: menu bar, command palette, mode palette, the Attributes panel (top right) and Objects panel (below it), the Coordinates panel, status bar and the calm dark theme. Hands viewport input to `tools` and carries out the commands they return. See DESIGN.md §5.

## Must never

- Change the scene except by executing commands
- Hold modelling logic: that belongs in `tools`, `mesh` or `scene`
- Use proprietary icons or artwork copied from other software

## Depends on

`tools` (the camera), `scene` (the document), `mesh` (primitive setting limits; `config` planned). Outside libraries: `glam` (maths), `egui` and `egui_dock` (interface and docking), `serde` (saving the layout).

## Status

The window's layout is in place:

- **`Workbench`**: draws the whole window each frame and returns actions (`Quit`) for the app. Takes the app's name in `AppInfo`, so this crate never names the app.
- **Panels** (`Panel`): Viewport, Attributes, Objects, Coordinates, docked with egui_dock. Not closeable, not floating. The standard layout (`layout::default_layout`) puts Attributes top right, Objects below, Coordinates under the viewport; a damaged saved layout is replaced with a status-bar note.
- **Menu bar** in the design's order (File, Edit, Create, Modes, Select, Tools, Mesh, UV, View, Window, Help), with the project's name (and an Unsaved badge in the accent colour) and the layout tabs (Standard; Modelling and UV Edit greyed out) on the right. Working: File (New, Open, Open recent, Save, Save As, and Quit, which has no key on purpose), Modes, View → Reset view, Grid lines (three levels) and Reset panel layout, Edit → Settings…, Help → Add a heavy test shape (a 3-million-polygon ball as one undo step, `Workbench::add_heavy_test_shape`) and Help → About. Everything else is listed greyed out with the phase that brings it.
- **Panel menu lines** (`panels::menu_line`): Attributes (Mode, Edit, Units) and Objects (Edit, View, Object).
- **Palettes** (`palette`): the command palette (top: undo, redo, the select tool button showing the active `SelectTool`, shapes and group work; the rest greyed out) and the mode palette (left; Model, Object, Points, Edges, Polygons and UV can be picked, and the status bar says which is active).
- **Theme** (`theme`): the darker studio look (decision 0014), applied at startup by the app: colours, the viewport backdrop, the Inter font (Regular and SemiBold), text sizes, spacing, frames and the docked tabs' style. **`Accent`**: red (standard), blue, marine green, plum, olive or petrol blue, each with fill, light and soft shades; `set_accent` switches it app-wide and `accent` reads it back from egui's memory. Tests check every text colour is readable (WCAG 4.5:1), every accent's marks are visible, and neighbouring areas stay gently close.
- **Layout** (`layout`): besides the standard arrangement, `hide_viewport_tab` hides the viewport's tab each frame (its menu line replaces it) unless another panel shares its group.
- **Icons** (`icons`): 32 original icons (shapes, groups, eyes and tools) drawn with egui shapes on a 24 × 24 grid.
- **Viewport** (`viewport`): its own menu line (`viewport::chrome`: View, Cameras, Display, Options, Panel, and buttons to drag for pan, zoom and orbit), the image from a `ViewportRenderer` sized in physical pixels, overlays (view and camera names; the axis marker in the bottom-left corner with the grid spacing above it) and the navigation controls picker (`viewport::picker`) in the bottom-right corner. The look row (`viewport::look`, [decision 0063](../../docs/decisions/0063-wireframe.md)) sits beside the axis marker: a button naming `ViewOptions::shading` (`Shading`: Shaded, Shaded with wires, Wireframe) that opens the choices, also at the top of the Display menu; `ViewportView::shading` tells the renderer. The Display menu's Cavity switch (`chrome::cavity_entry`: `ViewOptions::hide_cavity`, on as standard, in the group with Hard edges and Normals; `ViewportView::cavity`, decisions [0065](../../docs/decisions/0065-cavity.md) and [0066](../../docs/decisions/0066-clickable-axes.md)). The marker's letters are buttons in perspective views (`chrome::axis_clicks`, `axis_letters`, `look_along_label`): a click starts `View::start_look_along`, a glide to `Navigator::looking_along` (the other side when already looking that way). Without a GPU (`NoRenderer`), it shows the backdrop and says why. Turns drags and wheel turns into `tools::Navigator` moves using the chosen `NavigationSettings` (the drag kind is fixed when the drag starts) and sets the mouse cursor while navigating. `ViewOptions` (the `GridDetail` level: 1 m, 50 cm or 10 cm; navigation settings; four views) is saved by the app between sessions; an old `fine_grid` setting is read and turned into the 10 cm level by `ViewOptions::migrated`. While the mouse hovers over the image, the panel asks the renderer what is under it (`ViewportRenderer::pick`, never waiting; a resting mouse asks nothing new); orbiting then turns around that surface point, and zooming moves towards it (or the ground, or along the line of sight). A ring marks the pivot while orbiting or drag-zooming. Frame all (`H`) and frame selected (`Z`, [decision 0047](../../docs/decisions/0047-loop-cut.md); it was `F`), from the keys or the View menus, glide the view (`GLIDE_SECONDS`) until the boxes from `SceneSummary` fit (the selection, else everything shown, else `DEFAULT_FRAME`, a 1 m box at the origin); taking hold of the view stops a glide.
- **Four views** (`viewport`): the panel holds four views (perspective, top, front, right; `VIEW_COUNT`), each with its own camera, drag, glide and picking answer, shown one at a time or in a 2 × 2 layout (`ViewOptions::four_views`, saved). `F1`–`F4` show one (`VIEW_KEYS`), `F5` all four (`FOUR_VIEWS_KEY`), and a middle click switches. The last view clicked is active (an accent outline); the menu line, its buttons, Reset view and framing act on it, and `camera()`/`navigator()` return it. Cameras changes the active view's kind; Panel → Four views and the square header button switch the layout. `ViewportView` carries the view's `slot`, `flat_height` and `grid_plane`; only the view under the mouse asks what is there. In four-view mode each image is labelled with its view, e.g. "3D viewport (Top)".
- **Objects panel** (`objects`): the scene tree with fold arrows, kind icons, selected names in the accent colour (`AccentColors::text`, readable on the row's tint) and an eye per row. Click, Ctrl+click and Shift+click select; double-click renames in place (Enter or clicking away keeps the name, Esc cancels); dragging onto a row nests, onto its edge reorders, below the rows moves to the top level; right-click opens the object commands. It hands back `DocRequest`s (a command, undo or redo) for the workbench to carry out; only the selection is changed directly. Its menu line (Edit, View, Object) and the main Edit and Create menus share the same entries.
- **Document** (`Workbench`): owns the `scene::Document`, carries out requests from the panel, menus, palette and keys (Ctrl+Z, Ctrl+Y, Ctrl+Shift+Z, Delete, Backspace, Esc, Alt+G, Shift+G; not while typing or renaming; Esc closes an open menu first), and reports undo, redo and refusals in the status bar, which also counts the selection. **Project file** (`project`): the title (`Workbench::title`: name, with `●` and "(unsaved)" for unsaved changes), File → New, Open…, Open recent (eight, `RECENT_COUNT`), Save, Save As… with `NEW_SHORTCUT`, `OPEN_SHORTCUT`, `SAVE_SHORTCUT`, `SAVE_AS_SHORTCUT`; a modal "Save changes?" question (Save / Don't save / Cancel; Enter, `DISCARD_SHORTCUT` Ctrl+D, Esc) before New, Open, Quit or `request_close`; scene keys are ignored while it shows. Files are never touched here: `WorkbenchAction::Save`/`Open` go to the app, which reports back with `saved`, `save_failed`, `save_cancelled`, `opened` (fresh drawing cache, undo limit kept, frames the scene), `open_failed` (forgets a missing recent project). Replacing the document makes the drawing cache forget its summary but keep counting versions up (`DrawingCache::forget`), so the renderer never mistakes the new scene for one it drew. **Backup copies** (`autosave`, [decision 0036](../../docs/decisions/0036-autosave.md)): `AutosaveSettings` (on, `MINUTES` 1–30, `COPIES` 1–20; standard on, 5, 5; saved by the app) and an `Autosaver` timer that asks for a copy (`WorkbenchAction::Autosave` with a snapshot of the document) an interval after the last copy when anything changed since it, on its own clock separate from saving, one at a time; the app reports back with `autosaved` or `autosave_failed`. After a crash the app hands over `RecoveryOffer`s (`offer_recovery`), and a modal "Recover unsaved work?" (`RECOVERY_TITLE`) asks about each: the copies newest first with `ago` times, Restore (Enter) → `WorkbenchAction::Restore`, Open as saved (only with a project) → `Open` and `DiscardBackups`, Discard → `DiscardBackups`. `recovered` takes the work back as unsaved under the project's name; `recovery_failed` says why it couldn't. **Settings** (`settings`, [decision 0023](../../docs/decisions/0023-one-settings-window.md)): a window opened from Edit → Settings… or `SETTINGS_SHORTCUT` (Ctrl + ,) with Interface (accent, units, and a Radial menu section), Viewport (navigation settings, grid level), Undo (undo memory, with the memory in use) and Autosave (on or off, minutes, copies kept, and the folder) sections and Restore standard; it borrows the workbench's own values each frame, so quick switches elsewhere stay in step. **Undo memory** (`undo_memory`): `UndoMemory` (256 MB to 8 GB or Unlimited, standard 2 GB) sets the document's history limit; the app saves it. The status bar says once when old steps start being forgotten. **Make editable** (`MAKE_EDITABLE_SHORTCUT`, Ctrl + E, also in the Mesh menu and the Objects panel's Object and right-click menus) runs `Command::MakeEditable`; with nothing to bake it explains in the status bar. The command palette's undo, redo, shape and group buttons work, and the Create menu lists every primitive and Group. **Command list** (`commands`, [decision 0037](../../docs/decisions/0037-tool-menu-and-command-list.md)): `CommandId` and `CommandId::info` (name, `Icon`, key, help, `arrives` for one not built yet) for every command the tool menu offers; `modes` and the fixed rings `OBJECT_RING` and `MODELLING_RING`; `menu_for(mode)` gives a mode's `Slot`s (gaps kept). The Tools and Mesh menus, the palette's transform buttons (`palette::listed`), the Objects panel's entries and the object keys read from it; `workbench::command` has `readiness` (`Ready::Yes`, `NotNow(why)`, `Later(when)`) and `run_command`, the one way a command is carried out. **Tool menu** (`tool_menu`): `TOOL_MENU_KEY` (`E`) opens it at the mouse (not while typing; `Ctrl + E` left to Make editable); `E`/`Shift + E` and the arrow keys step, `Enter`/`Space` use, `Esc` closes; letting go of a held `E` after pointing at a tool uses it. `ToolMenuSettings`: the `ToolMenuStyle` (ContinuousRing, the standard; InnerEdge; Outlined; SolidTiles; or List, like the shape list), `show_keys` (keys just outside the ring, standard on; off, in the middle) and `tap_to_open` (standard on; off, the menu shows only while `E` is held and letting go without pointing closes it); chosen in Settings → Interface → Radial menu and saved by the app. The ring (`Ring`) has `RING_PLACES` (14) fixed places at `RING_RADIUS` in every mode, icon-only buttons (the whole slice towards a tool its target), and a middle with the cancel sign (`Icon::Cancel`, a click there closes) and a caption naming the highlighted tool, with an accessible label. Tool icons were picked in the icon mockup and converted point for point (`icons`: `Ink` and `Paint`, one line width). It and the shape list never show at once. **Shape list** (`shape_picker`): `B` (`SHAPE_LIST_KEY`) opens a list of primitives by the mouse; `B` again moves down, `Shift + B` or the arrow keys move, `Enter` or `Space` creates, `Esc` or a click elsewhere closes; clicking an entry creates it. Not while typing. The Attributes panel names the selection. The Attributes panel's Edits section also says when an edit was skipped because the shape would be too large, and the recovery question says when a copy was left out because restoring it closed the app ([decision 0045](../../docs/decisions/0045-limits-for-files.md)).
- **Attributes panel** (`attributes`): for one selected object, a heading (icon, name, kind), Basic (Name, Visible) and, for primitives, Shape: the kind's settings (`attributes::fields`) as drag-or-type fields with step arrows (`attributes::stepper`: one step per click, ten with Shift; lengths in the chosen `Units`, metres or centimetres, switched in the Units menu or Settings and saved by the app), counts and switches, with their limits. Changes go out as `SetPrimitive` commands through `DocRequest::RunMerging`, keyed by a gesture count so one drag, typed value or click is one undo step. Its menu line: Mode (object settings; tool settings later) and Edit (Copy settings, Paste settings to the selected shapes of the same kind; the same entries, `attributes::settings_entries`, are in the Objects panel's right-click menu, sharing one clipboard) and Units. Every shape has a Mesh section: the `MeshReport` of its evaluated mesh (points, edges, polygons, triangles, pieces when more than one, closed or open, and problems in `theme::WARNING`), checked again only when the mesh changes (cached by `Arc` pointer); for heavy shapes (50 000 polygons or more) the check runs on another thread and the section says "Checking the mesh…" meanwhile (`MeshChecker`); a plain mesh (`ObjectKind::Mesh`) adds a note and has no settings; an object with recorded edits shows an Edits section counting them and flagging, in `theme::WARNING`, any that no longer fully fit. Several selected objects are counted.
- **Scene drawing** (`drawing`): `DrawingCache` turns the document into a `SceneSummary` (the `SceneDrawing` of shown objects with a shape, each a mesh, world transform and selected flag, plus the boxes `H` and `Z` frame), rebuilding only when the scene revision or selection changes. Each item's mesh is the object's evaluated shape (`Scene::evaluated`: base plus recorded edits), which the scene caches, so an unchanged object keeps the same mesh. `ViewportView::scene` hands the drawing to the renderer; its `version` says when it changed.
- **Element modes** (`components`, [decision 0027](../../docs/decisions/0027-component-modes.md)): `MODE_KEYS` (`1`, `2`, `3`; the same key again goes back to Model, not while typing; shown in the Modes menu and palette tooltips via `Mode::key`). In Points, Edges and Polygons modes `SceneDrawing::editing` is set (the renderer then draws the edited objects' edges plainly instead of outlined) and `SceneDrawing::points` in Points mode (the renderer shows every point). `ComponentTracker` finds the element of a selected object under the mouse from the hovered view's `ViewPointer`, in a `tools::PickScene` made from one kept `tools::MeshPick` per object ([decision 0035](../../docs/decisions/0035-heavy-meshes.md)): kept while the mesh is the same, updated around moved points, and for new shapes of 50 000 polygons or more built on another thread (`prepare`, with the mesh's topology). Hovering never waits for that thread (nothing is highlighted until it finishes); clicks do ([decision 0042](../../docs/decisions/0042-fast-edits.md)). While a gizmo or Coordinates drag is under way (`freeze`) the scene stays as it was and nothing is looked up under the mouse. It builds the `ComponentOverlay` (the selected elements, and the hovered one as `OverlayStyle::Hovered`, a hovered polygon tinted and outlined) only when the mode, drawing, selection or hovered element changes; it walks only what is selected, never the whole mesh. The status bar names the hovered element ("point 4 of Cube") or asks for a selection.
- **Selecting** (`selecting`, `viewport::select`, `workbench::selection`, [decision 0029](../../docs/decisions/0029-selection.md)): the viewport turns left-button input into `Gesture`s (click, double-click, a finished box or lasso, brush dabs sharing a stroke number, `Ctrl` + wheel steps) unless navigation uses the button, and draws the box, lasso or brush circle; the active `SelectTool` (`Q`, `SELECT_TOOL_KEY`, steps Box → Lasso → Brush) and brush radius (`BRUSH_RADIUS`, `Ctrl` + wheel with the brush) live in `ViewportPanel`. The workbench carries gestures and the Select menu's and keys' requests (`SELECT_ALL_SHORTCUT`, `INVERT_SHORTCUT`, Esc) out with a `Selector` once the frame's drawing is known: whole objects in the Model mode (not undoable), elements of the selected objects in the element modes (`Document::select_elements`, undoable). `ViewOptions::select_visible_only` (left palette button `VISIBLE_ONLY_LABEL`, Select menu, Settings) limits regions to what can be seen. The overlay draws selected elements as `OverlayStyle::Selected`; the status bar counts them. Which keys add and remove follows one `SelectKeys` pattern (standard Ctrl adds, Shift removes; also Shift adds and Ctrl removes, Ctrl flips, Ctrl adds and Alt removes), saved in `ViewOptions::select_keys` and chosen in Settings; Alt + double-click selects a ring in every pattern. A flipping brush stroke flips each thing once (`StrokeMemory`). Double-clicks allow `DOUBLE_CLICK_SECONDS` (0.5 s, set on egui's input options) and must land within 6 points of the first click.
- **Transforming** (`transforming`, `viewport::gizmo`, `workbench::transform`, `coordinates`, [decision 0030](../../docs/decisions/0030-transform-gizmos.md)): the `Tool` (`TOOL_KEYS` W, R, S, T; Q back to selecting), `LOCK_KEYS` X, Y, Z and `AXES_KEY` L (also on the palette and in the Tools menu). Each frame `Target::of` works out what the gizmo would change (whole objects in the Model mode, else the points of the selected elements), and the viewport draws and drags its `Gizmo`, handing over `GizmoEvent`s (a press on a handle never selects; Shift is fine; a right click cancels, Esc too). A drag is a `Session`: every frame `Document::abandon`s the previous frame and executes the whole change again with one merge key (`Target::commands`: `SetTransforms`, or `MovePoints`/`TransformPoints`); an unchanged frame does nothing. Point edits record `mesh::Anchors` from the selected elements, so they find their points again after a settings change; the Attributes panel's Edits section notes that, and warns when some weren't found ([decision 0032](../../docs/decisions/0032-edits-find-their-points-again.md)). `SnapSettings` (in `ViewOptions`, with `locked` and `Axes`) snap moves to points, edges, polygons or the grid shown, turns and scaling to steps; Ctrl flips it per drag unless set to button only. The Coordinates panel (`coordinates`) shows and edits world position, size and rotation through the same sessions.
- **Bevel** ([decision 0040](../../docs/decisions/0040-bevel.md)): `Tool::Bevel` on `V` in the Edges and Points modes, through the same modelling session; the drag counts how much further the mouse is from the nearest selected edge (or point) on screen than where it began. `AttributesPanel::bevel` (`BevelLook`: segments, profile) shows as the tool's settings and feeds the next drag; the Last edit shows Width, Segments and Profile (`EditSettings`), each in its own id scope.
- **Loop cut** ([decision 0047](../../docs/decisions/0047-loop-cut.md)): `Tool::LoopCut` on `F` in the Points, Edges and Polygons modes (`Tool::clicks_on_shapes`: the viewport's `set_click_tool` sends left clicks to it as a press, no movement and a release, and never to selecting; the component tracker is `set_quiet` and shows the tool's `set_tool_lines`). `workbench::loop_cut` finds the edge under the mouse (`ComponentTracker::find`), shows `mesh::loopcut::preview` while pointing, and on a press records `Operation::LoopCut` under one merge key, recording it again as the drag slides it along the edge on screen (Shift for fine steps); Esc or a right click abandons it. One cut follows the mouse along the edge; several are centred and slide together. `AttributesPanel::loop_cut` (`LoopLook`: cuts) shows as the tool's setting, and Ctrl + wheel changes it; the Last edit shows Cuts and Position. The axis locks are `LOCK_KEYS`, Shift with X, Y or Z.
- **Ctrl + wheel for a tool's count** ([decision 0048](../../docs/decisions/0048-ctrl-wheel-changes-a-tools-count.md)): while a tool listed by `Tool::has_wheel_count` (Bevel, Loop cut) is in use in an editing mode, the viewport (`set_wheel_tool`) hands Ctrl + wheel notches to the workbench (`take_tool_steps`) instead of growing the selection; `Workbench::wheel_count` changes the count as the Attributes panel's arrows would: the tool's setting, the edit being dragged, or with no drag the last edit when the tool made it (`ReplaceOperation`, one step per notch).
- **Slit** ([decision 0049](../../docs/decisions/0049-slit.md)): `Tool::Slit` on `X` in the editing modes, a click tool. `workbench::slit` keeps a `SlitSession` (the objects cut, the line's points in the world, the camera it is seen through, the point being dragged): a press takes a point within reach to drag, Ctrl removes it, a press on the line adds a point there, anywhere else adds one at the end; points snap to a point, an edge or a polygon (`ComponentTracker::find`) or float at the earlier point's depth; Shift keeps the stretch at 15° on screen. Every change abandons and records the `Operation::Slit`s again under one merge key. Esc or Enter (`document_keys`), another tool (`set_tool`), leaving the editing modes, or any other change to the undo steps finishes it. A press on a point that moves less than `CLICK_SLACK` is a click (`pressed_at`) and leaves the point be; a click on the first point with three or more points closes the line (`closed`, saved as the first point again) and finishes it ([decision 0058](../../docs/decisions/0058-closed-slit.md)). The viewport draws the line and where the next point would go on top of each view (`set_tool_path`, `ToolPath`). `AttributesPanel::slit_through` is the Cut through switch, also shown as the Last edit.
- **Pipe bends** ([decision 0050](../../docs/decisions/0050-pipe-bends.md)): `AttributesPanel::bend` (a `scene::Bend`) shows as Angle, Direction, Bend radius, Segments and Rise per turn (`bend_rows`; Angle up to 3600° with a radius, Rise per turn greyed out without one, [decision 0062](../../docs/decisions/0062-spirals.md)) under Extrude's settings and on an extrude's Last edit (a straight one shows the tool's bend at no angle); the modelling session records `ExtrudeBent` when the bend turns. Extrude is in `Tool::has_wheel_count`: Ctrl + wheel changes the bend's segments.
- **Keyboard shortcut viewer** ([decisions 0056](../../docs/decisions/0056-shortcut-viewer.md) and [0061](../../docs/decisions/0061-drawn-keyboard.md)): `workbench::shortcuts`. `bindings()` builds every key's jobs (`Binding`: a `KeyId`, its modifiers, the action and its `Situation`) from the key constants and the command list; `mouse()` lists the mouse actions. `keys_button` (menu bar, after Help) toggles `ShortcutViewer` (filter, pinned key, hidden situations); `shortcut_window` draws the keyboard from `keyboard::caps(layout)`, the focused key's jobs beside it and the mouse actions under it. `keyboard` holds the `KeyboardLayout`s (`guess` from a system language) and where each key sits, what is printed on it and which `KeyId` the app reads from it. The picked layout is `ViewOptions::keyboard`; until then `Workbench::set_system_language` (called by the app with `sys-locale`) decides.
- **Hide and isolate, copies and instances** ([decision 0055](../../docs/decisions/0055-basics.md)): `workbench::showing` (Shift/Ctrl/Alt + H: objects through `SetVisible` in the Model mode, polygons through `drawing::Hidden` in the element modes; `DrawItem::shown` is the mesh without hidden polygons, used for drawing, picking and selecting, while `DrawItem::mesh` stays whole for the tools) and `workbench::copying` (Ctrl/Alt + C and V, also the window's `Event::Copy`/`Event::Paste` with `CLIPBOARD_NOTE`; `Command::Duplicate`). `CommandId::Duplicate` and `Instance` sit in the object ring. The mode palette's isolate button returns `ModePick::Isolate`. Slide: the viewport doesn't start navigating for an Alt + left press on the Move gizmo (`navigate`'s `slide_gizmo`), `GizmoEvent::Begin { slide }` tells the workbench, and `Target::sliding` makes `commands` record `SlidePoints`. `workbench::parts::run_split_off` (Mesh menu) and `CommandId::Join` (object ring, Mesh menu).
- **Symmetry** ([decisions 0054](../../docs/decisions/0054-symmetry.md) and [0059](../../docs/decisions/0059-symmetry-for-every-edit.md)): `ViewOptions::symmetry` (`Symmetry`: on, axes, `SymmetryCentre` and offset) is switched by `PaletteCommand::ToggleSymmetry` and Tools → Symmetry (`toggle_symmetry`); the palette's arrow beside it (`SYMMETRY_MENU_LABEL`) opens `symmetry_settings`, changing a copy the workbench keeps if it changed. `Workbench::mirror_for` works out an object's `Mirror` (its middle in its own space: the pivot, the box's middle or the world centre, moved by the offset; the clip switch from `Symmetry::clip`; `mirror_planes` gives the same whether symmetry is on or not); `weld_middle_now` (the menu's button, `PaletteCommand::WeldMiddle`) records `Operation::WeldMiddle` on each selected shape where something joins, with `Symmetry::weld_distance`; with clip on `PointsStart::limits` clamps a move; `edit_mirror` gives it in the editing modes. When a gizmo or Coordinates change begins, `with_symmetry` turns its `Target` into `Target::mirrored`: each `PointsStart` gets its mirror groups, and `commands` records one move or transform per group. Every other modelling edit goes through `with_mirror` (in `record_each`, the modelling drags, loop cuts and slits), becoming `Operation::Mirrored`; the Attributes panel looks through it with `Operation::inner`.
- **Hard and soft edges** ([decision 0053](../../docs/decisions/0053-hard-and-soft-edges.md)): `workbench::hardness` (`run_hardness`, `run_hard_by_angle`, the angle kept in `AttributesPanel::hard_angle`, shown as the Last edit by `angle_row`); `SelectRequest::HardEdges`. The Display menu's switches are `ViewOptions::hide_hard_edges` and `show_normals`; the main Mesh menu has `hard_edges_by_hand` (`chrome::auto_hard_edges_entry`: Automatic hard edges, on as standard, [decision 0064](../../docs/decisions/0064-automatic-hard-edges.md): `auto_hard_angle` gives `AUTO_HARD_ANGLE`, 30°, passed on as `ViewportView::auto_hard_angle`; the guides then show `sharp_edges` by that angle, and the hard and soft edge commands' notes say the angle decides); `components::Guides` tells the tracker, which keeps the guide lines (`OverlayStyle::Hard` and `Normal`, objects up to `GUIDES_UP_TO` polygons) apart from the hover overlay and puts them first.
- **Clean-up** ([decision 0052](../../docs/decisions/0052-clean-up.md)): `workbench::cleaning`. In the element modes `Delete` (`run_delete_elements`) and `Backspace` (`run_dissolve`) record per selected object at once (`document_keys` sends them there instead of deleting objects); a dissolve or fill is tried on a copy first so nothing empty is recorded. Fill hole and Flip normals are commands in the ring; `run_fix_facing` is in the Mesh menu; `SelectRequest::Flipped` and `SimilarFacing` (`SIMILAR_FACING_ANGLE`, 30°) are `Selector::select_flipped` and `select_similar_facing`. The command list allows one key in two commands when their modes don't overlap.
- **Connect, Bridge and Weld** ([decision 0051](../../docs/decisions/0051-connect-bridge-weld.md)): `workbench::joining`. `A` (`run_connect`; `J` until [decision 0057](../../docs/decisions/0057-after-phase-4-testing.md)) and `M` (`run_weld`) record per selected object at once; `C` (`run_bridge`) does too when two borders or two polygons are selected, else picks `Tool::Bridge`. `Ctrl + M` (`WELD_TARGET_KEYS`) picks `Tool::Weld`. Both tools drag between elements (`Tool::drags_between_elements`): `place_drag_tool` gives them the viewport's drag only while something is highlighted under the mouse, so clicks and drags from empty space select. A `PairSession` keeps what the press was on, the mouse, and what the line snaps onto (`onto`, ringed in the `ToolPath`); the release joins the two. `AttributesPanel::join` (`JoinLook`) holds Connect's cuts and Bridge's segments and twist (`bridge_rows`).
- **Extrude and inset** (`workbench::modelling`, `viewport::tool_drag`, [decision 0038](../../docs/decisions/0038-extrude-and-inset.md)): `Tool::Extrude` and `Tool::Inset` (`Tool::drags_anywhere`), picked with `D` and `I` (from the command list; Ctrl + I stays Invert), the tool menu or the Mesh menu (whose built entries now run), only in their modes (`readiness` says why not). With one in use, a left drag anywhere in a view becomes `ToolDragEvent`s (the drag as a screen vector, Shift a tenth; a right click or Esc cancels; a click still selects); the workbench turns them into a `ModellingSession` on the selected polygons (or, for Extrude in Edges mode, edges) of every selected object, measuring points in centimetres at the selection's middle (`Camera::world_per_pixel`), for Extrude along the way the elements face on screen so they follow the mouse (right when they face the viewer), for Inset towards the polygons' middle on screen from where the drag began ([decision 0039](../../docs/decisions/0039-drags-follow-the-pull.md)); a drag that starts on an unselected element of the right kind selects it first, as its own undo step, and recording `ExtrudePolygons`/`InsetPolygons`/`ExtrudeEdges` with anchors, one undo step per drag by `abandon` (no step at no distance; Inset never outward). The distance shows by the mouse and in the status bar. After extruding edges the selection moves to the new outer edges, found by looking back from the newest polygons rather than working out the shape's topology. The Attributes panel (`AttributesPanel::tool`, `grouping`) shows the top edit, when it is an extrude or inset, as **Last edit** (Distance, Keep together; `ReplaceOperation` merged per gesture), else the tool's own Keep together switch.
- **Placeholders**: the other panels say what will appear in them and when.
