# uv

**Layer 1 · Core**

UV mapping: seams, unwrapping, relaxing, straightening and packing.

## Purpose

Turns a mesh's surface into flat UV islands and arranges them in the texture square: seams from hard edges, conformal unwrapping (LSCM), relax, straighten, packing with padding, texel density. See DESIGN.md §7.

## Must never

- Know about scenes, undo, files, the GPU or UI
- Change the mesh's shape: it only works out UV coordinates and seams
- Hang or allocate without bound on degenerate input (zero-area faces, islands with no area)

## Depends on

`mesh`, `words` (the language layer: stretch explanations go through it, [decision 0134](../../docs/decisions/0134-shared-language-crate.md)), `glam`.

## Status

- **Seams** (`seams`, [decision 0098](../../docs/decisions/0098-seams.md)): `pieces` (the polygons left joined once seams are cut), `is_disk` (whether a piece lies flat in one go: its Euler characteristic once cut open is 1), and `suggest` (seams for a whole shape: the hard edges given and corners sharper than `CORNER_ANGLE`, then each piece cut to a disk: a piece with no border opened along the least seen path from its lowest point to its highest, then a spanning tree of its polygons keeping the most visible edges joined, the rest cut and trimmed back; "seen" is higher facing up or to the front, across flat stretches and on outward corners). Tests in `seams/tests.rs`: disks and non-disks, a cube's twelve edges, a cylinder's one line, a sphere's bottom-to-top cut at the back, every primitive, random starting cuts, empty and stray input.

- **Unwrapping** (`unwrap`, [decision 0099](../../docs/decisions/0099-unwrap-relax-straighten.md)): `unwrap` (least squares conformal maps through `solve`'s sparse conjugate gradients; pieces that can't lie flat cut open with `seams::disk_cuts` and the cut marked; strips of square quads laid out as straight grids; each piece turned to its smallest box, a quarter turn to match how it lay, sized to the object's texel density, put where it was; pieces over `SOLVE_UP_TO` wedges projected flat), `straighten` (the grid layout alone) and `relax` (springs towards real side lengths, the chosen polygons' corners only). `chart` holds a piece's wedges (a point once per side of a cut). `unwrap_with` takes `UnwrapOptions { strips, strip_angle, method, only, hide }` (every choice an unwrap makes, [decision 0110](../../docs/decisions/0110-controllable-automation.md): `strip_angle` up to `MAX_STRIP_ANGLE`, `hide` where closed pieces are cut open; `is_plain`, `is_real`; `relax` up to `MAX_RELAX_STEPS`) ([decision 0109](../../docs/decisions/0109-pins-and-unwrap-methods.md)): `only` (`UnfoldAxis::Across` or `Up`) fits the new layout over the old by a similarity and keeps the other coordinate. Pinned corners (`mesh::uvs::pins`) are held: two or more apart and the piece is solved around them in place (mirrored pieces solved mirrored back and turned over again), one and the piece is moved back to it; `relax` never moves them and `straighten` keeps the first. Tests in `unwrap/tests.rs`: a flat grid stays an even grid where it was, a box's sides keep their real sizes, a cylinder's side comes out straight, a ball without folds and a closed one cut open, quarter turns kept, only the asked pieces change, straightening and relaxing, and odd input; an ignored benchmark (`unwrap_benchmark`) times growing balls.

- **Texel density** (`density`, [decision 0100](../../docs/decisions/0100-texture-size-and-texel-density.md)): `areas` (UV and real areas of polygons), `density` (texture widths per unit of length) and `set_density` (each piece holding the polygons scaled about its middle to a density). Tests in the module.

- **Packing** (`pack`, [decision 0101](../../docs/decisions/0101-packing.md)): `pack` places pieces by shape on a grid of cells (`Grid`: rows of bits; each piece drawn, its inside and every cell its sides cross, grown by half the padding), lowest then leftmost, turned to its smallest box and tried a quarter turn round, at the largest common scale (found by halving); `PackOptions` (padding, rotate, stack, cells); identical pieces stacked (a half turn allowed). `islands_of`, `piece_of`, `place`. Tests in `pack/tests.rs`: padding kept and the square filled, a small piece nesting in a frame's hole, stacking, turning, nothing to pack, exact placing; an ignored benchmark (`pack_benchmark`).

- **Stretch** (`stretch`, [decision 0102](../../docs/decisions/0102-checker-and-stretch.md)): `stretch_of` measures each polygon (`Stretch`: how unevenly it lies, how much room it gets against the shape's density, mirrored, no room), `off` (how far off, as a power of two) and `explain` (the problem and its fix in plain words); `SOME` (1.5) and `FAR` (2). Tests in the module.

- **The numbered checker** (`grid`, [decision 0107](../../docs/decisions/0107-numbered-checker.md)): `numbered_checker(size, from, to)` makes the image (RGBA, first row the top of the square): `CELLS` × `CELLS` cells labelled by `label` (A1 at the top left) with a 5 × 7 pixel font of the app's own, each a `SQUARES_PER_CELL` checker of its colour's lighter and darker shades, the colours blended round the colour wheel from `from` to `to`. Tests in the module.

- **Ways of laying flat** (`methods`, [decision 0109](../../docs/decisions/0109-pins-and-unwrap-methods.md)): `UnwrapMethod` (`Conformal`, `AngleBased`, `SizeKeeping`, `Automatic`) and `lay_flat`, working on each triangle's flat shape (`real_shapes`): `conformal_shapes` (LSCM with any pins held), `angle_based_shapes` (linear angle-based flattening: the angles evened out under triangle, full-turn and wheel constraints, weighted by 1/β², solved as one sparse system with `solve`), `as_rigid_as_possible` (from LSCM, `RIGID_ROUNDS` of best turns and a fixed cotangent-weighted solve) and `distortion` (area-weighted stretch one way over the other, size off the average, a price for turned-over triangles). Automatic tries all three and keeps the lowest, a later one only when 1% better. Tests in `methods/tests.rs`: flat pieces perfect every way, the evened angles adding up round inner points, Keep sizes keeping sizes better on a dome, Automatic picking the best and the same every time, pins held every way, one pin, mirrored pieces staying mirrored, unfolding one way, relax and straighten with pins, and odd pieces; an ignored benchmark (`methods_benchmark`).
- **Full control, never random** ([decision 0110](../../docs/decisions/0110-controllable-automation.md)): `seams::suggest_with` and `SuggestOptions { corner_angle, hard_edges, hide }` (`HideSeams`: below and behind, below, behind, shortest); `mend::mend_with` and `MendOptions { on, uneven, size_off }` (`LIMITS`). Nothing uses chance or the clock; hash maps are only looked up, ties go by element ids. `automation_tests.rs` runs suggest, unwrap with every method, relax and pack repeatedly and compares bit for bit, and checks each choice changes what it should.
- **Seam preferences and stretch budgets** ([decision 0111](../../docs/decisions/0111-seam-preferences-and-stretch-budget.md)): `seams::visibility` reads each edge's `mesh::uvs::SeamPreference` (prefer counts as least visible, never as most), Suggest seams leaves never edges out of its hard edges and corners, and `seams::budget_cuts` slits a disk piece from its most squeezed or one-way-stretched point to its border, the most hidden way, until nearly all of it (`methods::nearly_all`, all but the worst `BUDGET_SPARE` of its area) is within budget (at most `MAX_BUDGET_CUTS`). `SuggestOptions::budget` and `UnwrapOptions::budget` are the budget for every piece; a piece's own (`mesh::uvs::budget_of`) wins. `methods::stretch_parts` gives each triangle's one-way stretch and size. `preference_tests.rs` covers them.
- **Locked pieces and piece settings** ([decision 0112](../../docs/decisions/0112-locked-pieces-and-piece-settings.md)): `unwrap::chosen_pieces` (so Unwrap, Relax, Straighten and trim placing) and `density::set_density` skip locked pieces (`mesh::uvs::any_locked`), `mend_with` skips locked polygons; `PieceSettings { method, strips, density }` (read with `of`, laid over the unwrap's options with `over`) gives each piece's own settings to `lay_out`. `lock_tests.rs` covers them.
- **Trim sheets** (`trim`, [decision 0106](../../docs/decisions/0106-trim-sheets.md)): `place(mesh, faces, bottom, top)` lays each piece holding `faces` on a row: a straight grid for strips of square quads (else as it lay, or unwrapped if squashed), the right way round, its long side along the row, its height filling the row, end to end from the row's left end; `on_row` (within `ON_ROW`) tells pieces on a row, which `check::UvReport::new` skips. Tests in `trim/tests.rs`: a turned strip laid straight, pieces end to end, a cylinder's side at its true proportions, every primitive, bad rows.

- **The UV bake check** (`check`, [decision 0105](../../docs/decisions/0105-bake-readiness-for-uvs.md)): `UvReport::new(mesh, hard, rows)` (hard edges where the UVs don't split, polygons folded or squashed against their piece, pieces outside the square, pieces `UNEVEN_DENSITY` times off the area-weighted usual density) and `check_sheet(pieces, size, padding)` (`SheetPiece`s of one texture drawn into a grid of up to `RESOLUTION` cells: overlaps where a cell's middle is in two pieces, `stacked` when the two are identical, `too_close` where pieces' outward spreads meet within the padding, with one cell's slack). Tests in `check/tests.rs`: every primitive clean and packed clean, hard edges inside a piece, folded, outside and uneven pieces, stacked against overlapping, a piece nudged 1 px from another, odd input; an ignored benchmark (`check_benchmark`).
- **Warnings and measuring** ([decision 0115](../../docs/decisions/0115-seeing-and-checking.md)): `warnings::warnings(meshes, group, rows)` gives each shape's `Warnings` (polygons of pieces overlapping another on the same texture, by `check_sheet` on a `WARNING_CELLS` grid, and folded within their piece; polygons of mirrored pieces; counts), leaving out stacked and trim pieces; `mirrored_pieces`. `measure::Surface` (a shape's triangles in the texture and the world, gridded; `at`) and `surface_length` (the line walked in `MEASURE_STEPS`, world distance added within each piece). Tests in `warnings/tests.rs` and `measure/tests.rs`. Pieces outside the texture square (`Warnings::outside`, `outside_pieces`, `OUTSIDE_SLACK`; trim pieces left out) since [decision 0118](../../docs/decisions/0118-select-connected-and-outside-warnings.md).
- **Seam reasons** ([decision 0116](../../docs/decisions/0116-why-is-this-here.md)): `seams::suggest_explained` is `suggest_with` with a `SeamReason` per seam (`HardEdge`, `SharpCorner(angle)`, `ToLieFlat`, `Preferred`, `StretchBudget(budget)`); `suggest_with` is built on it. `seams::edge_angle` gives the angle two polygons meet at. `reason_tests.rs` checks the reasons cover exactly the suggested seams and say the right thing.

- **Mending** (`mend`, [decision 0104](../../docs/decisions/0104-uvs-that-survive-modelling.md)): `mend(before, after)` after a modelling edit. It finds the changed polygons with broken UVs (squashed, mirrored, `UNEVEN`, `SIZE_OFF`, judged against up to `NEARBY` polygons around them) and marks seams where changed, unbroken polygons meet with different UVs. It solves each cluster of broken polygons around the kept ones next to it (`unwrap::conformal_pinned`, the kept corners held; the kept group with the longest shared border, found by `kept_groups`' search that stops as soon as it can tell, others parted by seams). Clusters with nothing kept next to them, or still broken, go to `unwrap::lay_out` at the shape's usual density and are slid inside the square (`keep_inside`). Nothing past `MEND_UP_TO`. Tests in `mend/tests.rs` cover:
  - extrude, inset, fill, bevel (edge and point) and loop cut;
  - a hand-turned piece and a mirrored one;
  - no UVs, and no seam marks;
  - every tool on every primitive.

  An ignored benchmark (`mend_benchmark`) is included.

The basics every part of the app reads (UVs per corner, islands, seam marks, moving UVs) are in `mesh::uvs`, and the primitives' ready-made UVs in `mesh::primitives`.
