# sculpt

**Layer 1 · Core**

Sculpting: a Sculpt layer's data, the brushes, and the live sculpt a stroke works on.

## Purpose

Holds what a Sculpt layer keeps and everything a stroke does, as plain data and plain functions that need no window: how far each point of a shape has moved, measured along its surface, in chunks shared between copies (so a stroke's undo step keeps only the chunks it touched); fitting a sculpt to a shape whose points changed, by position; what a dab of each brush does (Draw and Smooth, and the brushes for forms of decision 0306); greyscale alphas that shape a dab (decision 0308); a tree of boxes round the polygons that follows moving points; and the live sculpt the interface runs strokes on. See [decision 0304](../../docs/decisions/0304-sculpt-mode-and-layers.md) and the plan in [docs/plans/phase-17.md](../../docs/plans/phase-17.md).

## Must never

- Know about objects, the scene, undo, files, the GPU or the interface: the scene keeps a `Sculpt` in an edit and evaluates it; the interface turns strokes into dabs
- Change a `Sculpt` once made: a stroke makes a new one sharing every chunk it didn't touch
- Crash on bad input: a dab with a radius or place that isn't a number, an empty shape, a sculpt made for another shape (fitted, not refused), parts from a damaged file (refused by `Sculpt::from_parts` and `Moves::from_chunks`)
- Give a different result for the same input: the same shape gives the same frames, to the last bit, wherever they are worked out

## Depends on

`mesh` (the shape, its topology, `parallel`), `words` (brush and falloff names), `glam`.

## Status

- **`Sculpt`** (`layer.rs`): the structure of the shape it was sculpted on, where its points were, and `Moves`; `new`, `from_parts`, `with_moves`, `fits`, `fitted_to` (nearest point by position through `nearest::Grid`), `apply` (only chunks with moves are looked at; `Fit::found_again`), `memory_apart` and `memory_size` for the undo budget. Equal sculpts compare by pointer first. `MAX_POINTS` 30 million.
- **`moves`**: `CHUNK` 16 384 points; a chunk is nothing until a point in it moves; `differing` compares only chunks that aren't shared.
- **`frames`**: a point's facing (its polygons' area-weighted facing) and a direction along the surface (towards its first neighbour); a loose point has the object's axes.
- **`brush`**: `BrushKind` (thirteen: Draw, Smooth, Clay strips, Clay, Inflate, Crease, Pinch, Flatten, Scrape, Fill, Grab, Elastic grab, Snake hook; `grabs`, names and plain explanations), `Falloff` (Smooth, Sharp, Flat, Spike), `BrushSettings` (radius on screen, strength, falloff, spacing; `standard` per brush, `clamped`), `Dab`, `draw` and `smooth`.
- **`forms`** ([decision 0306](../../docs/decisions/0306-sculpt-brushes-for-forms.md)): what a dab of each other brush does to a point: `Plane` (the plane under the brush) and `clay` (up to a level above it, hollows first; Clay strips measured to a square turned with the stroke, `square_distance`, `along_plane`), `inflate`, `crease`, `pinch`, `flatten` (with `Side` for Scrape and Fill), `grab`, `kelvinlet` (a regularised Kelvinlet, volume kept) and `elastic`.
- **`tree::FaceTree`**: boxes round polygons in Morton order (built in a fraction of a second for millions), `refit` and `refit_points` round moved polygons (all the boxes above at once when many moved), `refit_faces` (from the polygons a dab found) and `refit_all` (every box, the leaves on every core, when half the shape or more moved; decision 0313), `ray_hit` (either side, with a little slack at the edges of boxes and polygons so a line through a point never slips past the near side), `near`.
- **`alpha`** ([decision 0308](../../docs/decisions/0308-sculpt-pens-strokes-alphas.md)): `Alpha` (one byte a pixel, at most `MAX_ALPHA_SIDE` 1024; `at` blends between pixels, `y` up, black outside; refused when empty, too big or black everywhere), `Made` (Stones, Bark, Scales, Stitches, Rivets, Cracks, Scallops, 256 pixels, made once by `made`), `DabAlpha` (the picture, its up, an angle, following the stroke) laid on the plane under the brush.
- **`mask`** ([decision 0310](../../docs/decisions/0310-sculpt-symmetry-and-masks.md)): `Mask` (one byte a point, 0 free to 255 fully masked, in chunks of `CHUNK` shared between copies; `free`), and what changes a whole mask: `invert`, `grow` and `shrink` by one ring, `sharpen`, `soften`, `from_cavity`, `from_edges`, `from_facing`, `set_where` (the box and lasso masks). `Sculpt::mask` and `with_mask`; `fitted_to` fits the mask with the moves.
- **`symmetry`**: `Symmetry` (mirror axes and radial 2 to `MOST_RADIAL` 64), `copies` (each a `Repeat`: its turn and whether it reflects), `mirror_pairs` for Make symmetrical (`NotSymmetric` when a point has no partner).
- **`LiveSculpt`** (`live.rs`): `new` (fitted, applied, the tree built), `hit`, `dab` (points in reach once each, their new places on every core above 16 384 points, the tree refitted; the plane and facing under the brush worked out once a dab; a grabbing brush takes hold on its stroke's first dab, `held` saying where, then goes to each dab's centre: Grab from where its points were, Elastic grab step by step, Snake hook gathering afresh), `dab_with` (the same with an alpha: each point's move times the alpha under it; the grabbing brushes take none), `shape` (shared, new only when changed), `finish` (moved points become moves; the shape set to exactly what `apply` gives), `cancel`, `follow` (undo and redo, chunk by chunk). Smooth keeps open edges in line. Decision 0310: every move scaled by how free the mask leaves the point; `set_symmetry`, the copies of a dab worked out together from the shape before it and added (a reflected copy's alpha turned over, a grab held per copy); the Mask brush; `set_mask`, `mask_values` (for drawing), `unmasked_middle`, `begin_move` and `move_unmasked` (the gizmo), `make_symmetrical`; `finish`, `cancel` and `follow` carry the mask.
- **Speed** ([decision 0313](../../docs/decisions/0313-sculpting-speed.md)): `parts` shares a dab's work between the cores in fixed blocks of 4096 (`in_blocks`, `sum`, `each`), so sums come out the same on any number of cores. `LiveSculpt` finds the points in reach on every core (each polygon's corners, then each point once in the order found), works out the plane and facing in blocks, places the points at once (`Mesh::set_positions`) and refits the boxes round the polygons the dab found. It records where each point the stroke moves was (`first`): `finish` works the moves out on every core and keeps those places, and where it left them, with the new `Sculpt` (`Sculpt::keeping`, never saved, not part of equality, counted in `memory_size` and `memory_apart`), so `follow` undoes and redoes a stroke made on the same shape by putting points back (`kept_way_to`), and `cancel` puts the stroke's points back without working them out. `speed_benchmark` (ignored): every brush with about 140 000 points in reach on the heavy test shape, a symmetric stroke, finishing, undo and redo.
- **Tests** (`mask_tests.rs` for the mask and symmetry: a fully masked shape still under every brush and the gizmo, partly masked points moving partly, grow then shrink, invert, sharpen, soften, masks from edges, facing and cavity, a mirrored stroke and alpha staying symmetric, radial 2 and 64, Make symmetrical and its refusal; `alpha_tests.rs` for alphas: a stamp matching its alpha exactly, black parts staying, turned by its up, angle and the stroke, grabbing brushes taking none, a stroke with an alpha evaluating exactly to the live shape; `alpha_benchmark` (ignored); `brush_tests.rs` for the brushes for forms: each on a flat grid against known results, with Ctrl, at no strength and bigger than the shape, Grab and Snake hook following, Elastic grab keeping a ball's volume within 2%; `tests.rs` and each module's own): a dab moves only points in reach, by the falloff; Ctrl digs in; no strength does nothing; a finished stroke evaluates to exactly the live shape; a stroke keeps only its chunks; the edge of an open shape; one polygon and an empty shape; Smooth flattens a bump; cancel; undo and redo through `follow`; fitting a finer shape by position; detail riding a turned shape; damaged parts. `dab_benchmark` (ignored; run with `--release --ignored --nocapture`): on a million points, a dab of about 12 000 points takes about 1.4 ms and of about 140 000 about 19 ms on two cores; finishing the stroke about 35 ms. `brush_benchmark` (ignored): ten dabs of each brush with about 140 000 points in reach on a million points.
