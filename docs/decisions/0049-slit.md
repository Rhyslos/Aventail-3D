# 0049. Slit: a line of points over the shape, cut as seen; file format version 7

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-14
- **Follows:** [0028](0028-ask-about-controls.md) (ask about controls), [0043](0043-attribute-layers-and-the-builder.md) (the builder), [0047](0047-loop-cut.md) (X kept for Slit)

## Problem

Slit cuts new edges wherever you draw a line, across any polygons, not only rings of four-sided ones as Loop cut does. Three things needed deciding:
- the controls;
- what "cutting along a line" means on a 3D shape seen through a camera;
- how the cut is stored so it can be replayed.

## Decision

1. **Controls** (the project lead: "the same way Cinema 4D lets you do it", visible only with a switch):
   - Press `X` in the Points, Edges or Polygons mode.
   - Click, or press and drag, to put the line's points down. Each point snaps to a point, an edge or a polygon of a selected object under the mouse; with nothing there it floats in space.
   - The shape is cut as the line grows.
   - Until it is finished, the line stays editable:
     - drag a point to move it;
     - click the line to add a point;
     - Ctrl + click a point to remove it;
     - hold Shift to keep a stretch at 15° steps on screen.
   - **Esc** or **Enter** finishes the line, as does picking another tool, leaving the editing modes, or any other change to the document. Ctrl + Z takes the cut back.
   - Right click or Esc while dragging a point puts it back.
   - The line is drawn on top of the view.
   - **Cut through** (off as standard) is in the Attributes panel, for the next line and as the Last edit.
   - A whole line is one undo step.
2. **What is cut.** The line is seen from an eye: the camera, or its direction in a flat view.
   - Each stretch between two points sweeps a fan from the eye (a strip in a flat view). Every polygon crossed inside it is cut along it.
   - A line point inside a polygon becomes a new point, so the cut can turn there.
   - A cut has to go in and out of a polygon. A line ending inside one doesn't cut that one, so no dangling edges are made.
   - Visible only: a polygon must face the eye, and the middle of its cut must not be hidden behind another polygon. Through: everything under the line is cut.
   - Polygons beside a cut edge that aren't cut themselves take the new point as a corner, so no gap opens.
   - Values come along through the builder. It gains `PointFrom::Inside` and `CornerFrom::Inside`, which blend a polygon's corners at a spot inside it (mean value weights, which reproduce flat UVs exactly).
3. **The edit:** `Operation::Slit { line: SlitLine }`.
   - It holds the line's points, the eye and Cut through, all in the object's own space.
   - It is worked out from the shape as it is, with no ids or anchors, so it keeps cutting the same place when the shape's settings change.
   - Up to 256 points.
   - The size guard counts the pieces it would cut off.
   - Each selected object gets its own slit.
4. **File format version 7:** version 6's operations, plus slits. On opening:
   - 2 to 256 points, all real numbers;
   - an eye of real numbers, and a flat view's direction not zero.

## Consequences

- Any polygon can be cut, including triangles and polygons with many sides. This is the general-purpose cutting tool, with Loop cut for rings.
- Checking what's hidden tests the cut's middle against every polygon. That's quick for props, but slow on shapes of millions of polygons. If that matters, it can use the picking index later.
- Not yet:
  - snapping a point to the middle of an edge;
  - a "restrict to selection" switch;
  - cutting with a straight line through the whole shape (like a plane cut).

  These can join later as Attributes panel switches.
- Projects saved now need this version of the app or newer.
