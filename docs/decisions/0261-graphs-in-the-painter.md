# 0261. Graphs in the painter

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-07

## Problem

Phase 12's fifth milestone ([the plan](../plans/phase-12.md), [decision 0256](0256-material-graph-plan.md)) is "Graphs in the painter": use a material graph as a fill layer or a smart material, with the model's baked maps as inputs, so "lighter on edges" or "moss in the crevices" follow the model being painted.

A graph makes one seamless square that repeats (decisions 0257, 0259); the model's maps (curvature, occlusion…) cover its whole texture square, laid out by its UVs, and don't repeat. Things to decide: how the two meet in one graph, how a graph reads the model, what the layer keeps and when it is made again, how settings are changed per layer, where it runs, and how undo and saving work.

## Options

- **Running a graph on the model:** work the whole graph out on the model's texture square (every pattern would then repeat once across all the UVs, the wrong size, and patterns counted in repeats would be stretched), work it all out as a repeat and lay it (the model's maps can't be in a repeat), or **split the graph where the model comes in** (chosen). Boxes nothing from the model feeds make the repeat at its real-world size, as always; a box from the model and every box it feeds work on the model's texture square at the texture's size, and a repeat going into one is first laid across the square the number of times the model's size and UVs say (`Value::repeated`), exactly as the Material mode's preview lays it (`material::laying::repeats`, decision 0260). Boxes on the square are told it covers that many repeats' worth of centimetres, so sizes in centimetres stay right in both parts.
- **How a graph reads the model:** an input on Material out, settings on the layer naming maps, or **boxes of their own** (chosen): a **From the model** group in the box search (after Make) with **Curvature** (Curvature, Edges and Creases), **Occlusion**, **Position** (Up, Across, Front to back), **Thickness** and **Facing up**. Boxes fit everything else (wires, Levels after them, Mix materials' Where) and need nothing new in the canvas. In the Material mode there is no model, so they give a **stand-in** of rounded bumps that tiles, worked out the same way, so a graph can be built and seen first.
- **Where the model's maps come from:** the Bakes section's maps (only when the person has baked, and at the bake's quality), or **baked for the graph** (chosen), on the same thread as the graph, only the maps its boxes read, the way the baker and smart masks work them out (`bake::model_maps`), at the Bakes section's quality. Kept per material until the shape, UVs, size or the maps needed change. Texels no polygon covers take their nearest covered neighbour's value eight texels out, then a neutral value, so a blur or warp near a UV piece's edge doesn't pull nonsense in.
- **The layer:** a new kind of layer content, or **a paint layer made from settings** (chosen), like gradient, light-and-shade and material layers (decisions 0194, 0196, 0210): `Layer::graph` (`paint::GraphFill`: the graph's id, the layer's own values, and `made`). Its pixels are ordinary paint, so masks, opacity, blend modes, clipping, merging, the composite, padding and the texture export all work unchanged, and an older layer list shows it as paint. Only polygons' texels are painted, as for material layers.
- **Settings per layer:** change the graph from the layer (every layer using it would change), copy the graph per layer, or **let a graph mark settings "shown in the painter"** (chosen): `GraphBox::shown` (keys), changed by `GraphEdit::ShowInPainter` (one undo step), ticked under **Shown in the painter** in Attributes. Each graph fill shows those settings and keeps its own values (`GraphFill::values`), applied to a copy of the graph before it runs (`Graph::with_settings`; a value whose box or setting is gone is ignored). Milestone 7's "your own boxes" choose their sliders the same way; this is the minimal version of that idea, kept on the boxes so it can grow into it.
- **When it is made again:** on a list of events, or **by comparing** (chosen): `GraphFill::made` keeps a hash of what the pixels were made from (the graph with the layer's values, the texture size, the model's layout key, the repeats, the realism switch). Whenever the scene or the drawing changes and nothing is held down, a layer whose hash differs from what it would be made from now is made again. That one rule covers its settings, the graph changed in the Material mode, paint following the model (decision 0189), the texture size, and undo: a layer undone has its old pixels and its old hash, so it is already up to date.
- **Where it runs:** on the interface's thread (as gradients are made today, with a "Making…" note), or **on another thread** (chosen), one layer at a time, with an evaluator kept between runs so a small change works out only the boxes it touched.
- **Undo:** the made pixels join the step of the change that asked for them (`Document::execute_with_last`), as other made layers' do, **but only if nothing changed while they were made**; otherwise they are worked out again for the scene as it now is (quick: the maps and boxes are kept). Joining a later, unrelated step would make undo take back pixels without their settings.

## Decision

- **matgraph:** `model` (`ModelMap`, `ModelMaps`, `OnModel` with `tile_size` and `square_cm`, `uses_model`, `maps_needed`, `Value::repeated`), `Evaluator::run_on_model` and `material_on_model`; `Group::Model` ("From the model") and five boxes (`boxes::from_model`); `Job::with_model` / `model_map`; `GraphBox::shown`, `GraphEdit::ShowInPainter`, `Graph::shown`, `Graph::with_settings`.
- **paint:** `GraphFill` and `GraphValue` (`graph_fill`), `Layer::graph` (a made layer; checked in `is_valid`). paint now depends on matgraph (both layer 1).
- **bake:** `model_maps` (`ModelBake`: the maps and the covered texels) and `graph_fill` (`MadeGraph`: colour, and roughness, metal and height greys). bake now depends on matgraph.
- **ui:** `workbench::paint_graphs` makes graph fills; the Paint layers panel's **···** → **+ Graph fill** lists the project's graphs, and the shelf's Materials tab has a card per graph (a dot for one that follows the model); the layer shows the graph mark in its picture and **Graph fill** under its name; its settings show the graph, **Open in the graph** (switches to the Material mode on it), how its making stands or why it can't be made (in red), what of the model it follows, and the shown settings with **↺** back to the graph's value; Attributes in the Material mode has **Shown in the painter**.
- **File format 97:** graph fills (`v64::LayerV64::graph`, `v97::GraphFillV97`) and shown settings (`v95::BoxV95::shown`). The pixels are saved as for any paint layer, so a project opens without making them again.
- **Controls:** no new keys or mouse actions: buttons, menus, switches and the shelf's cards.

## Consequences

- A smart material is just a graph with From the model boxes; there is no separate kind of thing to learn.
- On the model's square, Warp, Blur and edge finding wrap round the texture's edges as they do in a repeat; UV pieces touching the edge may see a little of the other side. The model's maps reaching past each piece's edge keep this small.
- Repeats come from the first shape using the material (as the preview's Model does); several objects of very different sizes sharing a texture set share one repeat, the same as giving them one material.
- The From the model boxes show a stand-in in the Material mode; the preview on the selected model doesn't yet use that model's maps. Milestone 6 (giving a graph's material to a shape) can pass them in through `OnModel`.
- A graph fill whose graph is deleted keeps its last pixels and says so; undo brings the graph back.
- A graph that can't run (no Material out) isn't tried again until something it is made from changes; the panel says why.
