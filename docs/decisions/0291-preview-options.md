# 0291. Preview options

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-13
- **Follows:** [0269](0269-material-extras.md) (the material extras), [0260](0260-preview-and-checks.md) (the preview), [0207](0207-realism-channels.md) (the realistic preview's turnable sun), [0230](0230-viewport-looks.md) (viewport looks), [0028](0028-ask-about-controls.md) (asking about controls)

## Problem

Phase 12's thirteenth extra (milestone 28, [decision 0269](0269-material-extras.md)): the Material mode's preview had a fixed camera from the front right, one light and one backdrop. The plan asked for a choice of lighting and backdrop, comparing two graphs side by side, and a before/after slider. Turning the preview needed a mouse action, so the project lead was asked first. Milestone 27's health check also says detail "turns to mush at 12 m", which nobody could see for themselves.

## Options

- **Turning the preview** (asked of the project lead, 2026-11-13, [decision 0028](0028-ask-about-controls.md)). Offered: the same as the 3D view (recommended), a left drag to turn (common in material previews, but the left button is the app's selecting button everywhere else), or buttons only. **Chosen: the same as the 3D view.** Right drag turns, middle drag moves, the wheel zooms, Alt + mouse when **Alt + mouse everywhere** is on, and whichever navigation scheme is chosen in the viewport's picker works here too. **Shift + right drag turns the preview's light** (chosen with it: no scheme uses Shift with the right button, so it never clashes).
- **Where the options go.** (a) More rows of buttons above the picture. (b) **A bar of two menus above the picture** (chosen): **Light and backdrop** and **Compare**, so the picture keeps its room in a short panel (milestone 17 suggested a bar menu). **Reset the view** shows under the picture only once it has been turned, moved or zoomed, with how far away it is seen from.
- **Lighting.** The four looks stay (Unlit, Lit, Toon, Realistic). The light turns in **Lit and Toon as well as Realistic**: the renderer's painted surface already carried a light turn for the realistic sun; the Lit and Toon key light now turns with it (the shader's `turned_key`). The main viewport keeps its light still outside Realistic (its slider is only there), so nothing changes for it. Realistic adds the viewport's **Environment** (Studio lights, Outdoors, Sunset) and **Exposure** in the same menu. Unlit has no light: Shift + right drag there says so in the status bar, and so does Flat.
- **Backdrops.** The viewport looks (decision 0230) already pair a backdrop with its lights, so the backdrop choices are looks: **Studio** (the standard, as before), **Even grey** (one middle grey, sRGB 118, in the studio's light: nothing to throw the eye off when judging colours), **Dark** (the Classic look) and **Dusk** (warm light, cool shade). A plain colour picker was left: four tested looks are quicker and none can be set to something unreadable.
- **Comparing.** (a) Two graphs only, as planned. (b) **One Compare menu with Before or another graph, shown either way** (chosen): "before" is a picture kept with **Keep this as before** (shared, not copied: free to keep), so a change can be judged against it; another graph is any other graph in the project. **Slider** (one picture split by a line, the standard; a left drag or a click on the picture moves the line) or **Side by side** (two smaller pictures). Both turn together, with one camera. Each side is named on the picture when the names fit. Considered and left: before as an undo step to go back to (the undo history already does that); comparing more than two (the panel is small).
- **Seeing it from afar.** (a) A separate "far away" shape. (b) **The wheel** (chosen): zooming out on a 3D shape says how far away it is seen from, so a health check distance can be tried; on **Flat** the wheel zooms out to as many as 64 repeats across, saying how many metres show. Flat's picture is averaged down by whole halvings as it zooms out (as an engine's smaller copies are), so it shows what the distance really does, without shimmer.
- **No slowing down.** Turning, moving and zooming change only the camera: the same laid scene is drawn again from elsewhere, the graph is not worked out again and the material is not laid again (tested: no runs, no lays). The graph compared with is worked out on its own thread only when it changes, and each side has its own renderer slot (`ViewportView::slot` 0 and 1, a second `GpuViewport` in the app) so the two never upload over each other.
- **Left for later.** The translucency back light (milestone 14): the painted surface has no translucency channel, so a light from behind can't show light through a leaf yet; it needs the renderer to carry the map. A crisp main viewport for painted pixel art (milestone 24): it is about the main viewport rather than the preview, and needs a nearest sampler for painted textures in the renderer. A looping range or speed on the player. Saving the preview options with the project (like the canvas's pan and zoom, they are ways of looking, kept for the session).

## Decision

- `ui::material::preview_options` (the bar: `Backdrop`, `Lighting`, the Light and backdrop and Compare menus), `turning` (`Turning`: the camera as a `tools::Navigator` framed on the shape, Flat's repeats and offset; `drag_kind` maps a button and keys through the viewport's `NavigationSettings`, Shift + right first; `preview_view`), `comparing` (`CompareWith`, `CompareHow`, `Comparing`, `Source`, the other graph's `Pictures`), `preview_picture` (drawing either way, the input), `preview_ball` (the processor's ball, Flat averaged down, the checker).
- `PreviewContext::navigation` (the viewport's navigation settings).
- Renderer: `ViewportRenderer::render_preview` uses `view.slot` 0 or 1 (the app keeps two preview renderers); the painted surface's light turn turns the Lit and Toon key light (`shade_turned`, `turned_key` in `viewport.wgsl`); the main viewport sends a light turn only in Realistic.
- **Controls:** right drag turns, middle drag moves, wheel zooms, Alt + mouse when on (as in the 3D view, any scheme); Shift + right drag turns the light; a left drag or click moves the compare line. No keys.
- **No file format change.**

## Consequences

The preview can be looked at from any side and in any of four lights and backdrops, and the effect of a change can be seen against a kept picture or another graph, without the picture slowing work on the graph. The health check's distances can be checked by zooming out. Two renderers for the preview take a little more graphics memory while comparing (one more picture and texture); both are let go of when the tab sleeps.
