# 0285. Stamp images: a Stamps box scattering small pictures of your own

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-13

## Problem

Phase 12's seventh extra (milestone 22, [decision 0269](0269-material-extras.md)): **scatter your own small images** (a rivet, a leaf, a rune) as **stamps**, the way Scatter ([decision 0271](0271-scatter.md)) scatters a shape. A box should hold **one or more** small pictures brought in through the existing open dialog, keeping their **see-through parts** (milestone 21's photos are laid over grey and packed as JPEG, which has no alpha); each copy picks one at random (with weights if simple), placed with Scatter's placing (grid or random, keep apart, the Where mask), with size, turn, colour and height variation, tiling seamlessly and the same for the same seed. It should give the stamps' colours, a height, where they are, and a whole material when one is plugged in. Undo, saving, copy and paste (also into another tab) must carry the pictures. No new keys.

## Options

- **A new box, or Scatter gaining a "stamp images" source.** Scatter already has four inputs (Shape, Colour, Material, Where) and five outputs, and decides each copy's outline from what is plugged in; a list of pictures with weights would add a third way of working to one box, and its Attributes would hold both settings for shapes and a list of pictures. **A separate Stamps box** keeps both easy to explain: Scatter copies what is *plugged in*; Stamps copies the *pictures it holds*. It shares Scatter's placing code and settings (the same names, the same meaning), so nothing new has to be learnt about placing. Chosen.
- **Storing the pictures.** As `Photo`s (milestone 21: RGB only, packed as JPEG: no see-through parts, and JPEG blurs a small picture's crisp edges); as a `Painting` (square only, 256 pixels or more); **a new `Stamp`**: sRGB bytes with straight alpha, any shape up to **512 pixels** on its longer side (a stamp covers a small part of a texture; more would only make projects bigger), packed once as a **PNG** when brought in, which keeps every pixel exactly, so saving never packs again and opening gives the same pixels. A box holds a **list** (`Stamps`, at most **16**, at most **8 MB** packed together so a graph file stays readable), each with a **weight** from 1 to 10.
- **Which copy is on top.** Scatter puts the higher copy on top for shapes. Stamps are pictures with soft, see-through edges: each pixel **blends the copies over it in a fixed random order** (Scatter's `rank`), colour and height alike, so a stamp's edge lies cleanly over the one under it. Keep apart is **on to start with**: rivets and runes usually sit side by side.
- **The height.** From the picture's **outline** (solid parts high, flat on top) or its **lightness** (so a drawn leaf keeps its veins), then **puffed**: rounded up from the edge over a share of the way to the stamp's middle (a distance inside the outline, measured once per stamp), 0 a flat step, 1 a dome.
- **Picking a picture.** Each copy's pick comes from **its own hash** of the seed and the copy's place, never from the placing's random numbers, so adding a picture or changing a weight never moves a copy, and Scatter's random order (every graph's look) is untouched.
- **The project's own textures or painted layers as stamps.** A painted texture set is a whole square of colour without see-through parts, laid out by the model's UVs, and a layer is only part of it; cutting a stamp out of one needs a way to choose the part and its outline. Not cheap: left out (export the texture and bring a cut-out PNG in, or paint the stamp in a Painted box and use Scatter).

## Decision

- **The data** (`matgraph::stamp`): `Stamp` (width, height, RGBA bytes, the packed PNG, its name, a key hashed from its pixels; refused when empty, over `MAX_STAMP_SIDE` 512, over `MAX_STAMP_PACKED` 4 MB, or see-through everywhere), `StampEntry` (the stamp, its weight), `Stamps` (1 to `MAX_STAMPS` 16 entries, at most `MAX_STAMPS_PACKED` 8 MB packed, weights kept from 1 to `MAX_WEIGHT` 10; `with_added`, `with_weight`, `without`, `pick`, a key over every picture and weight, `memory_apart` for the undo history). `GraphBox::stamps`, kept only by kinds whose `BoxType::takes_stamps` (dropped by `from_parts` otherwise), changed by `GraphEdit::SetStamps` with the whole list ("Change a Stamps box's stamps", one undo step; the pictures are shared between the two lists), given to the job (`Job::stamps`), hashed into its key, copied by `copy_edits` (so pasting, also into another tab, and your own boxes carry them).
- **The box** (`stamps`, Detail, after Scatter; `matgraph::boxes::stamps`): inputs **Under** (a colour the stamps lie over), **Material**, **Where** (a shape); outputs **Material** (only with one plugged in), **Colour**, **Height**, **Copies** (where any stamp is, by its see-through), **Random** (a grey per copy). Settings: Scatter's Arrange, Spacing (15 cm), Size (10 cm, the longer side), Size varies, Turn, Turn varies, Height varies, Colour varies (tints each copy's own colours), **Keep apart (on)**, Loose, Shift rows, plus **Height from** (Its outline / Its lightness) and **Puff** (0.3), and Seed (a `SettingKind::Seed`, so the dice and variations find it). Placing is `scatter::placing` unchanged. Each picture is made ready per job at about the biggest copy's size (averaged down, so small copies don't shimmer); up to 12 copies blend at a pixel. The material keeps its shine, metal, occlusion and curvature: the stamps' colours go over its colour, their height stands on its height (never above white), its normals are bent by their edges (Material from height's standard Depth), and a see-through material is solid where stamps are. With nothing brought in it refuses in plain words ("No stamps brought in yet. Choose Add a stamp… in Attributes…"); `run` alone stamps a white dot, for the shared box checks.
- **Reading pictures** (`fileio::image_file::read_stamp`, `open_stamp`, `stamp_from_packed`): through the existing image reader (sizes limited before pixels are read, fuzz-tested), **see-through parts kept**; made smaller to fit 512 with colours weighed by how solid they are (no dark fringe); fully see-through pixels' hidden colours cleared; packed as PNG, its pixels taken from that PNG. A picture with no see-through parts is a whole-square stamp, and the note says how to give it an outline.
- **Attributes** (`ui::material::stamp_rows`): each stamp's picture (on mid grey, so see-through parts show), its name, **How often** (1 to 10) and **Take off**; **Add a stamp…** (the app's open dialog through `WorkbenchAction::ChooseStamp`, `FilePurpose::Stamp` without one; greyed out, with why, when the box holds 16). A double-click on the box asks for the next stamp too. The app hands the stamp back with `Workbench::add_box_stamp`.
- **Saved:** project file format **109** (`fileio::project::v109`, `v95::BoxV95::stamps`: each stamp's name, weight and PNG, unpacked through the image reader and refused when too big or too many). Graph and box files read format 5 and write it only when a box holds stamps. The undo history counts only the pictures a change's before and after don't share (`scene::memory`), so changing a weight beside 16 stamps holds almost nothing.
- **No new keys or mouse actions**: buttons, the existing open dialog, and the double-click that already opens a box.

## Consequences

Rivets on a metal plate, leaves on the ground or runes on a stone are a Stamps box with a picture or two and a material plugged in. Weights make some stamps rarer. The pictures are small by design; a stamp bigger than 512 pixels is made smaller when brought in. A graph holding two full Stamps boxes plus a big photo can pass the 16 MB a graph file reads (as milestone 20 noted for paintings). Stamps from the project's own textures or painted layers aren't built (see Options). Stamps aren't laid out on a sheet without wrapping (milestone 14's leaf sheet); milestone 26 (trim sheets) could add that.
