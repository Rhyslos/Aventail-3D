# 0263. Your own boxes

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-07

## Problem

Phase 12's seventh milestone ([the plan](../plans/phase-12.md), [decision 0256](0256-material-graph-plan.md)) is "Your own boxes": select boxes and turn them into one box, choosing which settings become its sliders; keep them in a library on the shelf and share them as files; open one to see the boxes inside. The controls were chosen with the plan: **Alt + G** makes one, **Shift + G** opens it back up, **Alt + C / V** pastes a linked copy. Things to decide: what an own box is in the project, whether boxes placed from one are linked, where its plugs and sliders come from, how its inside runs, what can't go inside, where the library lives, the box file, and how the canvas shows the inside.

## Options

- **What placing one puts in the graph:** a copy of the boxes inside each placed box (every copy changed apart), or **a box whose kind is the own box's id** (chosen). A project keeps its own boxes beside its graphs (`GraphStore`), each a definition: the graph inside, its plugs, its sliders, its name, words and picture. Every box placed from one runs the same inside, so placed boxes are **linked**: change the boxes inside one and they all change. Each placed box's sliders are its own settings, so copies can still differ where the maker chose.
- **Linked copies (Alt + C / V):** the plan's linked copies needed something to link; own boxes give it. **Alt + V** pastes boxes of your own linked to the ones copied (the same own box); **Ctrl + V** makes each own box copied a new own box of its own ("Rocky copy"), changed apart. The app's own boxes have nothing inside to share, so Alt + V pastes them as plain copies and says so: make them into your own box to link them. Alt + C copies just as Ctrl + C does; the paste decides.
- **Your library and the project:** library boxes linked into every project that uses them (an edit in one project would change others behind your back), or **the library as templates** (chosen). Placing a box from your library brings it (and the own boxes inside it) into the project, where it is linked as above; changing it there doesn't change your library until you keep it again (**Keep in your library** replaces the library's box with the same id). A project already holding a changed version of a library box places its own, and says so.
- **Where the library lives:** with the project, or **with the app's settings** (chosen), as your brushes are (decision 0185): each box as a box file's text, so the library and box files are one format. The shelf in the Material mode lists **In this project** and **Your library**; a click places one at the canvas's middle, a right-click offers Place it, Open it, Keep in your library, Export as a box file…, Delete from the project or Remove from your library.
- **Plugs:** chosen by hand, or **from the wires crossing the selection's edge** (chosen), editable in the making window: an input for each output outside feeding the selection (feeding every selected plug it fed, taking what the wire carries), an output for each selected output feeding a box outside; with none, the selected boxes feeding nothing give out. Each can be left out and renamed. Once boxes of it are placed, its plugs keep their keys and kinds (so wires stay sound; the scene refuses a change), but names change, and inside, a selected box offers "Give … from this box" and "Feed … into its …" to point them elsewhere.
- **Sliders:** every setting of the selected boxes is offered with a tick and a name (those shown in the painter ticked to start, and shown in the painter as sliders); inside, a box's Attributes have a **Slider:** tick per setting. A slider starts at the inner box's value, so making one never changes the picture.
- **How the inside runs:** the own box's kind (`OwnBoxType`) works its graph out with what is plugged into it (`Evaluator::run_given`: an inner input with no wire takes the own box's input), the outer graph's **seed** and **real-world size**, so making one and opening it back up keep the picture exactly. Its result is cached like any box's, keyed by a version of its definition and of the own boxes inside it, so a change inside is never mistaken for the old result.
- **What can't go inside, said in plain words:** Material out (where a graph's material comes out), the From the model boxes (they read the model's maps in the painter; a box of your own works on one repeat), a box outside the selection both fed by it and feeding it (the box would feed itself; Look at it is offered), an own box inside itself (through others too), and more than eight own boxes deep. A selection with no output, no name or too many plugs (16 a side) or sliders (32) is refused with what to do.
- **The inside on the canvas:** a window over the canvas, or **the canvas itself** (chosen): double-click a box of yours (or Open it in Attributes, or Open it on the shelf) and the canvas shows the boxes inside; the bar above shows the way back (‹ Back out, the graph, each box opened) and Esc goes back out. Inside, every edit is `scene::Command::EditOwnBox`, undone as any edit; the pictures inside follow what is plugged into the box opened (`Evaluator::run_inside`). With nothing selected inside, Attributes shows its name, What does this do?, picture (**Take its picture again**), plugs and sliders.
- **Its picture:** chosen from a file, or **what its first output makes** (chosen), taken when it is made (48 pixels square, kept in the definition) and again on request.
- **Box files:** readable JSON (`.matbox`, generic so it survives a rename), holding the box and every own box inside it; read with the limits rulebook, checked as a project's own boxes are, and fuzz-tested. **Bring in a box file…** adds one to your library.

## Decision

- **matgraph:** `own` (`OwnBox` with `OwnInput`, `OwnOutput`, `OwnSlider`; `OwnParts`; `check`, `normalised`, `info`, `inner_with`, `uses`; `OwnBoxType`; `registry_with`, `add_own`; `fresh_id`, `is_own`, `is_own_id`, `picture_of`; `Proposal` with `ProposedInput`, `ProposedOutput`, `ProposedSlider`, `make`; `open_up`); `Group::Own` ("Your own boxes", listed last in the box search); `BoxType::version` and `BoxType::own`; `Job::graph_seed`, `Job::resolved_settings`; `Evaluator::run_given` (`Given`), `Evaluator::run_inside`, `Evaluation::key`; `GraphStore` keeps own boxes and the project's registry (`registry`, `own`, `own_boxes`, `put_own`, `with_own`, `users_of`).
- **scene:** `Command::SetOwnBox` (add, replace, remove: checked; removing one in use and a box holding itself are refused) and `Command::EditOwnBox` (a change inside, checked); `Edit::SetOwnBox`; graph commands check against the project's registry.
- **fileio:** file format **98** (`project::v98`: own boxes in `GraphsV95::own`, read in an order where each comes after those inside it); `own_box_file` (`BoxFile`, `write_box_file`, `read_box_file`, `BOX_EXTENSION`, `BOX_FORMAT`).
- **ui:** `material::own_boxes` (going inside, making, opening up, placing, the library), `material::own_rows` (Attributes), `material::own_shelf` (the shelf in the Material mode, now in the Material layout under the canvas); `run_with` adds own boxes and graph edits in one undo step; Alt + G, Shift + G and Alt + C / V carried out; double-click a box of yours opens it; `WorkbenchAction::SaveBoxFile` / `LoadBoxFile`, `FilePurpose::SaveBoxFile` / `LoadBoxFile`; your library kept with the app's settings (`own_boxes`).
- **Controls:** Alt + G, Shift + G and Alt + C / V as chosen with the plan; **double-click a box of yours** to see inside (as the milestone asks: "double-click or a button"; Open it is the button), Esc to come back out (Esc already closes things on the canvas).

## Consequences

- Graphs can be built from bigger parts and shared: the starter graphs (milestone 8) and scatter (milestone 9) can lean on own boxes.
- A saved Material layout from before this milestone is replaced once by the new one with the shelf.
- Opening an own box from the shelf (not through a placed box) shows its inside with nothing plugged in, so boxes needing an input say so.
- A paint layer's own values of a graph's shown settings are lost for boxes made into your own box (they are now its sliders); shown settings themselves carry over.
- From the model boxes can't go inside yet; letting them (working on the model's square inside) is left for later.
