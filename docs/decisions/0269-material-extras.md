# 0269. Material page extras

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-12

## Problem

After milestone 8 the project lead asked for a list of features that would make the material page nicer before building on, and for a way to resize a material quickly (the bricks of a starter four times smaller).

## Options

Sixteen ideas in four groups were offered: working on the graph, new kinds of input, looks for games, and checking and sharing. The project lead chose all of them. One, a guided tour, they asked to make a feature for every part of the app instead, planned for later.

## Decision

- **8b first:** a Tiling slider on graph materials and graph fill layers, and Transform and Repeat working on colours and whole materials.
- **Then, after milestone 15, milestones 16 to 29** in [docs/plans/phase-12.md](../plans/phase-12.md):
  - working on the graph: bypass and reroute dots; find and minimap; variations and the dice;
  - new inputs: project palettes; paint inside a graph; image to material; stamp images;
  - looks for games: stylised light boxes; pixel-art output; weathering set; trim sheets;
  - checking and sharing: material health check; preview options; library browser.
- **Guided tours for the whole app** go in DESIGN.md's later list: stepping through any part of the app with what each step does shown live.
- **Closing a tab stays mouse only** for now (the project lead).
- **Controls** for the extras are asked before each is built, unless the plan already names them.

## Consequences

Phase 12 grows from 15 milestones to 30 (8b plus 16 to 29). The material page gets much of what game artists use Substance Designer and Sampler for, in the app's friendlier way.
