# Keyboard shortcuts

> Every key and mouse action in one window: click **Keys** on the menu bar.

The app has a lot of keys, and it's easy to forget one, especially when coming back after a break. The **Keyboard shortcuts** window lists them all, grouped by what you're doing, and finds one as you type.

## When to use it

- You remember a tool but not its key.
- You want to know what a key does before pressing it.
- You're learning: leave it open beside the viewport.

## How to use it

1. Click **Keys** on the menu bar, right after **Help**.
2. Scroll through the groups, or type in **Find a key or action** at the top: "weld", "ctrl", "hide". Only the lines with every word you typed stay.
3. Click **Keys** again, or the window's ×, to close it.

The keys are shown the way your system writes them: **Ctrl** on Windows and Linux, **⌘** on a Mac. The list always matches what the keys really do: it comes from the same place the app reads them from.

## Settings

None. Keys can't be changed yet; custom keys are planned.

## Shortcuts

| Action | Shortcut |
|---|---|
| Open or close the list | Click **Keys** on the menu bar |

## Tips and common problems

- **Tip:** the window can be moved and resized; drag its corner to see more at once.
- **Problem:** typing in the search field does nothing in the viewport. **Why:** while you type there, keys go to the field, not the tools. **Fix:** click the viewport to use the keys again.
- **Problem:** a key doesn't work in some mode. **Why:** many keys belong to a mode: the modelling tools work in the Points, Edges and Polygons modes; copying objects in the Model mode. The group headings say which.
