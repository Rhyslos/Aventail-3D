# What exists now

A short list of what the app does today, area by area: one line per thing, with where to find it. It covers the `feature/curves` branch as of 2026-10-24 (Phase 10). How each thing works is in the [user manual](manual/SUMMARY.md); why it is so is in the [decision records](decisions/README.md); what comes next is in the [progress tracker](../README.md#progress-tracker) and [DESIGN.md](../DESIGN.md) §10.

Keep this page current: a pull request that adds or changes something a user would notice updates its line here (see [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md#documentation-rule)). Nothing goes here until it is built.

Keys are written as on Windows and Linux; on a Mac, Ctrl is Cmd. Menu paths use →.

## The window and panels

- **Splash screen:** the name, version and what the app is doing while it starts ([interface tour](manual/interface-tour.md#starting-the-app)).
- **Menu bar:** File, Edit, Create, Modes, Select, Tools, Mesh, UV, View, Window, Help, then **Keys**; the project's name and a red **Unsaved** badge on the right ([interface tour](manual/interface-tour.md)).
- **Layouts:** **Standard** and **UV Edit** (viewport and UV Editor side by side), tabs on the right of the menu bar; each keeps its own arrangement.
- **Command palette** (top row of icons): undo, redo, the select tool (with a list arrow: Box, Lasso, Paint select), Move, Scale, Rotate, axis locks, world or object axes, snapping, symmetry, the four blue shape buttons, group, and the command search button. Names show beside the main ones when the window is wide.
- **Mode palette** (left column): the six modes (Model, Points, Edges, Polygons, UV, Paint), **Edit pivot** under Model, **Select only visible** and the grid button; icons only, each named in its tooltip.
- **Viewport:** one view or four (F1 to F5), its own menu line (View, Cameras, Display, Options, Panel), the view dice and the shading button bottom left, the navigation picker bottom right ([navigating the view](manual/navigating-the-view.md)).
- **Modifiers palette** (thin column right of the viewport): **Add edit layer** ([edit layers](manual/edit-layers.md)).
- **Attributes panel:** the selection's settings (shape settings, Smoothing, Materials, Collision, a Levels of detail line, Last edit), changed live by dragging or typing ([attributes](manual/attributes.md)). Beside it, the **Levels** tab ([levels of detail](manual/levels-of-detail.md)).
- **Objects panel:** the object tree: select, rename, nest, reorder, hide, groups (Alt + G, Shift + G) ([objects](manual/objects.md)).
- **Edit layers tab** (beside Objects): appears for an object with more than one layer, or from **Window → Edit layers**.
- **Coordinates band** (under the viewport): position, size and rotation (X, Y, Z) on one line, typed or dragged ([transforming](manual/transforming.md#typing-exact-values)).
- **Status bar:** the mode, what is selected or under the mouse, and short messages.
- **Panels** dock and rearrange by their tabs; **View → Reset panel layout** brings the standard back.
- **Windows** (Export, Settings, Check for baking, About, questions) share one look: Enter presses the main button, Esc closes.
- **Looks:** always dark; one accent colour (Red as standard; Blue, Marine green, Plum, Olive, Petrol blue); Z is up, axes X red, Y blue, Z green.

## Viewing and navigating

- **Navigation:** as standard right drag orbits, middle drag pans, wheel zooms (Alt + mouse too); four other schemes from the bottom-right button or Settings ([navigating the view](manual/navigating-the-view.md)).
- **Orbit and zoom around the point under the mouse** (switchable in Settings).
- **Frame:** H frames everything, Z the selection.
- **Views:** F1 perspective, F2 top, F3 right, F4 front, F5 all four; middle click switches one or four; **P** perspective or orthographic.
- **View dice:** click a face, edge or corner to look from there; Ctrl + click for the opposite side; Ctrl while orbiting snaps to its views.
- **Shading:** Shaded, Shaded with wires, Wireframe (bottom-left button or **Display**) ([how shapes are drawn](manual/viewport-display.md)).
- **Display menu:** Cavity (on as standard), Show hard edges, Show seams, Show normals, Show collision (every object's collision shapes outlined, not only the selected; [collision shapes](manual/collision-shapes.md)), Show triangles (faint lines inside every shown shape's polygons where the export cuts them into triangles; [triangles](manual/triangulate.md)), and **Surface**: Colours, Checker, Numbered checker (always blue to green), Stretch.
- **Grid:** lines every 1 m, 50 cm or 10 cm (left palette button).
- **Hide and isolate:** Shift + H hide, Ctrl + H isolate, Alt + H show everything; also **View → Show and hide** ([hide and isolate](manual/hide-and-isolate.md)).

## Modes and selecting

- **Five modes:** Model, Points (1), Edges (2), Polygons (3), UV; the same key again goes back to Model; **Tab** goes straight to Model and puts a tool away ([modes](manual/modes.md)).
- **Edit pivot:** a switch in the Model mode (button under Model, **Tools → Edit pivot**, no key) to move and turn pivots instead of objects ([pivots](manual/pivots.md)).
- **Click, box, lasso, Paint select** (brush); Q steps through Box, Lasso, Paint select ([selecting](manual/selecting.md)).
- **Selection keys:** Ctrl adds, Shift removes (standard; three other patterns in Settings).
- **Loops and rings:** double-click, Alt + double-click; a loop runs round an n-gon's rim (a cylinder's cap).
- **Grow and shrink:** Ctrl + wheel, or the Select menu.
- **Select all, invert, deselect:** Ctrl + A, Ctrl + I, Esc.
- **Select connected:** Alt + A (over the UV Editor, or in the UV mode: whole UV pieces).
- **Select menu:** Select flipped, Select same facing, Select hard edges, Select by material, Select seams (UV menu).
- **Select only visible:** on as standard (left palette, Settings).
- **The selection is drawn last, thickest, in the accent colour;** **Edit → Settings → Interface → High contrast** makes it bigger with a dark rim.

## Moving, rotating and scaling

- **Gizmos:** W move, R rotate, S scale, T all three; Q back to selecting ([transforming](manual/transforming.md)).
- **While dragging:** Shift ten times finer, Ctrl snaps (or not), Esc or right click cancels.
- **Axis locks** Shift + X / Y / Z; **L** world or object axes.
- **Snapping:** to points, edges, polygons and grid lines (or the project's kit grid), with turn and scale steps (palette button, Settings).
- **Slide:** Alt + drag the Move gizmo slides points along their edges.
- **Typed values** in the Coordinates band.
- **Pivot commands:** Pivot to world origin, Pivot to object…, Object to world origin, and **Pivot presets** (bottom centre, bottom corner, centre, top centre; [modular kit helpers](manual/modular-kits.md)) (Tools menu, or E with Edit pivot on).
- **Snap to and Align:** **Tools → Snap to…** and **Tools → Align…**, then click the target ([snap to and align](manual/snap-to-and-align.md)).
- **Apply transforms:** **Mesh → Shape and object → Apply transforms**, or Apply rotation and scale in E (Model mode) ([apply transforms](manual/apply-transforms.md)).
- **Units:** metres or centimetres per project (Attributes → Units, or Settings).

## Creating shapes

Every shape stands on the ground with its pivot at the middle of its base, keeps its settings in the Attributes panel, and comes with UVs, seams and hard edges where it should ([primitives](manual/primitives.md)).

- **B list, the seven basic shapes:** Cube, Plane, Cylinder, Sphere, Disc, Tube, Cone. B opens it and moves down, Shift + B up, Enter or Space creates, Esc closes.
- **Alt + B list, the eleven others:** Icosphere, Torus, Capsule, Dome, Pipe elbow, Pie sector, Gear, Hex grid, Hex sphere, Stairs, Arch, then the curves: **Draw curve**, **Line**, **Circle**, **Arc**, **Spiral**. Once open, B moves down this list too; Shift + Alt + B opens it at the bottom.
- **Create menu:** all 18, with a line between the two groups, the curves after another line, **Group**, and **Modular kit**: the project's **Kit grid** (1 m, 2 m, 4 m or typed), **Kit-sized shapes**, **Size to kit grid** and **Snap moves to the kit grid** ([modular kit helpers](manual/modular-kits.md)).
- **Palette buttons:** cube, cylinder, plane, sphere.
- **Bake into shape** (Ctrl + E): turns a shape into a plain mesh without settings.

## Modelling tools

In the Points, Edges and Polygons modes. Every tool is also in the tool menu (**E**), the **Mesh** menu (in groups: Build, Cut and join, Remove, Shading, Shape and object) and command search.

- **Extrude** (D): drag to push polygons out; border edges and points along an open edge grow new polygons; a selection all round a polygon pushes it out; Direction, End size, Steps, Flat end ([extrude and inset](manual/extrude-and-inset.md)).
- **Pipe bends and spirals:** Extrude's Angle, Direction, Bend radius and Segments; past 360°, a spiral with Rise per turn.
- **Inset** (I): drag towards the polygons' middle; Keep together or each on its own.
- **Bevel** (V): edges and points; width, segments, profile, even width, skew, stop at overlaps, weld points ([bevel](manual/bevel.md)).
- **Groove** (K, Edges mode): cut a thin line into a surface or raise a ridge ([groove](manual/groove.md)).
- **Loop cut** (F): a new loop of edges, shown before the click, slid by dragging ([loop cut](manual/loop-cut.md)).
- **Slit** (X): cut along a line of clicked points, visible only or through; close the line to cut a shape in; Enter or Tab finishes, Esc cancels ([slit](manual/slit.md)).
- **Connect** (A), **Bridge** (C, or drag edge to edge), **Weld** (M at the middle, Ctrl + M drag onto a point; in the Polygons mode, each group of polygons melts at its middle) ([connect, bridge and weld](manual/connect-bridge-weld.md)).
- **Delete** (Delete) and **Dissolve** (Backspace) points, edges or polygons; **Fill hole** (J); **Flip normals**; **Fix normals** (Mesh → Shading) ([clean-up](manual/clean-up.md)).
- **Ctrl + wheel** changes the count of the tool in use: Bevel's segments, Loop cut's cuts, Extrude's steps or bend segments, Bridge's segments.
- **Last edit:** the newest edit's settings stay changeable in the Attributes panel.
- **Split off** (Mesh → Shape and object) and **Join objects** (E in Model mode, or Mesh) ([split and join](manual/split-and-join.md)).
- **Copies and instances:** Ctrl + C, Ctrl + V paste copies, Alt + V paste instances; Duplicate and Duplicate as instance in E ([copies and instances](manual/copies-and-instances.md)).
- **Booleans** (Mesh → Booleans, the modifiers palette, or command search; no keys): select the shape to keep, Ctrl + click the cutters, then **Union**, **Subtract** or **Intersect**. Cutters stay objects, drawn as outlines; move or edit one and the cut follows; hidden ones still cut; Attributes → **Booleans** changes the kind or takes a cutter out; **Bake into shape** makes it permanent and hides the cutters. Closed solids only: anything else is left uncut with the reason shown ([booleans](manual/booleans.md)).
- **Modifiers** (the modifiers palette, Mesh → Modifiers, or command search; no keys): added to the selected shapes, worked out live after the edits, booleans and mirror, first to last; Attributes → **Modifiers** switches each on or off, moves it Up or Down, removes it, changes its settings and **Show in viewport** (off: skipped in the viewport, still exported and baked). Exports carry those that are on; **Bake into shape** makes them permanent, with the mirror ([modifiers](manual/modifiers.md)).
- **Remesh** (a modifier): a closed shape rebuilt as an even surface of quads, **Voxel size** 2 cm and **Smooth** 2 passes as standard, at most 2 million voxels; materials kept, box-projected UVs with seams; open shapes left as they are with the reason shown ([remesh](manual/remesh.md)).
- **Subdivide** (a modifier): Catmull-Clark subdivision smoothing, **Levels** 1 in the viewport and **Levels for export** 2 (0 to 5, at most 4 million polygons), **Hard edges stay sharp** on objects shaded by their marked edges; UVs smoothed keeping corners, materials and seams kept, open borders and sheet corners kept ([subdivide](manual/subdivide.md)).
- **Lattice** (a modifier with its own object): **Add lattice** fits a cage of points round the selected shapes (**Divisions** 2 × 2 × 2 as standard, up to 16 a side; **Interpolation** Smooth or Linear; **Reset points**); move its points in the Points mode (selecting, gizmo, Coordinates) and the shapes bend smoothly; the cage is drawn as lines, picked by them, and left out of exports; deleting it removes its modifiers in one step ([lattice](manual/lattice.md)).
- **Scatter onto surface** (a modifier; the surface selected first, the shapes to scatter Ctrl + clicked after): copies of the shapes on the surface's **Points**, **Polygon centres** or **Random over the surface** (**Count** 50), with **Seed**, **Spacing**, **Align to surface** (on), **Random turn**, **Smallest/Largest size**, **Offset** and **On polygons** (**Use selected**, **Whole surface**); the shapes stay objects (hidden, they still scatter), copies stand on their pivot, materials matched by name; exported as one mesh with the surface ([scatter](manual/scatter.md)).
- **Bend along curve** (a modifier; the shape selected first, a curve Ctrl + clicked after): the shape laid along the live curve from its start, through its pivot, on the sweep's frames, with **Axis** (Longest side, X, Y, Z), **Stretch to fit**, **Offset** and **Twist** ([bend along curve](manual/bend-along-curve.md)).
- **Shrinkwrap onto** (a modifier, **Shrinkwrap**; the shape selected first, the target Ctrl + clicked after): the shape's points moved onto the target's surface, **Mode** Nearest surface point, Along the shape's normals or Along X/Y/Z, with **Offset** and **Points** (**Use selected**, **All points**) ([shrinkwrap](manual/shrinkwrap.md)).
- **Displace** (a modifier, family Deform, after Shrinkwrap onto; the modifiers palette, Mesh → Modifiers, command search; no keys): the shape's points pushed by a hand-written noise that is the same for the same settings on every computer, along the normals (standard) or the shape's X, Y or Z, with a **Noise** (**Smooth**, both ways; **Ridged**, sharp crests, and **Cells**, rounded cells with grooves, outward only), **Strength** (5 cm, negative for the other way), **Scale** (25 cm), **Detail** (3 layers, 1 to 8), **Seed** (1), **Points** (all, or those chosen with **Use selected** in the Points mode) and **Falloff** (20 cm, along the surface, round the chosen points). Only points move: UVs, seams and materials stay. A new Displace goes before Weighted normals.
- **Bend, twist or taper** (a modifier, family Deform, after Displace; the modifiers palette, Mesh → Modifiers, command search; no keys): one modifier with a **Kind**: **Bend** (the shape curled into an arc through an **Angle**, 45° standard, towards a **Direction** round the axis), **Twist** (each slice turned round the axis, an **Angle** in all) or **Taper** (each slice scaled across the axis, **Amount** −50% one limit's length from the centre, **Curve** bulging the middle); along the shape's **Axis** X, Y or Z (Z standard), from its pivot moved by **Centre X/Y/Z**, between **Limit from** and **Limit to** (shares of the shape's length along the axis, 0% to 100% standard; past them the shape carries on rigidly; the centre brought within them stays still). Named by its kind in the list. Only points move. While the shape is selected the viewport draws a helper: the axis as it bends, a cross at each limit and the centre. A new one goes before Weighted normals.
- **Decimate** (a modifier): fewer triangles with the shape kept, by quadric error collapse; **Reduce by** a **Ratio** (50% as standard) or **Target triangles**; **Keep UV seams**, **Keep hard edges** (as the shading shows them), **Keep material borders** and **Keep open borders** (all on), a kept line's points sliding only along it and its corners staying; triangles out, or **Quads where flat**; UVs, materials, hard edges, seams and creases carried; **Triangles shown** in the Attributes panel ([decimate](manual/decimate.md)).
- **Weighted normals** (a modifier, best last): shading where bigger polygons count for more, so big flat faces shade flat and bevels round; **Weight by** Polygon area (standard), Corner angle or Area and angle, **Face influence** 75%, **Strength** 100%, **Keep hard edges** (on, by the object's Smoothing); stored as custom normals per corner, drawn, exported (glTF normals and tangents, OBJ normals) and kept by Bake into shape; a modifier after it, or an edit that moves points after baking, drops them, with an amber note in Attributes (grey, saying what it would do, while that modifier is off); every kind that changes the shape goes before it when added ([weighted normals](manual/weighted-normals.md), [decision 0164](decisions/0164-interface-fixes-from-the-phase-10-testers.md)).
- **Live bevel** (a modifier, family Generate; added before any Subdivide or Weighted normals): the improved bevel on the edges chosen by **Edges**: **By angle** (sharper than **Angle**, 30° standard), **Hard edges** (as the object's Smoothing shows them) or **Marked edges** (by **Bevel weight**, each that share of the width); **Width** 2 cm, **Segments** 2 (1 to 64), **Profile** 100% (round), **Stop at neighbours** on; even width and welding on; refused with a reason when there's nothing to bevel; worked out on another thread from about 1 200 bevelled edges ([live bevel](manual/live-bevel.md)).
- **Quadify** (a modifier, family Clean up; added before any Live bevel, Subdivide or Weighted normals) and **Quadify now** (once, a recorded edit: **Mesh → Clean up**, command search; the selected polygons in the Polygons mode, whole shapes in the Model mode; settings under Last edit, remembered for the next): pairs of triangles joined into quads, the squarest first; **Max angle** (40°) between the two triangles, **Max shape distortion** (40°, a corner's distance from a right angle), no dents; **Keep UV seams**, **Keep hard edges** (as the object's Smoothing shows them), **Keep material borders**, all on; creased and bevel-weighted edges always stay; no keys ([quadify](manual/quadify.md)).
- **Weld by distance** (a modifier, family Clean up; added before any Decimate, Quadify, Live bevel, Subdivide or Weighted normals) and **Weld by distance now** (once, a recorded edit: **Mesh → Clean up**, command search; whole shapes in the Model mode, the selected points, or those of the selected edges or polygons, in the other modes; settings under Last edit, remembered for the next): points closer together than **Distance** (0.1 mm, up to 1 m, shown in millimetres) melted into one, the lowest-numbered point staying put, never along a chain; corners keep their UVs; **Only at open borders** (off), **Keep UV seams** (on: joins where the UVs split marked as seams); no keys ([weld by distance](manual/weld-by-distance.md)).
- **Array** (a modifier, family Generate; the modifiers palette, Mesh → Modifiers, command search; no keys): copies of the shape, the first the shape itself, in a **Line** (**Count** 3, an **Offset** step X/Y/Z), a **Grid** (**Count X/Y/Z** 3 × 3 × 1, a **Spacing** per axis), offsets **Relative to size** (on: 1 = touching) or distances, or **Radial** (**Count** 8 over an **Angle** of 360°, a whole turn shared out evenly, less a fan with copies at both ends; round the shape's **Axis** X, Y or Z (Z standard) through its pivot or a **Centre** object picked from a list, live; a **Radius**, 0 standard); **Merge touching copies** (off; Weld by distance on open borders, **Merge distance** 0.1 mm); **UVs side by side** (off: each copy's UVs in a square of their own). At most 100 000 copies. Symmetry is the mirror generator ([decision 0153](decisions/0153-array.md)).
- **Shell** (a modifier, family Generate, after Array; the modifiers palette, Mesh → Modifiers, command search; no keys): the surface given thickness, the surface itself keeping its ids and a copy facing the other way, with a **Thickness** (1 cm), a **Direction** (**Inward** standard: the surface stays the outside; **Outward**; **Both sides**), **Even thickness** (on: each point moved so every polygon round it ends up the thickness away, at most 5 times it) and **Fill the rims** (on: a quad per open border edge joining the sides, its edges along the sides hard and seams; with UVs, each rim strip a row of its own above the texture square at the shape's texel density; the back side shares the surface's UVs). Added before any Live bevel, Subdivide or Weighted normals; self-intersections not fixed ([decision 0154](decisions/0154-shell.md)).
- **Struts** (a modifier, family Generate, after Shell; the modifiers palette, Mesh → Modifiers, command search; no keys; named apart from the viewport's Wireframe look): every edge a closed prism (a **strut**) and every point used by a polygon a closed faceted ball (a **joint**, its faces the struts' corner radius away, covering their ends), the pieces overlapping, not welded; **Thickness** (2 cm, the struts' width), **Sides** (4, from 3 to 24; a flat facing the way the edge's first polygon faces; a joint's rings along its first polygon's facing, a flat towards its first edge), **Even thickness** (on: measured across the flats; off: across the corners) and **Keep the faces** (off; on, the shape's polygons stay with their ids and UVs). Profiles of 6 sides or fewer have hard corners (and hard joints); every strut's ends are hard. Each strut and joint is a UV island (sides unrolled in rows, ends hung off them, winding kept), all at one texel density, shelf-packed into the texture square (the square above it with Keep the faces), seams round each; materials from the edge's or point's first polygon. Added before any Live bevel, Subdivide or Weighted normals and after Weld by distance and Quadify ([decision 0159](decisions/0159-struts.md)).
- **Triangulate** (a modifier, family Game ready, after Weighted normals; the modifiers palette, Mesh → Modifiers, command search; no keys; every other kind added later goes before it): every polygon with four or more corners (or, with **Quads only**, every quad) cut into triangles; **Method** **Beauty** (standard: the export's cut, then lines between flat triangles turned to the other diagonal where that widens the smallest corner; bent polygons keep their fold) or **Fixed** (exactly the export's cut); each polygon's first triangle keeps its id; corner values, materials and edge marks kept; carries custom normals (Weighted normals before it reach the export); all triangles already, or no quads with Quads only, shown as it is with a grey "Nothing to do" note ([triangles](manual/triangulate.md)).
- **Bevel weights** (Edges mode; no keys): a **Bevel weight** field (0 to 1) beside Crease in the Attributes panel's Selected edges section, a recorded edit (one drag, one edit and undo step; mirrored with symmetry); drawn lime green while editing ([live bevel](manual/live-bevel.md#marking-edges-with-a-bevel-weight)).
- **Creases** (Edges mode; no keys): **Crease edges** and **Uncrease** in the E ring's **Creases** group (Flip normals' place), Mesh → Shading and command search; the Attributes panel's **Crease** field (0 to 1) for the selected edges; drawn magenta while editing ([subdivide](manual/subdivide.md#keeping-edges-sharp-creases)).
- **Curves** (Create, Alt + B, command search; no keys): **Draw curve** (click a sharp point, press and drag a smooth one; Enter or right click finishes, Esc cancels, Backspace takes back, the first point closes; on the ground or a flat view's plane, snapping when on) or ready-made Line, Circle, Arc, Spiral. Attributes → **Makes**: Line only, **Sweep** (circle, square, rectangle or another curve as the cross-section; width, height, sides, segments, adaptive, twist, caps) or **Lathe** (axis, spin angle, segments round, caps), live. Points mode: points and handles selected and moved like a mesh's; double-click switches sharp and smooth; Delete takes points away; Closed in Attributes. Bake into shape makes a mesh; a bare line exports as nothing ([curves](manual/curves.md)).
- **Spline editing** (Points mode, curves selected): double-click the line adds a point keeping the shape; Delete keeps the shape near; **M** / **A** on two end points weld them at the middle / join them with a straight stretch (one curve: closes it); **Join objects** joins curves end to end; Draw curve carries a selected curve on from either end (click the end first). **E** shows the curve ring; **Mesh → Curve** and command search hold **Split curve**, **Reverse curve**, **Open or close curve**, handle types (**Sharp**, **Smooth**, **Broken**, **Auto**), **Round corners** (radius) and **Even spacing** (count); Attributes → **Curve points** has the handle type, radius and count ([curves](manual/curves.md)).
- **Heavy shapes:** a 3-million-polygon object stays usable; **Help → Developer → Add a heavy test shape** to try it ([heavy shapes](manual/heavy-shapes.md)).

## The edit history and edit layers

- **Undo and redo:** Ctrl + Z, Ctrl + Y (or Ctrl + Shift + Z), for everything; limited by memory (2 GB as standard), not by steps.
- **Every edit is recorded on top of the shape,** so a primitive's settings stay editable afterwards ([concepts](manual/concepts.md#objects-and-scenes)).
- **Drags in a row** of the same points make one edit (each still its own undo step).
- **The edit list** (Edit layers tab): click any edit to change it in Attributes; switch it off, **Bake into shape up to here**, or **Delete edit** ([edit list](manual/edit-list.md)).
- **Edit layers:** **Add edit layer** (modifiers palette, Mesh → Shape and object, or the panel); switch off, rename, move up or down, merge down, delete ([edit layers](manual/edit-layers.md)).
- **Edits find their parts again** when an earlier edit or a shape's settings change; ones that can't say **parts missing**.

## Smoothing and symmetry

- **Smoothing per object:** Attributes → **Smoothing**: By angle (30° as standard) or marked edges only; **Mesh → Shading → Smooth by angle** for several objects ([hard and soft edges](manual/hard-and-soft-edges.md)).
- **Mark hard edges, Mark soft edges** (E or Mesh → Shading); on an object smoothed by angle, the first mark keeps its look and switches it to marks, in one undo step.
- **Hard edges by angle** (Mesh → Shading): marks every edge from an angle, changeable under Last edit.
- **Symmetry per object:** the **Symmetry** palette button or **Tools → Symmetry** gives the object a live mirror at its pivot, moving and turning with it; the arrow beside it sets the axes, the mirror's offset and the weld distance ([symmetry](manual/symmetry.md)).
- **Apply mirror** (Symmetry menu, Mesh → Shape and object, or command search): makes the mirrored half real and switches symmetry off, in one undo step.

## UVs

The UV tools are in the **UV** menu (main menu bar, and the **UV** button on the UV Editor's menu line), in **E** over the UV Editor or in the UV mode, and in command search.

- **UV Editor:** the selected objects' UVs on the texture square, one selection with the viewport; **UV Edit** layout or **Window → UV Editor**; menus UV, Texture, Trim, Snapshots, View ([UV Editor](manual/uv-editor.md)).
- **UV mode:** a click selects a whole piece, in the editor or on the model.
- **Editing by hand:** W, R, S, T gizmo in the editor; **Turn & flip** in E ([editing UVs](manual/editing-uvs.md)).
- **Editing on the model:** in the UV mode, W, R, S and drag in the viewport ([UVs on the model](manual/uvs-on-the-model.md)).
- **Seams:** Mark seams, Clear seams (E), Seams from hard edges, Suggest seams, Select seams (UV → Seams); shown in orange ([seams](manual/seams.md)).
- **Seam preferences:** Prefer seams here, Never seams here (UV → Seams); **stretch budgets** (UV → Pieces) ([seam preferences](manual/seam-preferences.md)).
- **Unwrap, Relax, Straighten** (E or UV → Lay out); live unwrap as seams change; Unfold across only, Unfold up only; four methods ([unwrapping](manual/unwrapping.md)).
- **Pins:** Pin, Unpin (E or UV → Pieces), no key ([pins](manual/pins.md)).
- **Locked pieces and piece settings** (UV → Pieces) ([locked pieces](manual/locked-pieces.md)).
- **Texture size and texel density:** the editor's **Texture** menu; Set and Match texel density (UV → Lay out) ([texture and density](manual/texture-and-density.md)).
- **Pack** (E or UV → Lay out): padding in real pixels, nesting, texture sets per material or object, stacking identical pieces ([packing](manual/packing.md)).
- **Settings of every automatic tool:** UV → Settings, and the **UV settings** panel in the UV Edit layout; same model and settings always give the same UVs ([UV settings](manual/uv-settings.md)).
- **Checks while you work:** overlapping (red), mirrored (purple) and outside-the-square (amber) pieces counted; **View → Numbers on pieces**, **View → Measure** ([seeing and checking](manual/seeing-and-checking.md)).
- **View → Explain choices:** every seam and piece says why it's there and how to change it ([why is this here?](manual/why-is-this-here.md)).
- **Checker and Stretch views** on the model and in the editor ([checker and stretch](manual/checker-and-stretch.md)).
- **UVs survive modelling:** new polygons get fitting UVs; hand work is kept ([UVs and modelling](manual/uvs-and-modelling.md)).

## Painting

- **Paint mode** (mode palette, the brush under UV): a left drag paints the texture set of the material under the brush, into the layer chosen in Paint layers (else its top paint layer), with no seam joins and hidden parts left alone; one undo step a stroke; the Painted surface while in it. Brush in Attributes: size (on screen or in centimetres on the surface, warning below a texture pixel), flow, hardness, spacing, colour, paint through. Keys (Substance Painter's): 1 paint, 2 erase, [ ] size, Ctrl + right drag size and hardness, Ctrl + left drag flow, Esc leaves. Each stroke's dabs are kept with its layer. In the UV Editor the same brush paints flat, only inside the UV pieces of the material under it ([painting textures](manual/painting-textures.md)).
- **Paint tab** (`Panel::Paint`, decision 0213; Paint layout, a tab in front of Attributes): the paint brush's settings (Paint on, tip, size, flow, hardness, spacing, realism rows, mirror, pen and smoothing, effect rows, polygon fill, colour); outside Paint mode a note. While a layout has it, Attributes shows only the selected object in Paint mode. A saved Paint layout without it gets it beside Attributes, in front.
- **Show in the views** (decision 0213, `attributes::channel_view`): the Paint tab's first row: All, Colour, and with the texture set's realism channels Rough, Metal, Height, Glow (accessible names "Show …"). Each sets the paint preview (Colour: Unlit; the rest: Roughness, Metal, Height, Glow only); All puts back the preview in use before. The lit button follows the preview (any other preview chosen elsewhere: All). New previews **Height only** (greys, 0.5 flat) and **Glow only** (the glow on black), shader modes 10 and 11.
- **Colours tab** (decision 0213, `colour_shelf`): the brush shelf (Panel::Brushes) has Brushes | Colours tabs. Colours: **Used lately** (the brush or fill colour at each stroke start, newest first, no repeats, at most 16; kept by the app between sessions, key `used_colours`), then the palette list (New, Import…, Export…, Delete), **+** (add the brush colour), the palette's colours (click: paint with it; double-click: paint with it and open the brush bar's picker, switching to the paint brush if needed; right click: remove). With the brush bar, the picker pop-up has no palettes.
- **Paint tools row** (decision 0213, `paint_tools`): in Paint mode, a floating row along the viewport's bottom between the Shading and navigation buttons (above the Shading button when the view is too narrow): paint brush, eraser, projection, polygon fill, smudge, clone (icons drawn for the app), the one in use in the accent fill; a click does what keys 1–6 do; never paints.
- **Brush bar** (decision 0213, `paint_bar`): in Paint mode, floating groups over the viewport's top left under the view's name, in the viewport buttons' look: the colour square (brush or polygon fill colour; not for the eraser and effect brushes; click: the colour picker as a pop-up, closed by a click outside), the tool's name; for brushes Size (px, or cm on the surface), Flow, Hardness, Spacing as drag fields (type to set), and Mirror (on/off, axis arrow), Pen (pressure → size on/off), Smoothing (on/off). While shown, the Paint tab leaves out size, flow, hardness, spacing, mirror and the colour picker. A click on it never paints. While painting, the UV Editor draws seams grey (`UV_PAINT_SEAM`), not orange.
- **Shared paint note** (decision 0214): while painting one selected shape, Paint layers warns (yellow) when other shapes with one of its materials have UV pieces over its own (check_sheet, 256 cells; not its own overlaps, trim pieces or instances), naming them; **Give it its own texture** (material renamed "Material – Object", unused, set copied; one step) and **Pack them onto one texture** (Pack of all their pieces by material; one step).
- **Paint layers panel** (Paint layout, under Attributes): a material's layers top first: paint and fill layers and groups (nested), eye, rename, opacity, blend mode (Normal, Multiply, Screen, Overlay, Add, Subtract, Colour, Hue), clip to the layer below, + Paint / + Fill / + Group, Up, Down, Duplicate, Merge down, Group / Ungroup, Delete; one undo step per change. The chosen layer is the brush's.
- **Realism maps in exports** (decision 0208): when a set uses realism, glTF gets an ORM (R occlusion 255, G roughness, B metal) as `metallicRoughnessTexture` (factors 1) and, if a layer glows, an emissive map (`emissiveFactor` 1); Export → Realism maps (shown when such a set goes out; remembered; Unity preset: Unity mask map): In the file (OBJ: ORM beside), ORM beside it, Unity mask map beside it (R metal, G occlusion 255, B 0, A 255 − roughness), Separate maps beside it (`_Roughness`, `_Metallic` greys); files `stem_Material_ORM.png` etc., padded; OBJ `.mtl`: `map_Pr`/`map_Pm` (separate, or `-imfchan g`/`b` of the ORM), `Ke 1 1 1` and `map_Ke`; the texture budget lists each map.
- **Live engine preview** (Export → Game project → While painting: Update the game live; `ExportTarget::live`, off as standard, format 82; decision 0212): a change to any texture set is exported into the game project folder (the Export to game project request) once it has rested 0.8 s, not during a stroke or file work, not again while unchanged; painting only.
- **Height and normal maps** (decision 0211): `Channel::Height` (grey; 128 level; full range `HEIGHT_DEPTH` 0.5 cm), a brush, fill tool and fill layer row (off; 75% when ticked); packed in the views' surface map blue; screen-space bump mapping of the surface normal in every painted preview; export: a tangent-space normal map from the mixed height over level (slope from neighbours, texel size from the texel density), `normalTexture` inside glTF (green up), beside OBJ (`norm`) and beside glTF when Realism maps isn't In the file; Export → Normal maps: Green up (OpenGL) / Green down (DirectX) for files beside (Unreal Engine preset: down, ORM beside); format 81.
- **Material layers** (Paint layers → + Material; decision 0210): `Layer::material` (Brushed metal, Wood, Stone, Paint coat; Colour, Second colour, Scale 1–1000 cm, Sharpness, Roughness and Metal with realism, New pattern), made like gradients from noise projected along X, Y and Z weighted by |normal|^(1+15·sharpness); roughness and metal channel parts made with realism on; not painted into; format 80.
- **Realism channels** (Paint layers → Realism channels, per texture set; decision 0207): Roughness, Metal (grey 0–100%) and Glow (colour) per layer, painted or a fill value, standard 80% / 0% / none; mixed by layer order, opacity, masks, clipping and visibility with the Normal blend. Brush and polygon fill: Roughness, Metal, Glow rows (tick and value; 50% and 0% ticked as standard), painted with each colour dab; the eraser erases every channel the layer has; effect brushes and masks colour only. Fill layers: the same rows. Merge down mixes channels; channels follow the model and resize as pictures. Paint preview: Realistic (GGX sun turned by Turn the light 0–360°, sky light and reflection, glow, Filmic), Roughness only, Metal only; Godot adds glow. Format 78.
- **Presets and starting layouts** (Paint layers, brush bar; decision 0206): with no layers, Start with a layout: Hand-painted (Base colour, Light and shade, Edge highlight, Paint) or Realistic (Base colour, Cavity dirt, Edge wear, Dust, Paint), one step, top chosen. Presets row: Save style… (chosen layer or group), Save material… (colour and layers), Load preset… (a style goes above the chosen layer, a material replaces colour and layers, brushes join your own; one step). Brush bar: Save brushes…, Load brushes…. `.paintpreset` files: JSON with `format` 1 and `kind` brushes / style / material; recipes without pixels or strokes; at most 4 MB; newer formats refused.
- **Masks** (Paint layers): Add white / black mask, Invert mask, Remove mask; the mask square on the row chooses it to paint on (white shows, black hides, by the paint's lightness).
- **Polygon fill** (key 4): a click fills the polygon, UV piece or material under it (Attributes: Fill, Erase, Colour), on the model or in the UV Editor, on the chosen layer or mask; a drag fills more in one undo step.
- **Brush bar** (Paint layout, in Coordinates' place with Coordinates behind it): cards with a sample stroke and name; built-in Soft round, Hard round, Chalk, Bristle, Dry brush, Spatter; All / Built in / Saved; search; + keeps the brush (tip, hardness, spacing, flow, pen curves) under a name, kept by the app; right click deletes one's own. Choosing keeps size and colour. **Tip** in the Paint tab: Round, Chalk, Bristle, Dry brush, Spatter.
- **Gradient fills** (Paint layers → + Gradient): From and To colours, along X, Y or Z (bottom to top standard), Starts and Ends (0–100% of the box), Across each object, part or UV piece (format 75); made on the shapes, again when changed or when shapes or texture size change; strokes go on a layer above; format 72.
- **Smart masks** (Paint layers → Add smart mask): Edges, Cavities, Facing up, Height, Wear (edges cut by fine noise into chips), Dirt (creases and the lower part, Reach), Rust (blotches from edges and creases plus streaks down the sides, Spread; format 79); with realism on, + Dirt (fill 62,50,38 at 75%, roughness 95%), + Wear ("Worn metal" 176,176,182, roughness 35%, metal 100%), + Rust (118,52,24 at 90%, roughness 90%); Width/Reach/Level, Softness, Noise, Noise size (0–100%), New pattern; black base; made on the shapes, again when changed or when shapes or texture size change; not painted into; format 73.
- **Light and shade** (Paint layers → + Light and shade): Sun around (0–360°), Sun height (0–90°), Light and Shade colours, Warm and cool (0–100%, format 75), Softness (0–100%), Cast shadows; Overlay at 60%; made on the shapes, again when changed or when shapes or texture size change; not painted into; format 74.
- **Edge highlights** (Paint layers → + Edge highlight): fill [255, 246, 225], Screen at 70%, smart mask Edges (width 12%, softness 35%, noise 15%, noise size 5%); no format change.
- **Paint previews** (shading button list and Display menu, Painted surface only): Lit (studio light), Unlit, Toon (hard band from the key light, shade ×(0.50, 0.53, 0.62), rim light), Godot (sun 1, sky ambient, Filmic tone mapping), Godot toon (the same with a toon step, roughness 0.1), Value check (Rec. 709 luminance grey, unlit), Squint (unlit, sampled 16× blurrier); cavity off but for Lit; remembered with the viewport settings.
- **See it as the player will** (shading button list and Display menu): each view drawn at `game px per cm ÷ view px per cm` of its size (at most 1, at least 1/128), game px per cm = screen height ÷ (2 × distance × tan(angle ÷ 2)), view's from the camera's distance to its focus (or a flat view's height); enlarged with nearest filtering; Distance (0.5–200 m, 5 standard), Screen (720p, 1080p standard, 1440p, 2160p), View angle (20–120°, 60 standard); a note at the top of each view; remembered.
- **Outlines** (Attributes → Outline, per object): Width 0.05–50 cm (1 standard), Colour (warm near-black [28, 22, 26] standard); viewport: inverted hull from area-weighted point directions, pushed out in the world, outside faces discarded; export: `Name-outline` shell beside the object (reversed polygons, inward normals, material `outline-rrggbb`), glTF and OBJ, counted in the summary; Godot glTF exports add `name-outline.gdshader` (unshaded, cull_front, width in metres); format 76.
- **Painting trim sheets live**: strokes, fills, gradients, smart masks, light and shade and bakes cover UVs past the square where the texture repeats (each square a triangle reaches, at most 16 each way); a stroke or fill keeps to the trim row its start lies in (Attributes → Trim row: Stay in the row, on as standard, shown while there are rows); objects sharing a material share its texture set.
- **Why does this look wrong?** (Paint layers button, command search): per object and material, seams whose sides differ by over 16% of the colour range at most of three samples; polygons stretched over 2× one way; polygons under half the aimed px/m (Fix: the smallest texture size reaching it, up to 4096); lightness spread (10th to 90th percentile) under 12% with 16+ samples (Fix: a Light and shade layer); Show picks the polygons (Polygons mode; Stretch view for stretched pixels); looks again on any change while open.
- **Texture export** (Export window → Textures: PNG standard, TGA, Leave out; Padding 0–64 px, 8 standard; remembered): each exported painted material's layers mixed over its colour (opaque); padded ring by ring past UV coverage (repeats counted); glTF: PNG in the BIN chunk, `baseColorTexture`, factor white, sampler linear/mipmapped/repeat; OBJ: `stem_Material.png|tga` beside, `map_Kd`, `Kd 1 1 1`; kit pieces and export on save too; texture budget: size × size × 4 × 4/3 bytes each, and the total.
- **Vertex colour painting** (Attributes → Paint on: Vertex colours, Paint mode): corner layer `colour` (Vec4, sRGB + alpha, white default); a stroke paints corners within the brush (world radius from screen size or surface size, pen pressure), facing the camera unless Paint through, mixed toward the colour by hardness falloff × flow (eraser: toward white); fill tool fills polygon / piece / material corners; From the texture sets every corner of the selected objects to the mixed texture at its UV over the material colour (one step); one recorded `PaintColours` edit per stroke, replaced as it grows; Surface → Vertex colours (colour over material by alpha); glTF `COLOR_0` linear VEC4; format 77.
- **Guides** (click a map in Bakes): shown over the paint in the views (occlusion Multiply, curvature Overlay, others Normal; Strength); Add as a layer (above the chosen, same blend); Use as the layer's mask.
- **Bakes** (Paint layers → Bakes → Bake maps; Draft, Good, Best): position, normals, occlusion, curvature, ID and thickness from the shapes using the material, on another thread; shown small; out of date after the shape or texture size changes; not saved.
- **Texture size without blur**: sets follow UV → Texture size in the same undo step; paint and erase strokes and fills painted again at the new size when they explain the layer (one object per set), else resized (averaged smaller, smooth bigger); textures over the GPU's largest image shown from the largest copy that fits (views and UV Editor).
- **Re-pack and re-unwrap keep the paint**: polygons packed smaller average the old texels (up to 4 × 4 samples); status bar note when paint moves.
- **Paint follows the model**: after modelling or UV work, paint on triangles whose UVs moved is moved with them (same polygon point for point, else nearest point), in the same undo step.
- **Big textures**: 4K sets of 20 layers stay smooth (the heavy painting check in `tools/heavy/heavy-check.sh`); undo keeps only the tiles a stroke changed; mixing and dabs use every core.
- **Mirror painting** (L; Attributes → Mirror, X, Y or Z): the other side across the object's axis plane through its pivot, painted through the reflected camera (hidden sides too), shown as a dashed circle; the polygon fill too; flipped across U in the UV Editor; not smudge or clone.
- **Effect brushes** (brush bar, Effects): Smudge (5; carries a picked-up colour, picking up 30% a dab), Blend (9-texel average), Blur (4-texel average a pass), Hue shift (−180° to 180°); flow is their strength. **Projection** (3): Picture… in Attributes, laid over the views (Scale, Move, Centre, Shown), painted where it lies on screen; flat in the UV Editor. **Clone** (6): V + click sets the source; the offset is kept for later strokes.
- **Pen and smoothing** (Attributes, Paint mode): pressure → size (on, least 10%) and → flow (off), each with a least share and Soft / Even / Firm curves; smoothing (D, a lazy mouse up to 60 points); the pen's pressure shown. Pens through Wintab (Windows) or as touch with force; a mouse paints at full size and flow; the eraser end erases. Shift + click draws a line from the last stroke.
- **Colour** (Attributes, Paint mode): wheel (hue ring, saturation and value square) or sliders, New over Before (click to go back), lightness, Pick (P) (P over the model or UV Editor picks at once; the button waits for a click), hex and RGB, Copy; palettes kept with the project (New, Import / Export as GIMP `.gpl`, Delete; + adds the colour, click uses, right click removes), one undo step each.
- **Paint layout** (menu bar tab): the viewport and the UV Editor side by side, Coordinates under them, the Paint tab (in front) and Attributes over Paint layers (Objects beside); Paint mode switches to it and back; kept between sessions.
- **Texture sets:** each material (by name) can hold painted textures in layers, saved in the project ([painting textures](manual/painting-textures.md)).
- **Image…** (Attributes → Materials): a PNG, JPEG, TGA or BMP picture as a paint layer, stretched to the texture size.
- **Display → Surface → Painted:** the painted textures on the model over the materials' colours, and behind the UVs in the UV Editor.

## Saving, backups and the emergency copy

- **Projects** (`.avtl`): File → New (Ctrl + N), Open… (Ctrl + O), Open recent, Save (Ctrl + S), Save As… (Ctrl + Shift + S), Quit (no key); when the system's file window can't open (nothing comes back within half a second), the status bar says so and a small window asks for the path ([saving and opening](manual/saving-and-opening.md)).
- **Safe saving:** written beside the old file, checked to open again, then swapped in; the previous save kept as `.avtl.bak`.
- **Saving and exporting in the background:** Save, Export, Export again, Export to game project and Export kit run on another thread, the status bar saying *Saving …* or *Exporting …* with a turning wheel; one at a time (another waits, with a note); changes made meanwhile stay unsaved; closing waits ([decision 0163](decisions/0163-saving-and-exporting-in-the-background.md)).
- **"Save changes?"** before work is lost: Enter saves, Alt + N doesn't save, Esc cancels.
- **File format 63**, the development format with booleans, curves (and each curve point's handle type), improved bevels, modifiers, Subdivide with creases, lattice cages, Decimate, Scatter, Bend along curve, Shrinkwrap, Weighted normals, Live bevel with bevel weights, Quadify, collision in parts, Weld by distance, Array, Shell, levels of detail, Live bevel's Soft bevels, Displace, Bend, twist or taper, Struts, the kit grid, Triangulate and the Levels panel's levels (modifier ids, each level's changes, budgets and hand-made shapes), until the alpha; older projects still open ([decisions 0126](decisions/0126-rework-and-first-use.md), [0135](decisions/0135-live-booleans.md), [0138](decisions/0138-curves-sweep-lathe.md), [0139](decisions/0139-spline-editing.md), [0141](decisions/0141-improved-bevel.md), [0142](decisions/0142-modifier-stack-and-remesh.md), [0143](decisions/0143-subdivide-and-creases.md), [0144](decisions/0144-lattice-cage.md), [0145](decisions/0145-decimate.md), [0146](decisions/0146-scatter.md), [0147](decisions/0147-curve-deform-and-shrinkwrap.md), [0148](decisions/0148-weighted-normals.md), [0149](decisions/0149-live-bevel.md), [0150](decisions/0150-quadify.md), [0151](decisions/0151-collision-hulls.md), [0152](decisions/0152-weld-by-distance.md), [0153](decisions/0153-array.md), [0154](decisions/0154-shell.md), [0155](decisions/0155-levels-of-detail.md), [0156](decisions/0156-game-bevel.md), [0157](decisions/0157-displace.md) and [0158](decisions/0158-bend-twist-taper.md), [0159](decisions/0159-struts.md), [0165](decisions/0165-levels-panel.md)).
- **Damaged or hostile files** are refused with a message, never a crash.
- **Backup copies:** every 5 minutes when something changed, newest 5 kept, offered back after a crash (Settings → Autosave) ([backup copies](manual/autosave.md)).
- **Emergency copy:** if the app itself crashes, it saves the work as it was a moment before, offered first at the next start.

## Export and import

- **Export…** (File): glTF (`.glb`) or OBJ (`.obj` with `.mtl`); Everything shown or Selected only; what goes out shown first ([export and import](manual/export-and-import.md)).

Levels are always the exported shape. Exported only with the Export window's **Levels of detail** chip (under Includes; on when anything going out has levels, until set by hand, then remembered): each level's real shape beside its object as `name_LOD1` and on, with its distances in glTF extras (`lod_begin`, `lod_end`), hidden in Godot exports; a hand-made level's shape never goes out on its own. Godot makes its own levels on import; the manual gives a script that turns the app's into visibility ranges ([levels of detail](manual/levels-of-detail.md)).
- **What goes out:** shapes as they're shown, cut by their booleans, with cutters and lattice cages left out, with every modifier that is on; curves as their sweep or lathe (a bare line not at all); each object at its pivot (glTF), its own smoothing, the viewport's triangles, MikkTSpace tangents for shapes with UVs, materials by name.
- **Check for baking…** (UV menu, command search, or the Substance Painter preset's button): problems in the model and its UVs, with Select and one-click fixes ([check for baking](manual/bake-check.md)).
- **Import…** (File): OBJ files, as plain meshes.
